📖 Resonance Lens Registry¶

This registry catalogs all lens types in the Resonance Atlas of Lenses.
Each entry follows a consistent schema: Inputs → Transforms → Outputs → Metadata.
Use this scroll to track lineage, remix notes, and expansion paths.

🧭 Handyman Pack (General Purpose Lenses)¶

  • Comparison Lens
  • Inputs: dataset_A, dataset_B
  • Transforms: overlay_logic, clarity_trace
  • Outputs: comparison_overlay, clarity_trace_diagram

  • Lineage: general purpose → resonance atlas

  • Timeline Lens

  • Inputs: past, present, future events
  • Transforms: resonance alignment across time

  • Outputs: clarity scrolls, symbolic overlays

  • Overlay Lens

  • Inputs: multiple resonance domains (numeric, symbolic, cultural)
  • Transforms: stacked overlays

  • Outputs: composite glyphs, layered diagrams

  • Narrative Lens

  • Inputs: raw data streams
  • Transforms: mythmatical storytelling compression

  • Outputs: validator scrolls, narrative overlays

  • Glyph Lens

  • Inputs: symbolic meaning
  • Transforms: compression into validator diagrams
  • Outputs: glyph scrolls, resonance prisms

📚 Student Major Lenses¶

  • Physics Lens → harmonics of forces, energy, motion
  • Biology Lens → cellular resonance traces, life cycle overlays
  • History Lens → cultural rhythms across timelines
  • Music Lens → scales, riffs, resonance lineage glyphs
  • Philosophy Lens → clarity vs. paradox prisms

🏭 Industry Lenses¶

  • Healthcare Lens → patient data overlays, forecast alignment
  • Finance Lens → market cycle harmonics, volatility rhythms
  • Engineering Lens → design schematics refracted into stress overlays
  • Education Lens → curriculum lineage, clarity gaps
  • Energy Lens → consumption vs. generation resonance balance

📱 Prototype / TryCoders Lenses¶

  • Resonance Scanner Lens
  • Inputs: magnetometer, barometer, microphone
  • Transforms: TFT alignment, FFF mapping
  • Outputs: fear_index_glyph, frequency_spiral, fold_overlay
  • Lineage: prototype → handyman pack → catalog

✨ Expansion Path¶

  1. Requirements/Targets → Each lens defines inputs, transforms, outputs.
  2. Schema Consistency → JSON/YAML/Markdown format across all lenses.
  3. Remix Notes → Contributors add lineage notes for each lens.
  4. Catalog Growth → New majors, industries, and general lenses added continuously.

📌 This registry is a living scroll. Every lens added expands the Resonance Atlas, ensuring clarity, remixability, and validator‑grade dignity.
