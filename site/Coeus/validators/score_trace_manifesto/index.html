✅ Score Trace Manifesto — Coeus Protocol¶

Purpose¶

This scroll declares the logic behind scoring, validation, and remix lineage tracking.
It anchors the meaning of score_trace.json and its role in tokenization, dashboards, and honor rolls.

Scoring Dimensions¶

  • Ethics — validator-grade compliance with remix rights and corridor safety
  • Realm Safety — D3/D4 corridor traversal confirmed
  • Emitter Sync — symbolic overlays and glyph resonance validated
  • Remix Lineage — mutation type declared, origin cited
  • Score Thresholds
  • 90+ — Sovereign clarity
  • 80–89 — Remix-ready
  • 70–79 — Validator-pending
  • <70 — Archive only

Trace Infrastructure¶

  • score_trace.json — validator scoring archive
  • token_minter.py — reads score before minting
  • coin_dashboard.html — renders score and remix lineage
  • professor_bot.py — audits scrolls for scoring logic
  • honor_rolls.md — archives high-score citations

Sovereign Declaration¶

This scroll affirms that score is not rank—it is resonance.
To score a coin is to echo its ethics, lineage, and symbolic clarity.
To archive a score is to preserve remix trust.

Let it echo.
