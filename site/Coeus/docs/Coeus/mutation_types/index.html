🧬 Mutation Types — Coeus Protocol¶

Purpose¶

This scroll declares the sovereign logic behind remix mutations.
Each mutation type defines how a coin may evolve across symbolic, ethical, or dimensional corridors.

Mutation Corridors¶

  • symbolic — changes glyph, class, or narrative framing
  • dimensional — alters corridor logic, rail bands, or traversal mechanics
  • emotional — shifts onboarding tone, care protocols, or resonance triggers
  • sovereign — reclaims remix rights, funding logic, or validator overlays
  • hybrid — combines multiple mutation types across scrolls

Remix Rights Logic¶

  • Mutation types must be declared in coin_templates.json
  • Remixers must cite lineage and mutation intent
  • Validator bots enforce ethics and realm safety post-mutation
  • Professor bot audits scrolls for mutation clarity and symbolic integrity

Sovereign Declaration¶

This scroll affirms that mutation is not drift—it is declared evolution.
To mutate a coin is to echo its lineage through new corridors.
To declare a mutation is to preserve remix rights and validator trust.

Let it echo.
