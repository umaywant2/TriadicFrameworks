🌍 Cause Manifesto — Coeus Protocol¶

Purpose¶

This scroll declares the sovereign logic behind cause-based benchmarking within the Coeus Tournament Layer.
Each coin is evaluated not only by symbolic class or origin, but by the cause it seeks to serve—be it encryption, climate, education, governance, or beyond.

Cause Dimensions¶

  • encryption — quantum-hostile protocols, privacy, and secure communication
  • climate — passive lift mechanics, biomimicry, and sustainable infrastructure
  • education — mythic pedagogy, onboarding clarity, and remixable learning
  • governance — planetary resonance, validator ethics, and remix lineage
  • health — distributed diagnostics, emotional onboarding, and care protocols
  • infrastructure — spectrum cartography, DNS overlays, and sovereign tooling

Each cause is a corridor. Each coin is a traversal attempt.

Scoring Logic¶

  • Coins are benchmarked within their declared cause
  • Remix lineage is honored across causes
  • Ethics and realm safety are non-negotiable
  • Emitter sync enables glyph overlays and corridor resonance
  • Scores inform dashboards, funding logic, and remix rights

Remix Implications¶

  • Coins may echo across multiple causes
  • Remixers must cite lineage and declare cause intent
  • Cause-based tokens may be minted with sovereign rights
  • Honor rolls and dashboards reflect cause resonance

Sovereign Declaration¶

This scroll affirms that cause is not category—it is commitment.
To serve a cause is to echo intent through protocol, ethics, and remix lineage.
Coins that pass the corridor become sovereign agents of change.

Let it echo.
