Cross‑Module Propagation Animation Script¶

Title: “From Fields to Lattice”

Scene 1 — UE6: Fields in Motion¶

  • Visual:
  • Camera flies over the RTT_Showcase map.
  • Highlight: BP_RTT_Primitives, BP_RTT_ResonanceDemo, BP_RTT_EntropyDemo, BP_RTT_HybridDemo.
  • Narration:
    “Here, operators live as fields: φ, variance, resonance, entropy, and their hybrid.
    You see them as colors, shapes, pulses, and collapses.”

Scene 2 — Benchmarks: Behavior Emerges¶

  • Visual:
  • Freeze the UE6 view; overlay simple graphs and labels:
    • Phi Stability, Emergence Coherence, Resonance Envelope, Entropy Boundary Detection, Hybrid Coherence.
  • Narration:
    “Benchmarks watch these fields over time.
    They don’t care about every frame—only about how the operator behaves:
    how stable, how coherent, how close to collapse.”

Scene 3 — Transition: From Behavior to Structure¶

  • Visual:
  • Lines draw from each Benchmark label into a dark background.
  • The UE6 scene fades; only benchmark names remain, then shrink into nodes.
  • Narration:
    “Once behavior is measured and named, it can leave the scene.
    What travels forward is not the raw field, but the signature of how it behaves.”

Scene 4 — TEL: The Lattice Appears¶

  • Visual:
  • A lattice fades in: layers labeled Ladder, Cycle, Map, Atlas.
  • Nodes appear: TEL.Operator.Phi, TEL.Operator.Variance, TEL.Resonance.Core/Harmonics, TEL.Entropy.Field/Collapse, TEL.Hybrid.Operator.
  • Narration:
    “TEL is where these signatures become structure.
    Each node is a place in the lattice where an operator can live, connect, and propagate.”

Scene 5 — Wiring the Chain¶

  • Visual:
  • Draw arrows:
    • Phi Stability → TEL.Operator.Phi
    • Variance Smoothing → TEL.Operator.Variance
    • Resonance Envelope → TEL.Resonance.Core
    • Entropy Boundary Detection → TEL.Entropy.Field
    • Hybrid Coherence → TEL.Hybrid.Operator
  • Narration:
    “Every benchmark has a home in TEL.
    Once placed, it can connect to other operators, other modules, other regimes.”

Scene 6 — Cross‑Module Propagation¶

  • Visual:
  • From TEL nodes, lines extend outward to icons for other modules (SARG, Mode, Opacity, Integrations, TEL‑adjacent systems).
  • Narration:
    “From TEL, operators no longer belong to a single scene or engine.
    They become part of a shared substrate that other modules can query, reuse, and transform.”

Scene 7 — Closing Frame¶

  • Visual:
  • Overlay the full chain as a simple diagram:
    UE6 (fields) → Benchmarks (behavior) → TEL (structure) → Other Modules (propagation)
  • Narration:
    “You start by seeing fields.
    You learn to read behavior.
    You end by thinking in structure.
    That’s the path from UE6 to TEL.”
