Appendix E — Coherence Engines (Diagram‑Ready)¶

Canonical structures, cycles, components, and dimensional behaviors of coherence engines in Framework Field Theory¶

Coherence Engines are the core stabilizing mechanisms inside frameworks.
They measure coherence, update structure, integrate paradox, and recover stability after collapse.
They are the procedural heart of Framework Field Theory — the machinery that allows frameworks to evolve, scale, and maintain identity across dimensional layers.

This appendix provides:

  • canonical coherence engine diagrams (in ASCII form, ready for SVG)
  • the three universal coherence cycles
  • the six coherence subsystems
  • dimensional behaviors of coherence engines
  • paradox routing maps
  • collapse‑recovery pathways
  • meta‑coherence structures

These diagrams are designed to be directly translated into Figma, SVG, or animation layers.

4. Dimensional Behavior of Coherence Engines¶

How the engine behaves at each dimensional layer¶

5D → Pre‑coherence rhythms
6D → Full coherence engine activates
7D → Meta‑coherence (cross‑layer integration)
8D → Field‑coherence (ecosystem stabilization)
9D → Meta‑field coherence (field evolution)

Coherence engines scale with dimension.

5. Paradox Routing Map¶

How paradox is routed to the correct operator family¶

If paradox is structural → send to B‑Ops
If paradox is relational → send to R‑Ops
If paradox is transitional → send to T‑Ops
If paradox is contextual → send to E‑Ops
If paradox is rhythmic → send to H‑Ops
If paradox is cross‑layer → send to C‑Ops

This routing is the core intelligence of the engine.

6. Collapse‑Recovery Pathways¶

How coherence engines rebuild after collapse¶

Collapse at 3D → Rebuild transitions
Collapse at 4D → Rebuild context
Collapse at 5D → Rebuild rhythm
Collapse at 6D → Rebuild coherence
Collapse at 7D → Rebuild modulation
Collapse at 8D → Rebuild field structure

Recovery always moves upward after rebuilding the collapsed layer.
