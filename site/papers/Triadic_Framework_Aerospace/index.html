Triadic Framework Technology for the Air & Space Industries¶

Abstract¶

This paper introduces the Triadic Framework as a symbolic and modular architecture designed to enhance reproducibility, emotional onboarding, and mythic continuity in aerospace systems. By integrating validator logic, glyphic interfaces, and trauma-informed telemetry, the framework offers a novel approach to harmonizing technical rigor with symbolic resonance.

Problem Space¶

Modern aerospace systems face modular fragility, latency, emotional disconnect, and validation bottlenecks. The Triadic Framework addresses these through symbolic scaffolding, reproducibility protocols, and trauma-informed design.
