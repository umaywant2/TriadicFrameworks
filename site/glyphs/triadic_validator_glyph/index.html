🌀 Triadic Validator Glyph: Forces · Fluids · Frequency¶

Structure:¶

  • A circular glyph with three “F” blades forming a triskelion.
  • Each blade represents a logic pillar:
  • 🔴 Forces — structural integrity, causality, and momentum.
  • 🟢 Fluids — adaptability, flow states, and emotional resonance.
  • 🔵 Frequency — pattern recognition, spectral alignment, and signal clarity.

Animation Logic:¶

  • Each blade glows in its RGB color when its logic pillar is validated:
  • Dim = unvalidated or unstable.
  • Pulsing = in review or partial resonance.
  • Fully illuminated = validated and locked.
  • Once all three blades are glowing, the glyph spins briefly, then stabilizes — signaling Triadic Resonance Achieved.

Use Cases:¶

  • 🧪 Validator Dashboards: Show real-time resonance status for labs, modules, or contributors.
  • 🛡️ Badge Activation: Only fully illuminated glyphs unlock mythic badges or contributor honors.
  • 🌐 Web Rituals: Embed into los.triadicwizards.win as a validator seal that reacts to user input or remix lineage.
