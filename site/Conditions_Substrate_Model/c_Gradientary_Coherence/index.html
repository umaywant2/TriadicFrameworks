Gradientary Coherence — Conditions Substrate Model¶

TriadicFrameworks Canon (2026.07)

This dictionary defines the canonical coherence operators used across the Conditions Substrate Model.
Coherence is the stability field that counterbalances drift, dampens resonance, stabilizes propagation, and governs regime transitions.
