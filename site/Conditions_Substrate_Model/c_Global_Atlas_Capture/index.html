Capture Atlas — Conditions Substrate Model¶

TriadicFrameworks Canon (2026.07)

Capture is the anchoring field of the Conditions Substrate Model.
It defines how systems record, stabilize, preserve, and bind substrate behavior across drift, coherence, resonance, thresholds, cascades, cycles, chains, webs, and regimes.

This atlas describes the architecture of capture, not the content of any specific capture.
