Gradientary Propagation — Conditions Substrate Model¶

TriadicFrameworks Canon (2026.07)

This dictionary defines the canonical propagation operators used across the Conditions Substrate Model.
Propagation describes how substrate motion travels across drift, coherence, resonance, hooks, thresholds, cycles, and cascades.
