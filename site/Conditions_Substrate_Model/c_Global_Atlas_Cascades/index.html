Cascade Atlas — Conditions Substrate Model¶

TriadicFrameworks Canon (2026.07)

This atlas defines all cascade classes across the Conditions Substrate Model:
dimensional, composite, meta, substrate, hook, regime, propagation, cycle, chain, web, epoch, and deep‑time cascades.

Cascades represent transitions where drift overtakes coherence and substrates enter motion.

Module: Conditions Substrate Model

Purpose: Unified atlas of all cascade classes
