Gradientary Signatures — Conditions Substrate Model¶

TriadicFrameworks Canon (2026.07)

Signatures are the canonical markers of the Conditions Substrate Model.
They identify drift states, coherence states, resonance peaks, thresholds, cascades, cycles, chains, webs, and cross‑scale transitions.

This dictionary defines the canonical signature operators used across all substrates (D0–D7).

1. Drift Signatures¶

signature_drift_dominance¶

Definition: Drift(d_i) > Coh(d_i)
Operator Notes: instability marker
Pattern: drift ignition

signature_drift_sync¶

Definition: synchronized drift across dims
Operator Notes: cascade precursor
Pattern: drift synchronization

signature_drift_oscillation¶

Definition: Drift(D1,D7,D5) >> Coh
Operator Notes: volatility signature
Pattern: oscillation ignition

signature_drift_collapse¶

Definition: Drift across ≥4 dims >> Coh≈0
Operator Notes: terminal collapse
Pattern: collapse ignition
