Signature Atlas — Conditions Substrate Model¶

TriadicFrameworks Canon (2026.07)

Signatures are the canonical markers of the Conditions Substrate Model.
They identify drift states, coherence states, resonance peaks, thresholds, cascades, cycles, chains, webs, and cross‑scale transitions.

This atlas defines drift signatures, coherence signatures, resonance signatures, regime signatures, threshold signatures, propagation signatures, substrate signatures, cascade signatures, cycle signatures, chain signatures, web signatures, and cross‑scale signatures.
