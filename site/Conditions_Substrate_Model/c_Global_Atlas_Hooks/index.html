Hook Atlas — Conditions Substrate Model¶

TriadicFrameworks Canon (2026.07)

Hooks are the amplifiers, ignition points, and binding agents of the Conditions Substrate Model.
They intensify drift, synchronize cascades, trigger resonance, and shape coherence envelopes.

This atlas defines activation hooks, alignment hooks, resonance hooks, stress hooks, scarcity hooks, opacity hooks, composite hooks, hook chains, hook webs, hook envelopes, hook cascades, hook cycles, hook regimes, and cross‑scale hooks.
