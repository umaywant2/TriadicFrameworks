Regime Atlas — Conditions Substrate Model¶

TriadicFrameworks Canon (2026.07)

Regimes are the state‑machine of the Conditions Substrate Model.
They define how systems move through stability, expansion, coherence, oscillation, narrowing, destabilization, collapse, and regeneration.

This atlas unifies drift, coherence, resonance, thresholds, cascades, cycles, chains, webs, hooks, substrates, propagation, and signatures into a single regime architecture.

0. Identity¶

Module: Conditions Substrate Model
Role: Regime Cartography
Purpose: Unified atlas of all regime classes
Version: 2026.07

1. Regime Definitions (R0–R7)¶

R0 — Stability Regime¶

Coh↑, Drift↓
notes: stability ignition

R1 — Expansion Regime¶

Coh↑↑, Drift↑
notes: growth ignition

R2 — Coherence Regime¶

Coh↑↑↑, Drift↓
notes: peak stability

R3 — Oscillation Regime¶

Drift(D1,D7,D5) >> Coh
notes: volatility ignition

R4 — Narrowing Regime¶

Drift(D0,D5,D1) > Coh
notes: scarcity ignition

R5 — Destabilization Regime¶

Drift(D0,D7,D1) >> Coh
notes: collapse precursor

R6 — Collapse Regime¶

Drift across ≥4 dims >> Coh≈0
notes: terminal collapse

R7 — Regeneration Regime¶

Coh↑, Drift↓
notes: recovery ignition

2. Regime State‑Machine (Full Arc)¶

stability → expansion → coherence → oscillation → narrowing → destabilization → collapse → regeneration → stability

Notes:
- This is the canonical full‑cycle regime arc.
- Every substrate (D0–D7) passes through this arc at different velocities.
- Drift, coherence, and resonance determine transitions.
