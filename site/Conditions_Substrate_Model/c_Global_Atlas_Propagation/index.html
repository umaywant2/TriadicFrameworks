Propagation Atlas — Conditions Substrate Model¶

TriadicFrameworks Canon (2026.07)

This atlas defines all propagation classes across the Conditions Substrate Model:
vectors, webs, loops, synchronizations, envelopes, cascades, cycles, chains, and composite flows.

Propagation describes how substrate motion travels across drift, coherence, resonance, hooks, thresholds, cycles, and cascades.
