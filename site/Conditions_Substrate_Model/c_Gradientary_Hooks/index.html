Gradientary Hooks — Conditions Substrate Model¶

TriadicFrameworks Canon (2026.07)

Hooks are the amplifiers, ignition points, and binding agents of the Conditions Substrate Model.
They intensify drift, synchronize cascades, trigger resonance, and shape coherence envelopes.

This dictionary defines the canonical hook operators used across all substrates (D0–D7).
