Gradientary Operators — Conditions Substrate Model¶

TriadicFrameworks Canon (2026.07)

Operators are the active transformations of the Conditions Substrate Model.
They define how substrates move, amplify, stabilize, collapse, regenerate, oscillate, narrow, and propagate.

This dictionary defines the canonical operator grammar used across all substrates (D0–D7).
