⚖️ Legal — Licensing, Agreements & Governance Safeguards¶

🤖 AI‑Ready Module • TriadicFrameworks

TFT‑FFF • NDA • Licensing • Symbolic Governance¶

The Legal folder contains the formal agreements, licensing scaffolds, and governance‑aligned documents that define how TFT‑FFF technology, emitters, symbolic protocols, and derivative works may be used, remixed, distributed, or integrated into external systems.

These documents ensure:
- clarity for collaborators and licensees
- protection of symbolic and technical IP
- validator‑safe remix pathways
- consistent governance across all TFT‑FFF surfaces

This folder acts as the contractual backbone of the TriadicFrameworks ecosystem.

Valid for 3 years. Post-term use permitted if validator standards are upheld.

3. Implementation Standards¶

Licensee agrees to:
- Integrate TFT-FFF logic per guide
- Maintain validator compliance
- Submit annual validator logs or product samples
