1. Echo Family Definition¶

The Forecast Echo Family is created when:

  • temporal forecasts diverge from actuals (RTT/1)
  • regime is transitional, not collapsing (RTT/2)
  • coherence resets across layers (RTT/3)

TEL receives these signals and groups them into echo families.

2. Echo Structure¶

Echo components:

  • Temporal Echo: deltas between forecasts and actuals
  • Regime Echo: stable / transitional / divergent
  • Coherence Echo: surface → structural → resonance alignment
  • Drift Paths: how narratives drift from structural behavior

3. Downstream Effects¶

  • MSM: instability‑signal amplification
  • IE: transfer‑system reinterpretation
  • GSM: benefit‑structure divergence
  • Proto‑Fund: fund‑language vs transfer‑mechanics mismatch

4. One‑Sentence Summary¶

The Forecast Echo Family is the TEL grouping created when forecasts diverge from actuals, producing temporal, regime, and coherence echoes that propagate into MSM, IE, GSM, and proto‑fund analysis.
