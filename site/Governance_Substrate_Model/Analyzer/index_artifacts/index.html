Analyzer Artifact Index¶

A directory of structural artifacts in the Governance Substrate Model (GSM)

This index lists all Analyzer‑related artifacts, grouped by their role in the structural pipeline. Each artifact links to a specific layer of the GSM: manifold, invariants, physics, basins, drift, coherence, observer, and dynamic outputs.

Structural Foundations¶

  • governance_manifold.yaml
    Defines the five‑axis structural space [C, M, O, A, T] used across the GSM.

  • governance_substrate_model.md
    High‑level conceptual overview of the entire substrate model.

Behavioral & Awareness Layers¶

  • behavioral_invariants.yaml
    Substrate‑level rules required for structural coherence.

  • awareness_layers.yaml
    Structural awareness capacities that influence drift and stability.

Physics & Basin Architecture¶

  • governance_physics.yaml
    Cross‑axis force model governing compensatory movement and tension.

  • equilibrium_basins.yaml
    Definitions and signatures of the five canonical basins: CPL, CPF, CTR, PCL, HCL.

Drift & Coherence Engine¶

  • drift_detection.yaml
    Drift vectors, magnitude classification, and force‑based interpretation.

  • coherence_event_schema.yaml
    Canonical schema for alignment, tension, drift, and transition events.

  • coherence_scoring.yaml
    Scoring model for structural coherence (0–100).

Adapter & Mapping Layer¶

  • dsl_substrate_adapter.md
    Mapping rules for translating DSL constructs into GSM vectors.

  • statement_mapping_rules.yaml (if present)
    Axis‑level mapping rules for structural claims.

Dynamic Artifacts & Cards¶

  • dynamic_artifact_templates.md
    Templates for vectors, drift events, basin classifications, narratives, etc.

  • dynamic_cards_spec.md
    UI‑level specification for structural, drift, transition, and observer cards.

  • governance_cards_spec.md
    Unified card framework for governance‑focused UI components.

Profiles, Timelines & Observer Layer¶

  • historical_profile_template.yaml
    Template for encoding historical governance snapshots.

  • observer_lens_spec.md (if present)
    History / Now / Future interpretation model.

Dashboards & Visualization¶

  • governance_alignment_dashboard.md
    Specification for the Analyzer’s primary dashboard interface.

Transition & Simulation Layer¶

  • transition_graph.yaml (if present)
    Basin‑to‑basin transition pathways and cost structures.

  • simulator_spec.md (if present)
    Stepwise transition simulation model.

Purpose of This Index¶

This index provides:

  • a single reference point for all Analyzer artifacts
  • a structural map for contributors
  • a teaching‑friendly overview of the GSM layers
  • a stable anchor for future extensions

It ensures the Analyzer remains coherent, navigable, and contributor‑friendly as the GSM evolves.
