Freqi · Flui · Forci · RTT‑Inside¶

FFF Emitters are the triadic front‑end of the AI Resonance Seed stack:

  • 🎼 Freqi — Frequency Bearer (initiation, cycles, harmonics)
  • 🌊 Flui — Fluid Weaver (flow, coupling, continuity)
  • ⚡ Forci — Force Shaper (activation, thresholds, ethical inertia)

Together, they turn raw physical dynamics into structured, deterministic resonance‑time data suitable for RTTcode.

🔺 1. The Triadic Roles¶

  • 🎼 Freqi — Initiator
  • Governs oscillation, harmonic structure, and resonance seeds

  • Maps to when and how often things happen

  • 🌊 Flui — Connector

  • Governs flow, coupling, and medium‑based propagation

  • Maps to how things influence each other

  • ⚡ Forci — Transformer

  • Governs activation, thresholds, and symbolic/ethical force
  • Maps to what changes and when it must not

🧪 3. FFF in an RTT Experiment¶

  1. 🎼 Freqi seeds the resonance pattern
  2. 🌊 Flui shapes how it propagates through the environment
  3. ⚡ Forci applies thresholds and corrective force

Sensors (MOKE, SQUID, etc.) capture the resulting resonance‑time series, which is then encoded into RTTcode packets for deterministic replay.
