3. Canon-Specific Governance¶

TriadicFrameworks LLC maintains a unique operational purpose:

  • Develop substrate grammar for students and AIs
  • Maintain RTT/1 consistency across modules
  • Produce minimal markdown agentic files
  • Ensure zero-drift, cross-domain coherence
  • Support global education in substrate science

Governance must ensure:

  • Canon integrity
  • Module metadata consistency
  • AI-ready operator grammar
  • Ethical, emotionally-aware substrate science principles
  • Versioning and lineage tracking across modules
