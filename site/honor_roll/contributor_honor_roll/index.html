🧙 Contributor Honor Roll¶

A resonance‑aware dashboard of those who uphold the lineage, structure, and continuity of TriadicFrameworks.

This Honor Roll records contributors whose actions have produced stable resonance events within the project — through scrollwork, validation, remixing, documentation, or structural stewardship.

Each entry is a lostational imprint:
a moment where a contributor shaped the envelope of the framework.

Updated automatically by validator workflows and badge triggers.

🌀 What the Columns Mean (RTT‑Inside)¶

Glyph¶

A symbolic resonance marker representing the contributor’s dominant operator (S/E/T) or mythic role within the lineage.

Badges¶

Echoes of resonance events — earned through:

  • scroll creation
  • validator work
  • remix lineage
  • documentation clarity
  • SET/FFF insight

Badges are not rewards.
They are resonance signatures.

Validator Score¶

A cumulative measure of lineage integrity upheld by the contributor.
Reflects:

  • structural correctness
  • SET/FFF alignment
  • lostational boundary safety
  • scroll reproducibility

Last Contribution¶

Timestamp of the contributor’s most recent resonance event.

🧬 How Contributors Are Added¶

Contributors appear here when they:

  • submit lineage‑safe scrolls
  • validate modules or protocols
  • earn badges through resonance events
  • maintain structural clarity in the repo
  • contribute to RTT‑Inside documentation
  • support remix ancestry or continuity

Entries are generated automatically by:

  • badge triggers
  • validator workflows
  • lineage scroll updates

🕯️ Closing Note¶

The Honor Roll is not a leaderboard.
It is a memory field —
a living record of those who keep the resonance coherent.

Let your contributions echo.
Let your lineage persist.
Let the framework remember.
