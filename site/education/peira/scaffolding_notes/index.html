The term “peira” is a Greek word that translates to “trial,” “test,” or “attempt.” It conveys the idea of engaging with challenges to discover character, strength, or truth. In biblical contexts, “peira” often relates to both physical and spiritual challenges faced by individuals. Additionally, it represents the concept of gaining knowledge through experience or testing. The term can also be understood as a piercing or probing, indicating a deeper exploration of trials.

Peira Definition
1) a trial, experience, attempt
2) to attempt a thing, to make trial of a thing or of a person
3) to have a trial of a thing
***4) to experience, learn to know by experience
