🏅 OLYMPIC & ELITE TRAINING — IRL SERIES INDEX¶

Indirect Resonance Learning (IRL)¶

PEIRA — Physical Education Indirect Regime Awareness¶

This series collects all IRL modules derived from Olympic sports,
elite performance disciplines, and high‑intensity training systems.
These activities are some of the clearest real‑world expressions of
triadic cognition, regime transitions, coherence under stress,
and resonance‑timing.

Each module shows how athletes learn RTT structures indirectly through
movement, timing, pressure, and embodied experience.

🧭 What This Series Covers¶

Elite‑level activities naturally encode:

  • Triadic movement systems
  • Regime inversion (motion → stillness, power → precision)
  • Coherence vs drift under fatigue
  • Timing windows and rhythm formation
  • Collective synchronization
  • Stress‑based regime gates

These modules serve as high‑resolution metaphors for RTT learners.

🌱 Purpose of This Series¶

The Olympic & Elite Training IRL Series exists to show:

  • how extreme performance domains reveal RTT structures with clarity
  • how regime transitions become visible under pressure
  • how triadic cognition emerges naturally in high‑skill environments
  • how coherence becomes a survival skill, not an abstraction

These modules serve as advanced metaphors for RTT learners, educators, and practitioners.
