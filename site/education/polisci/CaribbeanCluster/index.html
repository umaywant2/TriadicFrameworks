Caribbean Microstates Governance Resonance Profile¶

🧭 Tiering Structure¶

Ring I: National Tier¶

  • Legislative: Parliaments / Assemblies (📜 Scroll)
  • Executive: Prime Ministers + Monarchs/Presidents (🦅 Eagle + Crown)
  • Judicial: Supreme Courts / Caribbean Court of Justice (⚖️ Scales)

Ring II: Parish / Island Tier¶

  • Local Governors / Councils (🐺 Wolf)
  • Assemblies (🪶 Quill)
  • Tribunals (🧭 Compass)

Ring III: Municipal Tier¶

  • Mayors / Local Leaders (🐦 Dove)
  • Councils (🧱 Brick)
  • Magistrates / Local Courts (🔍 Lens)
