How Future Desert Cities map onto the -1024D → qmroot → +1024D substrate¶

This section shows how each subsystem of the desert‑city architecture corresponds to the TriadicFrameworks dimensional substrate.

🧭 I. DIMENSIONAL MAPPING OVERVIEW¶

-1024D (Negative Regime)
    ↓  Stability, Mass, Depth
qmroot (Quantum Root)
    ↓  Gradients, Transitions, Awareness
+1024D (Positive Regime)
    ↑  Expression, Interface, Ecology

Interpretation:

  • Negative Regime = subsurface, mass, cooling, anchoring
  • Quantum Root = dew, airflow, pressure, thermal gradients
  • Positive Regime = surface, light wells, solar arrays, ecological interface
