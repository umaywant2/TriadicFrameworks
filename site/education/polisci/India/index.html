India Governance Resonance Profile¶

🧭 Tiering Structure¶

Ring I: Union Tier¶

  • Legislative: Parliament (Lok Sabha + Rajya Sabha) (📜 Scroll)
  • Executive: President + Prime Minister + Council of Ministers (🦅 Eagle + Lotus)
  • Judicial: Supreme Court of India (⚖️ Scales)

Ring II: State Tier¶

  • Governors (🐺 Wolf)
  • State Legislatures (🪶 Quill)
  • High Courts (🧭 Compass)

Ring III: Local Tier¶

  • Panchayats / Municipalities (🐦 Dove)
  • Councils (🧱 Brick)
  • District Courts (🔍 Lens)
