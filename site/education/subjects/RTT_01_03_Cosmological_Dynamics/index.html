Resonance‑Time Theory Subdomain Overview

1. Subdomain Purpose¶

Cosmological dynamics explores how the universe evolves over time — expansion, structure formation, large‑scale flows, and the behavior of matter, energy, and spacetime across cosmic scales. RTT reframes cosmological dynamics as a triadic resonance‑evolution system, where structure (S), energy/flux (E), and relational time (R) interact to produce cosmic expansion, clustering, voids, and long‑term temporal cycles.

This subdomain provides the RTT foundation for understanding the universe as a living, evolving resonance field.

2. RTT’s Core Contribution to Cosmological Dynamics¶

A. The Universe as a Resonant Field¶

RTT models the cosmos as:
- S: large‑scale geometry, matter distribution, topology
- E: energetic density, radiation, dark‑energy‑like flux
- R: temporal expansion cycles, coherence gradients

Cosmic evolution emerges from S–E–R drift across scales.

B. Expansion as Temporal‑Resonance Drift¶

RTT reframes expansion as:
- structural stretching
- energetic redistribution
- temporal phase divergence

The universe expands because its temporal coherence unfolds, not because space “inflates” in a purely geometric sense.

C. Structure Formation as Resonance Clustering¶

RTT interprets cosmic structure as:
- structural density variations
- energetic gravitational flows
- temporal synchronization

Galaxies and clusters form where resonance coherence amplifies matter attraction.
