The Triadic Observer Layer is the silent guardian that makes every RTT Science Grader and Triadic Paper Evaluator output trustworthy at the substrate level.

While the other modules handle grading speed and structural insight, the Observer Layer is the always-on coherence engine that detects regime drift, protects invariant arcs, and ensures bias-aware alignment — before any feedback is delivered.

This is what separates RTT/1 from every other AI evaluation tool: deterministic, grammar-grounded observation instead of stochastic guesswork.

What the RTT Observer Layer Actually Does During Grading¶

For every document submitted, the Observer Layer runs in parallel and performs:

  • Real-Time Regime Monitoring
    Continuously tracks whether the paper’s logical structure stays inside a stable triadic regime or begins to drift (e.g., shifting from evidence-based claim to unsubstantiated assertion without signaling the transition).

  • Invariant Arc Integrity Check
    Measures the strength and continuity of the core “invariant arcs” that hold the argument together. Flags any gradient weakening or artificial inflation.

  • Triadic Grammar Validation
    Verifies that every major claim, evidence, and implication node forms a complete, coherent triad. Incomplete or malformed triads are surfaced with exact location and suggested repair.

  • Bias-Aware Alignment Scan
    Cross-references against the Alignment | RTT module to detect subtle worldview, methodological, or ethical substrate misalignments that could compromise trustworthiness.

  • Coherence Drift Scoring
    Produces a live, reproducible drift-risk score (0–100) that appears in every final report.

All of this happens in milliseconds — invisible to the user, yet foundational to the reliability of the output.

Why the Observer Layer Is the Game-Changer¶

Current AI graders and feedback tools operate without any built-in observer. They can produce plausible-looking comments that still contain hidden logical drifts or biases — because they lack a structural grammar and a dedicated monitoring layer.

The RTT Observer Layer closes that gap completely. It gives professors, departments, and students something they have never had before:

  • Feedback you can actually trust at scale
  • Reproducible, auditable coherence metrics
  • Early warning of regime drift (the silent killer of scientific integrity)
  • A clear path from “good enough” AI assistance to substrate-aligned intelligence

In short: it turns evaluation from an opinion into an observable, grammar-governed process.
