Science Grader - RTT/1¶

The Flagship “Guide for Science” Module
Resonance-Time Tech (RTT/1) • TriadicFrameworks Education Toolbox

RTT Science Grader is the #1 daily tool for researchers, professors, and students who want grading, paper evaluation, and insight generation that is fast, trustworthy, and substrate-deep — instead of surface-level and stochastic.

It turns the traditional grading backlog into an instant, coherent response service while surfacing new insights that current AI tools simply cannot see.

What RTT Science Grader Actually Does¶

Upload any paper, thesis, article, grant proposal, or student submission and receive:

  • Triadic Structural Grammar Breakdown
    Instant mapping of the document’s invariant arcs, resonance gradients, and coherence nodes.

  • Regime-Drift Detection
    The Triadic Observer Layer flags any logical drift, hidden bias, or misalignment before it becomes a problem.

  • Bias-Aware + Alignment Scoring
    Ethical-alignment-at-the-substrate checks drawn from the Alignment | RTT module.

  • Coherence Score (0–100)
    A single, reproducible number grounded in triadic grammar — not a black-box LLM guess.

  • Actionable Revision Insights
    Specific, citable suggestions that strengthen the work rather than generic “improve clarity” comments.

  • New Insight Generation
    Cross-references to related literature and surfaces hidden connections that only become visible through triadic observation.

This is not another rubric-based auto-grader.
It is a resonance-time co-evaluator that works with the same workflows educators already use — just with the missing grammar and regime awareness baked in at the foundation.

Why This Module First?¶

Higher-ed grading backlogs are real. Faculty routinely spend 10–15+ hours per week on evaluation, leading to burnout, delayed feedback, and inconsistent outcomes. Existing tools (Gradescope, SciSpace, etc.) help with logistics but still operate without triadic structural grammar or Observer Layer protection.

RTT Science Grader clears the backlog today while proving the full power of TriadicFrameworks. Once adopted as the daily driver for science grading and analysis, the path to multisensory extensions (including full animal olfactory resonance profiles) becomes natural and trusted.

Example Output (Visual + Text)¶

When you upload a paper, you receive:

  1. Triadic Map (visual diagram of the document’s resonance structure)
  2. Coherence Report
    - Overall score: 87/100
    - Strong arcs: [list]
    - Regime-drift flags: 2 minor (explained with exact quotes + suggested fixes)
    - Alignment strength: High (no substrate-level ethical drift detected)
  3. Revision Recommendations (numbered, citable)
  4. New Insight Seeds (potential extensions or cross-domain connections)

Quickstart for Educators & Researchers¶

  1. Drop your document (PDF, Word, LaTeX, or plain text) into any RTT-enabled interface.
  2. Choose evaluation mode:
    - Grading (with rubric alignment)
    - Research Analysis (deep insight mode)
    - Peer-Review Assist
  3. Receive results in seconds.
  4. Export as Markdown, PDF, or LMS-ready comment.

Integration ready for:
- Learning Management Systems (Canvas, Blackboard, Moodle)
- GitHub / Overleaf workflows
- Institutional RTT Response Service (see rtt-higher-ed-response-service.md)
