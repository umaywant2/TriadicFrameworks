Drift Template — TriadicFrameworks Prompts¶

The Drift Template provides the canonical structure for surfacing drift across the five
RTT drift‑tensor layers: geometric, operational, temporal, conceptual, and domain.
Drift identifies differences in form, behavior, interpretation, or alignment.

This template defines the standard drift‑analysis grammar for TriadicFrameworks.

Purpose¶

Use this template when you need to:

  • surface drift across RTT drift‑tensor layers
  • identify structural differences
  • detect misalignment across processes or interpretations
  • evaluate domain drift
  • analyze substrate and dimensional drift
  • prepare material for coherence declaration
  • maintain structural neutrality during drift analysis

Drift Template¶

1. Geometric Drift¶

Identify differences in form, shape, structure, or spatial configuration.

Prompt:
- Identify geometric drift. What structural differences appear across these forms?

2. Operational Drift¶

Identify differences in steps, processes, workflows, or operations.

Prompt:
- Identify operational drift. What differences appear across these processes or workflows?

3. Temporal Drift¶

Identify differences across time, sequence, pacing, or temporal interpretation.

Prompt:
- Identify temporal drift. What differences appear across these timelines or sequences?

4. Conceptual Drift¶

Identify differences in meaning, interpretation, framing, or conceptual structure.

Prompt:
- Identify conceptual drift. What differences appear across these interpretations or explanations?

5. Domain Drift¶

Identify differences across the six canonical domains.

Prompt:
- Identify domain drift. What differences appear across psychology, physics, economics, governance, AI/agents, and biology?

6. Substrate Drift¶

Identify differences in substrate behavior or substrate‑tensor transitions.

Prompt:
- Identify substrate drift. What differences appear across these substrate conditions or transitions?

7. Dimensional Drift¶

Identify differences across dimensional rails or transitions.

Prompt:
- Identify dimensional drift. What differences appear across these dimensional layers or rails?

8. Drift Summary¶

Produce a drift‑aligned summary across all layers.

Prompt:
- Produce a drift summary across geometric, operational, temporal, conceptual, domain, substrate, and dimensional layers.
