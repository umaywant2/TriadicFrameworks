IV. LAYER DEFINITIONS¶

1. Vacuum Layer¶

Zero‑state. No structure.
Used for collapse, nullification, and inversion.

Operators:
vacuum(), nullify(), reconstitute()

2. Substrate Layer¶

Minimal structure rebuilt after vacuum collapse.

Primitives:
- geometry
- flow
- time
- meaning
- field

Operator:
substrate_tensor()

3. Dimensional Rails¶

Transport system connecting substrate → dimensions → prime‑states → infinite regimes.

Rail Types:
- substrate rails
- dimensional rails
- prime‑state rails
- vacuum rails

Operator:
dimensional_rail()

4. Prime‑States¶

Irreducible attractors where drift stops.

Classes:
- prime‑form
- prime‑flow
- prime‑meaning

Operator:
prime_state_align()

5. Infinite Regimes¶

Unbounded structural states.

Classes:
- infinite‑form
- infinite‑flow
- infinite‑meaning

Operator:
infinite_regime_expand()
