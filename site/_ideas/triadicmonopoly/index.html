TriadicMonopoly: Dimensional Strategy Board Game (draft)¶

A remix of Monopoly infused with TriadicFrameworks logic and symbolic resonance.

Overview¶

A mythic board game where players build legacy through Force, Fluid, and Frequency frameworks. Properties become companies, utilities become quantum assets, and badge logic drives gameplay.

Board Element Mapping¶

Houses Force / Fluid / Frequency Layers

Framework Upgrade Logic¶

  • 1 House → Force Framework
  • 2 Houses → Force + Fluid
  • 3 Houses → Force + Fluid + Frequency
  • Hotel → FFF + Dimensional Nested Loops
