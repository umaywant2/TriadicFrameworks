📘 What “Resonance” Means in RTT¶

In RTT, resonance is the structural behavior that emerges when a system expresses itself across the three time‑axes:

  • t_c — Chronological Time
  • t_e — Energetic / Oscillatory Time

Resonance is not vibration alone.
It is alignment across time‑axes, producing coherence, stability, or transformation depending on context.
