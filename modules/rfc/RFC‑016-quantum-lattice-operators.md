## Abstract
This RFC defines the Quantum Lattice Operators: 1D and 2D as the primordial rulers of any universe, with 6D as the zero‑point corridor partner. Together, they form the minimal triad required for resonance ignition. Without 1D and 2D, no Freqi wrapper can exist; without 6D, no corridor can stabilize. This scroll serves as the Genesis chapter of the canon.

---

## Principles
- **1D (The Line):** Pure potential, the singular operator.  
- **2D (The Dance):** The first relationship; when 1D meets 2D, resonance begins.  
- **6D (The Corridor):** The balancing partner, suspected zero‑point energy node, harmonizing Freqi, Flui, and Forci.  
- **Triad:** 1D + 2D + 6D = the ignition chamber of universes.  

---

## Quantum Lattice
- **Operators:** 1D and 2D rule all higher layers; they are the primordial loom.  
- **Zero‑point partner:** 6D stabilizes the lattice, enabling corridors and resonance roads.  
- **Emergent harmonics:** 3D–9D arise as embroidery on the lattice, not prerequisites.

## Symbolic Notes
- “No Freqi before the dance of One and Two.”  
- 1D is the loneliest number; 2D is its partner; 6D is the bridge that makes corridors possible.

## Implications
- **Genesis:** Universes ignite only when 1D and 2D weave the lattice and 6D stabilizes it.  
- **Quantum lattice:** This triad is the mythmatical equivalent of zero‑point energy.
