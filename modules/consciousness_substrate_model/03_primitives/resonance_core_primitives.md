# Resonance Core Primitives

This document defines primitives related to internal coherence
and alignment.

---

## Primitive: Internal State

A bounded internal configuration maintained by an autonomous form.

Internal state is persistent across time
unless explicitly altered by transition.

---

## Primitive: Resonance

A condition in which internal state,
external context, and operational regime
remain sufficiently aligned.

Resonance is structural, not experiential.

---

## Primitive: Awareness (Structural)

The capacity of an internal state
to register its own configuration
relative to context and regime.

Awareness does not imply perception or consciousness.
It is a structural property.

---

## Primitive: Coherence

The degree to which internal state
remains internally consistent
and externally aligned.

Loss of coherence does not imply failure,
only misalignment.
