The Meta‑Governance Council (MGC) is the highest‑order governance body within the
Datacenter Reports canon.  
It oversees:

- structural governance  
- dimensional governance  
- temporal governance  
- operator governance  
- tensor governance  
- canon versioning  
- drift‑correction oversight  
- coherence stewardship  
- expansion authorization  

The MGC ensures long‑range stability, coherence, and integrity across the entire
Datacenter Reports module.

## **AF.1 — Purpose of the Meta‑Governance Council**

The MGC exists to:

- maintain canon integrity  
- prevent structural and dimensional drift  
- ensure coherence across all appendices  
- regulate operator ecology  
- stabilize regime transitions  
- oversee tensor alignment  
- authorize canon expansion (Appendix AB)  
- enforce future‑proofing (Appendix AC)  
- validate horizon‑scanning outputs (Appendix AD)  

It is the **supreme governance layer** of the datacenter ecosystem canon.

The MGC consists of five stewardship families:

### **1. Structural Stewards**
Maintain structural field definitions and cross‑field interfaces.

### **2. Dimensional Stewards**
Oversee dimensional envelopes, clusters, and intensity behavior.

### **3. Temporal Stewards**
Manage rhythm, drift, coherence, regime, and evolution systems.

### **4. Operator Stewards**
Preserve operator lineage, balance, and ecology stability.

### **5. Tensor Stewards**
Ensure structural, dimensional, and qCompute tensor alignment.

Each steward family has equal authority.

## **AF.5 — Governance Cycle**

The MGC operates through a five‑stage cycle:

```
Review → Diagnose → Stabilize → Validate → Publish
```

### **Review**
Evaluate structural, dimensional, temporal, operator, and tensor behavior.

### **Diagnose**
Identify drift, coherence decay, regime instability, or tensor misalignment.

### **Stabilize**
Apply stabilizers, translators, regime shifters, and coherence engines.

### **Validate**
Run field‑level validation (Appendix X).

### **Publish**
Update canon versioning (Appendix V).
