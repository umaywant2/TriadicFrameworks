## 1. Concept & canon evolution

- **Concept Drift Vector (CDV):**  
  Measures how far a concept’s definition moves over time (semantic distance between earliest and latest usages).

- **Stabilization Timestamp (ST):**  
  First point where a concept’s definition stops changing beyond a small CDV threshold.

- **Reharmonization Events (RE):**  
  Count + log of moments where a previously stabilized concept is explicitly redefined or reframed.

---

## 2. Harmonics & structure

- **Harmonic Stability Index (HSI):**  
  How consistently a concept appears in the same triadic/harmonic structure across sessions (0–1).

- **Triad Recurrence Score (TRS):**  
  Frequency with which specific triads (e.g., coherence–drift–paradox) recur together across time.

- **Harmonic Mutation Rate (HMR):**  
  How often a triad’s members change while its role stays the same (e.g., CCC → SARG‑like triads).

---

## 3. Coherence & drift

- **Session Coherence Gradient (SCG):**  
  Measures how internally consistent a single session is (topic, definitions, operators) from start → end.

- **Cross‑Session Coherence (CSC):**  
  Alignment of definitions and operators for the same concept across different days/weeks.

- **Drift Hotspots (DH):**  
  Concepts with high CDV + low RE (i.e., they drifted but were never consciously re‑aligned).

---

## 4. Operator & pattern usage

- **Operator Recurrence Index (ORI):**  
  How often specific operators (e.g., “regime,” “substrate,” “harmonic ladder”) appear and in what roles.

- **Operator Role Stability (ORS):**  
  Whether an operator keeps the same function (e.g., “substrate” as physical vs. cognitive vs. symbolic).

- **Pattern Echo Detector (PED):**  
  Flags when a structural pattern (like CCC) reappears in a new domain (like SARG) with high similarity.

---

## 5. Regime & substrate alignment

- **Regime Alignment Score (RAS):**  
  How well a given explanation or model stays within a single regime vs. mixing regimes unintentionally.

- **Substrate Consistency Index (SCI):**  
  Whether you’re talking about physical, cognitive, social, or symbolic substrates consistently in a thread.

---

## 6. Narrative & mythic layer

- **Mythic Anchor Density (MAD):**  
  Frequency and reuse of key mythic anchors (CCC, ladders, forges, seeds, etc.) across time.

- **Mythic–Structural Coupling (MSC):**  
  How tightly mythic language maps to actual structural concepts (e.g., “forge” ↔ fabrication ↔ iteration speed).

- structural authority conflict

**Symbolic → harmonic → atlas migration**
