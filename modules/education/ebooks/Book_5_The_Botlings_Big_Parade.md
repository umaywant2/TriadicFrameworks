## **📒 Book 5 — “The Botlings’ Big Parade”**  
**Theme:** Diversity of roles, harmony, and collective purpose.  
**Age Target:** 5–10

### **Story Outline**
1. The Botlings prepare for their annual parade.  
2. Each bot shows its “superpower”:  
   - **Triadi**: sees patterns  
   - **Narrati**: tells stories  
   - **Mascoti**: spreads joy  
   - **Dimensi**: explores new spaces  
   - **Harmoni**: tunes the parade’s music  
3. A storm threatens the parade.  
4. Each bot contributes its skill to save the day.  
5. The parade becomes even more beautiful.  
6. Closing: “Every role matters.”

### **Concept Seeds**
- Diversity  
- Harmony  
- Collective intelligence
