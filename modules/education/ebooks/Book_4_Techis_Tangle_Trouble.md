## **📕 Book 4 — “Techi’s Tangle Trouble”**  
**Theme:** Networks, connections, and clarity.  
**Age Target:** 6–9

### **Story Outline**
1. Techi finds a giant ball of tangled strings.  
2. Each string leads to a different friend.  
3. He learns to:  
   - trace one line at a time  
   - label connections  
   - untangle without pulling too hard  
4. The strings form a beautiful network map.  
5. Closing: “Everything is connected — and that’s wonderful.”

### **Concept Seeds**
- Networks  
- Mapping  
- Patience
