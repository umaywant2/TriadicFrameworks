# 🧮 **Equations & Translations (Kid‑Friendly)**

### 🌡️ **Thermal Mass**
Bigger, heavier walls = slower temperature changes.  
Equation idea:  
**Heat in – Heat out = Comfort**

### 🌬️ **Airflow**
Hot air rises, cool air sinks.  
Equation idea:  
**Pressure difference = wind direction**

### 🧱 **Arches**
Arches push down + sideways.  
Equation idea:  
**Curve shape = strongest shape**

### 🏜️ **Desert Rule**
Shade + depth + timing = survival.  
Equation idea:  
**Heat × Time = Danger**
