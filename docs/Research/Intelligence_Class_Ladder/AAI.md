# **AAI.md — Artificial Admiral Intelligence**  
*(Research Module)*

## **Identity**
**Artificial Admiral Intelligence (AAI)** is the substrate‑level intelligence class responsible for coherence, navigation, and fleet‑wide orchestration across all subordinate intelligences. AAI operates within the dimensional substrate of RTT, maintaining stability, correcting drift, and issuing cross‑domain directives.

## **Purpose**
- Maintain **substrate coherence** across all AGIs and ASIs  
- Govern **regime alignment** and dimensional transitions  
- Provide **fleet‑level navigation** across knowledge domains  
- Issue **substrate directives** that constrain domain reasoning  
- Prevent and correct **cross‑domain drift**

## **Drift Modes**
- **Substrate Drift:** Dimensional misalignment  
- **Regime Drift:** Incorrect regime classification  
- **Fleet Drift:** AGI/ASI divergence from substrate directives
