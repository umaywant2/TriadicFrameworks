> This index maps the three primary intelligence classifications within the TriadicFrameworks Intelligence Class Ladder. Each module documents the capabilities, thresholds, and theoretical boundaries that define its respective class.

---

## Overview

The Intelligence Class Ladder is a structured framework for distinguishing between ascending tiers of artificial cognition — from narrow, task-scoped systems to the theoretical apex of superintelligent capability. The three modules below form the core triadic structure.

---

## Modules

### 1. Artificial Applied Intelligence (AAI)

Covers intelligence systems bounded by specific domains and predefined task sets. AAI represents the current operational reality of deployed AI — capable within scope, but lacking generalization.

→ [Read AAI Module](./AAI.md)

---

### 2. Artificial General Intelligence (AGI)

Addresses the threshold at which a system can perform any intellectual task a human can, with cross-domain reasoning, learning transfer, and autonomous goal-setting. AGI marks the pivotal transition from narrow to broad cognition.

→ [Read AGI Module](./AGI.md)

---

### 3. Artificial Superintelligence (ASI)

Explores intelligence that surpasses all human cognitive capacity across every dimension — scientific reasoning, creativity, social intelligence, and strategic planning. ASI represents the upper bound of the Intelligence Class Ladder.

→ [Read ASI Module](./ASI.md)

---

## Class Ladder at a Glance

| Class | Scope | Key Characteristic | Status |

| AGI   | Broad / Human-equivalent | Cross-domain reasoning & transfer | Theoretical |
