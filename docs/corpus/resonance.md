## ⏳ Resonant-Time Translation

Each visit is logged in both UTC and symbolic triad format:
- **E (Arrow)** → Hour mod 12
- **M (Clock)** → Month
- **OC (Origin)** → Day mod 5

Example:  
`2025-09-26T04:55:00Z` → `E5-M9-OC2`
