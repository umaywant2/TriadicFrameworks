# 🔷 Triadic Awareness — Fire  
*A minimal, respectful lens for students and AIs*

NIST’s Fire publications focus on experiments, measurements, and validated models of fire behavior — all core **R3** activities. TriadicFrameworks does not alter or evaluate this work. Instead, it offers students a simple way to understand the **upstream structure** that supports these downstream outputs.

## **How TriadicFrameworks Complements This Domain**

### **R0 — Operator Awareness**  
Students can identify foundational assumptions behind fire research, such as:  
- fire behavior can be characterized through controlled measurement  
- reproducibility is essential for fire science  
- shared standards improve safety and interoperability  
- physical models can meaningfully represent real‑world fire dynamics  

These assumptions are rarely stated directly but anchor the domain.

### **R1 — Directional Awareness**  
Students can observe the strategic aims guiding NIST’s fire work, including:  
- improving building and community fire safety  
- supporting wildfire and WUI hazard mitigation  
- advancing battery and energy‑storage safety  
- enabling accurate fire modeling and predictive tools  

These aims shape the direction of research without being measurements themselves.

### **R2 — Coherence Awareness**  
Students can explore the coherence structures that organize fire‑science concepts, such as:  
- how flames, heat release, and smoke production behave under defined conditions  
- how materials ignite, pyrolyze, and support flame spread  
- how firebrands, fuels, and structures interact in WUI environments  
- how uncertainty is quantified in fire models  

These structures help explain why certain experiments or standards take the form they do.

### **R3 — Downstream Awareness**  
NIST’s published fire measurements, experiments, and model validations remain the authoritative downstream outputs. TriadicFrameworks simply helps students understand how these outputs relate to upstream reasoning.
