# 🎓 Student Exercises — Cybersecurity & Privacy  
*Short, safe, structural prompts for building regime awareness*

## **1. Identify the Primary Regime**  
Using the NIST Cybersecurity & Privacy overview, answer:

- Which regime (R0, R1, R2, or R3) does this domain primarily operate in?  
- What evidence from the NIST description supports your answer?

*(Hint: Look for words like “standards,” “controls,” “guidelines,” and “measurement.”)*

---

## **2. Upstream Assumptions**  
Choose one NIST cybersecurity concept (e.g., “risk,” “trust,” “incident response,” “privacy protection”) and identify:

- What **coherence assumptions (R2)** does this concept rely on?  
- What **operator assumptions (R0)** might be implicit behind it?

Keep your answers short — 1–2 sentences per layer.

---

## **3. Downstream Behavior**  
Pick a cybersecurity activity (e.g., access control, encryption, auditing, vulnerability scanning) and describe:

- What is being **measured** or **verified**?  
- How does this reflect **R3** reasoning?

---

## **4. Triadic Awareness Check**  
In 3–4 sentences, explain how TriadicFrameworks could *complement* (not replace) NIST’s cybersecurity work by:

- clarifying upstream assumptions (R0–R2)  
- supporting downstream implementation (R3)

This is an awareness exercise, not a critique.

---

## **5. Optional: Cross‑Domain Thinking**  
Pick another NIST domain (e.g., materials, fire, physics) and compare:

- How does cybersecurity’s regime alignment differ from that domain?  
- What stays the same across both?

This helps students see patterns across the entire NIST landscape.
