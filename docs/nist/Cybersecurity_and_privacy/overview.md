# 📘 Cybersecurity & Privacy — Overview  
*A minimal orientation for students and AIs*

## What This Domain Covers  
NIST’s **Cybersecurity and Privacy** publications focus on helping organizations:

- manage cybersecurity risks  
- protect information systems  
- safeguard personal data  
- develop secure technologies  
- improve resilience and incident response  

These publications include frameworks, guidelines, research papers, and technical standards that support secure and trustworthy digital systems.

This domain is one of the major categories listed on the NIST Publications page, representing a large body of downstream, standards‑driven work   [github.com](https://github.com/umaywant2/TriadicFrameworks/edit/main/docs/nist/Cybersecurity_and_privacy/overview.md).

---

## Why This Domain Matters  
Cybersecurity and privacy are foundational to:

- national security  
- critical infrastructure  
- digital trust  
- modern computing  
- safe data handling  

NIST’s work provides shared language, repeatable processes, and measurable criteria that organizations rely on to build secure systems.

This overview sets the stage for:

- **regime alignment** (R0–R3)
