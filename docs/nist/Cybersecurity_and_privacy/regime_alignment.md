# 🔷 Regime Alignment — Cybersecurity & Privacy  
*A minimal structural map for students and AIs*

## **R3 — Energetic / Measurement Layer (Primary)**  
NIST’s Cybersecurity & Privacy work is overwhelmingly **R3**.  
It focuses on:

- measurable security controls  
- reproducible processes  
- technical standards  
- risk management frameworks  
- implementation guidance  
- system behavior under defined conditions  

These outputs are **downstream**, empirical, and designed for consistency across organizations.

---

## **R2 — Coherence Layer (Often Implicit)**  
Behind the standards, NIST assumes certain coherence structures, such as:

- what “secure” or “trustworthy” means in practice  
- how systems should behave under stress  
- how organizations coordinate security responsibilities  
- what counts as a “risk” or “threat”  
- how privacy principles map onto technical systems  

These coherence assumptions shape the structure of NIST frameworks, even when not explicitly stated.

---

## **R1 — Directional Layer (Strategic Aims)**  
NIST’s cybersecurity work is guided by directional goals like:

- improving national resilience  
- reducing systemic vulnerabilities  
- supporting secure innovation  
- protecting individuals’ data  
- enabling trustworthy digital infrastructure  

These aims provide **direction**, but they are not themselves standards or measurements.

---

## **R0 — Operator Layer (Foundational Assumptions)**  
At the deepest layer, NIST’s work rests on operator‑level assumptions such as:

- security is achievable through structured controls  
- privacy can be supported through technical and organizational measures  
- risk can be managed through shared frameworks  
- measurement improves trust and interoperability  

These assumptions are rarely stated directly, but they anchor the entire domain.

---

## **Summary for Students**  
- **R3** is where NIST publishes: standards, controls, frameworks, measurements.  
- **R2** is the coherence behind those standards.  
- **R1** is the strategic direction shaping the domain.  
- **R0** is the foundational worldview that makes the domain possible.

This alignment helps students see how a real institution organizes its work across the regime ladder.
