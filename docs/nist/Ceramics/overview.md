# 📘 Ceramics — Overview  
*A minimal orientation for students and AIs*

## What This Domain Covers  
NIST’s **Ceramics** publications focus on the measurement, modeling, and characterization of ceramic materials across structural, thermal, electrical, and processing contexts. The publication list includes work in:

- sintering behavior and densification  
- microstructure evolution and grain‑boundary phenomena  
- fracture mechanics and toughness characterization  
- thermal conductivity and high‑temperature performance  
- dielectric and ferroelectric ceramics  
- refractories and extreme‑environment materials  
- additive manufacturing of ceramic components  
- phase equilibria and thermodynamic modeling  

These topics appear throughout the NIST Ceramics publication listings, reflecting a domain centered on precision measurement, material reliability, and reproducible processing science.

---

## Why This Domain Matters  
Ceramics underpin technologies in:

- aerospace and energy systems  
- electronics and communication devices  
- biomedical implants and protective materials  
- manufacturing, refractories, and high‑temperature infrastructure  
- sensors, actuators, and dielectric components  

NIST’s work ensures that ceramic materials can be measured, modeled, and compared consistently across laboratories, industries, and applications.
