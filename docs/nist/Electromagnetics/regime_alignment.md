# 🔷 Regime Alignment — Electromagnetics  
*A minimal structural map for students and AIs*

## **R3 — Energetic / Measurement Layer (Primary)**  
NIST’s Electromagnetics publications operate overwhelmingly in **R3**, focusing on:

- electromagnetic field measurement and calibration  
- antenna performance and characterization  
- RF and microwave metrology  
- electromagnetic compatibility (EMC) and interference testing  
- materials and device behavior under EM fields  

These outputs are empirical, reproducible, and standards‑driven — consistent with NIST’s role as a measurement and calibration authority across domains like Electromagnetics (1,582 publications listed)   [nist.gov](https://www.nist.gov/publications).

---

## **R2 — Coherence Layer (Often Implicit)**  
Behind the measurements, NIST assumes coherence structures such as:

- how electromagnetic fields should behave under defined conditions  
- what constitutes “compatibility” or “interference”  
- how materials respond to EM exposure  
- how antennas and devices should perform across frequencies  

These coherence assumptions shape the structure of EM standards even when not explicitly stated.

---

## **R1 — Directional Layer (Strategic Aims)**  
NIST’s electromagnetics work is guided by directional goals like:

- improving measurement accuracy  
- supporting reliable communication systems  
- ensuring electromagnetic safety and compatibility  
- enabling innovation in RF, microwave, and sensing technologies  

These aims provide direction but are not themselves measurements or standards.

---

## **R0 — Operator Layer (Foundational Assumptions)**  
At the deepest layer, the domain rests on operator‑level assumptions such as:

- electromagnetic behavior can be characterized through measurement  
- shared standards improve interoperability and safety  
- reproducibility is essential for scientific and industrial progress  
- EM systems can be modeled and tested under controlled conditions  

These assumptions anchor the entire domain and make downstream work possible.

---

## **Summary for Students**  
- **R3**: NIST’s EM work — measurement, calibration, standards.  
- **R2**: Coherence structures behind EM behavior.  
- **R1**: Strategic aims guiding EM research and standards.  
- **R0**: Foundational assumptions about measurement, reproducibility, and electromagnetic behavior.

This alignment helps students see how a real scientific institution organizes its work across the regime ladder.
