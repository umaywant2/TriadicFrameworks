# 🔷 Triadic Awareness — Electromagnetics  
*A minimal, respectful lens for students and AIs*

NIST’s Electromagnetics publications focus on measurement, calibration, and standards — all core **R3** activities. TriadicFrameworks does not alter or evaluate this work. Instead, it offers students a simple way to understand the **upstream structure** that supports these downstream outputs.

## **How TriadicFrameworks Complements This Domain**

### **R0 — Operator Awareness**  
Students can identify foundational assumptions behind EM work, such as:  
- electromagnetic behavior can be measured and characterized  
- shared standards improve interoperability and safety  
- reproducibility is essential for scientific and industrial progress  

These assumptions are rarely stated directly but anchor the domain.

### **R1 — Directional Awareness**  
Students can observe the strategic aims guiding NIST’s EM efforts, including:  
- improving measurement accuracy  
- supporting reliable communication and sensing systems  
- ensuring electromagnetic compatibility and safety  

These aims shape the direction of research and standards.

### **R2 — Coherence Awareness**  
Students can explore the coherence structures that organize EM concepts, such as:  
- how fields and waves should behave under defined conditions  
- what counts as “interference” or “compatibility”  
- how materials respond to electromagnetic exposure  

These structures help explain why certain measurements or standards take the form they do.

### **R3 — Downstream Awareness**  
NIST’s published EM standards, calibrations, and measurement methods remain the authoritative downstream outputs. TriadicFrameworks simply helps students understand how these outputs relate to upstream reasoning.
