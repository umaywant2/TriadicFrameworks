# 📘 Electromagnetics — Overview  
*A minimal orientation for students and AIs*

## What This Domain Covers  
NIST’s **Electromagnetics** publications focus on the science, measurement, and standards associated with electromagnetic fields, waves, and interactions. This includes work such as:

- electromagnetic field measurement and calibration  
- antenna characterization and performance  
- radio‑frequency (RF) and microwave metrology  
- electromagnetic compatibility (EMC) and interference studies  
- materials and device behavior under EM fields  

These publications support accurate measurement, reliable communication systems, and consistent electromagnetic standards across industries.

---

## Why This Domain Matters  
Electromagnetics underpins technologies used in:

- wireless communication  
- radar and sensing  
- navigation systems  
- medical imaging  
- materials testing  
- national defense and infrastructure  

NIST’s work ensures that these systems operate predictably, safely, and in alignment with shared measurement standards.
