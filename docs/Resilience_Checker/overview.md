# Resilience Checker

**Resilience Checker** is a structured diagnostic surface — 108 canonical paradox analyses spanning logic, physics, computation, cosmology, and philosophy. Each paradox is treated as a structural stress test, not a contradiction to be eliminated. The module builds regime literacy progressively, culminating in a capstone arc that targets the hardest problems at the frontier of computation and emergence.

## Structure

The 108 analyses are arranged in a layered progression:

- **Early paradoxes** — Classical logic and philosophical cases; foundational regime literacy
- **Mid-tier paradoxes** — Physics, computation, and cosmological cases; cross-domain transfer
- **Capstone arc (101–108)** — Computation, modeling, emergence, and causation; the structural frontier

The capstone arc is deliberately open-ended — these are the paradoxes where SET decomposition reveals the most about the limits of the current framework.
