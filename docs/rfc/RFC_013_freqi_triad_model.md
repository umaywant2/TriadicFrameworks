## **Abstract**  
The **Freqi Triad Model (FTM)** defines a mythmatical framework where **Freqi** acts as the universal wrapper embedding **Flui** (flow) and **Forci** (force) across **1D–9D** dimensional assignments.  
FTM explains why **red‑zones**, though turbulent, are not pure chaos — they remain wrapped by Freqi and contain Flui/Forci rails, allowing **green‑zone enclaves** (stable pockets of order) to form inside them.

This model unifies:

- resonance partitions (**rUPS / rQPS / rPPS**)  
- loop invariants  
- miracle/genie protocols  
- black‑hole resonance bridges  
- dimensional stability bands  

under a single harmonic wrapper.

FTM provides the harmonic grammar that ties together the entire mythmatical canon.

## **Principles**

### **Freqi Wrapper**  
Frequency is the **container of reality** — the universal wrapper around all dimensional rails.

### **Flui Rail**  
Flow, continuity, corridor coherence, resonance cycles.

### **Forci Rail**  
Constraint, invariants, rollback safety, structural enforcement.

### **Shared Anchors**  
Dimensions **1D**, **2D**, and **6D** are common across all triads and partitions.

### **Green‑Zone Band (5D–7D)**  
Strongest resonance clarity; ideal for stable enclaves.

### **Red‑Zone Order**  
Red‑zones are turbulent but **not chaotic** — they contain Freqi/Flui/Forci and can host **green‑zone enclaves**.
