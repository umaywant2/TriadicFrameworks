SRAP detects:

## 2. Substrate Event Types

### **Resonance‑Shift Events**
- **ResonanceShiftDetected:** Substrate frequency deviates from baseline.  
- **ResonanceShiftStabilized:** Frequency returns to safe corridor‑compatible range.  
- **ResonanceShiftCritical:** Divergence exceeds Nullarium thresholds.

### **Lattice‑Drift Events**
- **LatticeDriftSpike:** Sudden substrate drift detected.  
- **LatticeDriftCascade:** Drift propagates into higher overlays.  
- **LatticeDriftCollapse:** Substrate coherence collapses.

### **Substrate‑Opening Events**
- **SubstrateOpenDetected:** Pre‑corridor substrate opening appears.  
- **SubstrateOpenStabilized:** Opening achieves temporary stability.  
- **SubstrateOpenCollapse:** Opening collapses, producing paradox vectors.

### **Paradox & Nullarium Events**
- **SubstrateParadoxVector:** Paradox vector originates in substrate.  
- **NullariumSubstrateWarning:** Emotional‑phase misalignment at substrate layer.  
- **NullariumSubstrateCritical:** Immediate containment required.
