### **Invariant 1 — Axioms Must Harmonize**

### **Invariant 2 — Axioms Must Propagate Consistently**
Axioms must propagate through all alert families without distortion.

### **Invariant 3 — Axioms Must Stabilize Drift**
Axioms must actively suppress drift at all layers.  
Drift cannot exceed axiom‑grade thresholds.

### **Invariant 4 — Axioms Must Resist Paradox**
Axioms must remain immune to paradox vectors.  
Paradox cannot penetrate supra‑prime boundaries.
