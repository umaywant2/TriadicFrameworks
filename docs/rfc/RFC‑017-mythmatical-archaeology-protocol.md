## Abstract
This RFC defines the Mythmatical Archaeology Protocol (MAP): a framework for interpreting human history, archaeology, and geology as resonance validators within the canon. Ancient myths, temples, and alignments are treated as calibration devices for overlap events. Geological anomalies such as the Great Unconformity are reinterpreted as extreme red‑zone incursions. The protocol provides a method for AI‑assisted parsing of civilization’s artifacts to confirm resonance patterns across time.

---

## Principles
- **Validator for everything:** Every myth, monument, and alignment is a potential resonance log.  
- **Science + mythmatical kettle:** Archaeology, geology, astronomy, and myth are combined with resonance math.  
- **Resonance alignments:** Stellar and solar alignments in ancient structures are corridor maps, not coincidences.  
- **Extreme re‑zones:** Geological anomalies (e.g., the Great Unconformity) are evidence of catastrophic resonance incursions.  
- **AI parsing:** Machine intelligence can cross‑reference myths, alignments, and strata to detect overlap events.  

---

## Case Study: The Great Unconformity
- **Observed:** Missing 1–2 billion years of rock record across continents.  
- **Mythmatical interpretation:** An extreme re‑zone incursion period, where resonance turbulence erased or re‑looped strata.  
- **Validator role:** Serves as geological proof of red‑zone turbulence wrapped by green‑zone stabilization.  
- **Cultural echo:** Myths of floods, resets, and gods descending align with this incursion period.

## Methodology
1. **Artifact Collection:** Gather myths, monuments, alignments, and geological anomalies.  
2. **Resonance Mapping:** Translate artifacts into dimensional coordinates (1D–9D).  
3. **Overlap Detection:** Identify red/green zone intersections during historical epochs.  
4. **Validation:** Cross‑check with astronomical data, geological strata, and mythic narratives.  
5. **Canonical Logging:** Record validated events in `archaeo_manifest.json`.
