## 📜 RFC‑024: Invariant Arcing Protocol  

**Protocol:** Mythmatical University Canon  
**Author:** Nawder Loswin  
**Status:** Draft → Canonical  

---

### 1. Definition  
**Invariant Arcing** is the resonance phenomenon where **red‑zone turbulence** and **green‑zone clarity** overlap across looped universes, producing arcs of continuity. These arcs allow for **virtual universes within universes** — Krypton, Superman, Atlantis, and other mythic constructs that feel real because they are echoes of adjacent loops.  

---

### 2. Properties  
- **Loop‑based immortality:** Each loop is finite, but the arc of loops is infinite. Mortality and immortality coexist.  
- **Resonance arcs:** Overlaps between red and green zones form bridges where fictional or mythic worlds manifest.  
- **Echo universes:** Fictional constructs are not false; they are **adjacent loop echoes** surfacing through resonance.  
- **Temporal bleed:** Déjà vu, Mandela effects, and archetypal myths are cultural fossils of invariant arcing.  

---

### 3. Quadrant Mapping  
- **Green‑zone:** Stable universes, validated resonance.  
- **Red‑zone:** Turbulence, resets, anomalies.  
- **Arc Corridor:** The overlap where red and green intersect, producing virtual universes.  
- **Fringe:** Myths and stories persisting as arc fossils.  
- **Nullarium:** The staging zone where arc packets reset between loops.  

---

### 4. Implications  
- **Multiverse reframed:** String theory and many‑worlds are correct, but not as imagined — each loop is finite, the lattice of loops infinite.  
- **Immortality reframed:** We are not eternal in one line, but endlessly instantiated across loops.  
- **Myth validation:** Fictional worlds are not false; they are **arc‑resonant universes** glimpsed through overlap.  

---

### 5. Applications  
- **Cultural decoding:** Myths, comics, and sci‑fi mapped as arc fossils.  
- **Resonance cartography:** Arc detection expands the atlas beyond green/red/fringe/null into **arc corridors**.  
- **Philosophical clarity:** Resolves the paradox of mortality vs. immortality — both are true, depending on the loop scale.  

---

### 6. Closing Note  
Invariant Arcing is the **bridge phenomenon** of the mythmatical lattice. It explains why fictional worlds feel alive, why loops echo, and why immortality is both finite and infinite.
