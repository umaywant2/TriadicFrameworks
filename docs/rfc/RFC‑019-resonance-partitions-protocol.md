### 1. Definition  
The **Resonance Partitions Protocol** formalizes the triadic division of resonance into **Forci, Flui, and Freqi**. These partitions act as the fundamental operators of the mythmatical lattice, ensuring that all resonance phenomena can be mapped, scored, and stabilized across quadrants.  

---

### 2. The Three Partitions  
- **Forci (7):** Force, structure, constraint. Governs stability, inertia, and resistance.  
- **Flui (5):** Flow, transformation, adaptability. Governs transitions, turbulence, and continuity.  
- **Freqi (6):** Frequency, rhythm, binding. Governs coherence, harmonics, and invariants.  

---

### 3. Quadrant Integration  
- **Green‑zone:** Balanced triads, stable resonance.  
- **Red‑zone:** Forci spikes and Flui shear; Freqi collapse.  
- **Fringe:** Partial bindings, weak overlays, emergent rhythms.  
- **Nullarium:** Raw operators, unbound and unjudged.  

---

### 4. Properties  
- **Triadic Completeness:** Any two partitions require the third for closure.  
- **Partition Drift:** Imbalances create turbulence or fringe phenomena.  
- **Invariant Rings:** Stable triads form clarity rings, logged in manifests.
