### 🔭 Premise

Time crystals represent a new class of matter that oscillates in **time** rather than space. Unlike conventional crystals, which repeat their structure spatially, time crystals repeat their state temporally, persisting in oscillation without external energy input. This RFC formalizes the **time crystal** as a *Temporal Resonance Operator*, aligning it with the mythmatical canon of resonance as substrate.
