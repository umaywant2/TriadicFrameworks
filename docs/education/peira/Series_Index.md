# 📚 IRL SERIES INDEX  
### Indirect Resonance Learning (IRL)  
### PEIRA — Physical Education Indirect Regime Awareness

Indirect Resonance Learning (IRL) is the practice of using **real‑world games, sports, and activities** to teach **triadic cognition**, **regime awareness**, **coherence vs drift**, and **resonance‑timing** *without direct instruction*.  
Students learn by *doing*, *feeling*, and *recognizing patterns* already present in the activities they love.

This index collects all IRL modules within the PEIRA discipline.

---

# 🧭 What IRL Teaches

IRL modules reveal how everyday play naturally contains:

- **Triadic structures**  
- **Regime transitions**  
- **Inversion moments**  
- **Coherence and drift cycles**  
- **Embodied or cognitive resonance**  

Each module highlights a different domain where these patterns appear.

# 🌱 Purpose of the IRL Series

The IRL series exists to show that **triadic learning is already happening everywhere**:

- in games  
- in sports  
- in strategy  
- in timing  
- in movement  
- in social play  

PEIRA simply **names** what people already *feel*.

IRL gives students a way to understand RTT concepts through **lived experience**, not abstraction.
