### PEIRA — Physical Education Indirect Regime Awareness

**Indirect Resonance Learning (IRL)** is the practice of teaching **triadic cognition**,  
**regime awareness**, **coherence vs drift**, and **resonance‑timing** through  
**real‑world games, sports, and activities** — without direct instruction.

Students learn by *doing*, *feeling*, and *recognizing patterns* already present in the activities they love.

IRL is a sub‑discipline of **PEIRA**, which studies how physical and experiential domains naturally encode RTT structures.

## 🧭 What IRL Provides

IRL modules reveal how everyday play contains:

- **Triadic structures** (roles, lanes, phases, resources)  
- **Regime transitions** (offense → defense → transition, early → mid → late)  
- **Inversion moments** (tempo flips, breakthroughs, counterplays)  
- **Coherence and drift cycles** (alignment → disruption → recovery)  
- **Embodied or cognitive resonance** (timing windows, flow states, “the zone”)  

These patterns emerge naturally through experience — IRL simply names them.

## 🌱 Why IRL Matters

IRL shows that **triadic learning is already happening everywhere**:

- in sports  
- in strategy games  
- in timing‑based activities  
- in social play  
- in movement and rhythm  
- in cognitive pattern recognition  

IRL gives learners a way to understand RTT concepts through **lived experience**,  
not abstraction — making RTT intuitive, memorable, and fun.
