## **📘 About the Book**

*Grammar for Intelligence* explains the missing structural layer beneath modern AI systems — the grammar that determines how systems behave, evolve, and drift. Instead of treating AI as a collection of features or prompts, the book reveals the operators, invariants, regimes, substrates, and governance structures that make intelligence coherent.

The book is concise, high‑signal, and designed for:

- AI builders and engineers  
- researchers and students  
- governance designers  
- founders building AI‑native companies  
- structural intelligence practitioners
