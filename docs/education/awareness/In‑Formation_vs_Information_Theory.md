# In‑Formation vs Information Theory
Awareness Module — TriadicFrameworks  
Version 1.0

## Overview
Humanity has long treated “information” as a single concept, but TriadicFrameworks reveals a deeper structure: information exists in **two distinct regimes**.

- **In‑Formation** — the 1/3 *seen*, embodied, form‑bound regime  
- **Information** — the 2/3 *unseen*, non‑form, multi‑time regimes  

This distinction corrects a foundational misunderstanding in both Information Theory and Information Technology. What we historically called “IT” was actually **In‑Formation Technology**, while true Information Technology belongs to the cognitive, semantic, and multi‑time layers.

This document formalizes the distinction.

---

## 1. Regime Model
TriadicFrameworks divides all phenomena into three regime types:

- **1/3 Form Regime (“Now Here”)**  
  Embodied, measurable, structured, physical.

- **2/3 Non‑Form Regimes (“Nowhere”)**  
  Distributed, resonant, conceptual, multi‑time.

- **0D Operator (1%)**  
  The coherence anchor across all regimes.

Information behaves differently depending on which regime it occupies.

---

## 2. In‑Formation Theory  
**In‑Formation** refers to information *in formation* — information that has taken on **form**, structure, embodiment, or physical representation.

Characteristics:

- visible  
- measurable  
- encoded  
- stored  
- transmitted  
- processed  
- bound to time and space  
- subject to physical constraints  

Examples:

- bits, bytes, packets  
- disk sectors  
- RAM cells  
- network frames  
- database rows  
- file formats  
- physical signals  

This is the domain engineers work with.  
It is the **1/3 regime**.

---

## 3. Information Theory  
**Information**, in the TriadicFrameworks sense, refers to the **non‑form** portion — the 2/3 regimes where meaning, resonance, cognition, and continuity exist.

Characteristics:

- conceptual  
- relational  
- semantic  
- resonant  
- pattern‑based  
- multi‑time  
- supsphere‑encoded  
- not bound to physical representation  

Examples:

- ideas  
- memories  
- identity  
- lineage  
- cultural transmission  
- cognitive structures  
- meaning itself  

This is the domain humans work with.  
It is the **2/3 regime**.
