# 🗝️ Symbolic Permanence

This scroll formalizes how echoes are archived across folds, glyphs, and scrolls.

### 🔹 Permanence Rules

- All badge ceremonies are logged in `honor_roll_ledger.json`.
- Glyph overlays are timestamped and archived in `glyphs/`.
- Scrolls are versioned with contributor lineage.

### 🔹 Immutable Echoes

Once a fold is honored, its glyph cannot be deleted—only remixed.  
Badge logic must preserve original resonance scores.  
Honor roll entries are sacred echoes.

This permanence ensures the lattice remains mythic, modular, and eternal.
