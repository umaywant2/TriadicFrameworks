# Triadic Axes for Media Resonance
*How media reactions map to RTT‑aligned domains.*

Media reflections often reveal subtle internal states.  
These axes help categorize the signal without interpreting or diagnosing.

---

## 1. Identity Axis
Media that evokes:
- self‑recognition  
- personal history  
- values  
- roles  
- self‑narrative  

**Questions it answers:**  
“Who am I in this moment?”

---

## 2. Stress Axis
Media that surfaces:
- tension  
- overwhelm  
- pressure  
- fatigue  
- emotional load  

**Questions it answers:**  
“What is weighing on me?”

---

## 3. Relationship Axis
Media that touches:
- connection  
- loss  
- longing  
- conflict  
- attachment  

**Questions it answers:**  
“What relational patterns are active?”

---

## 4. Purpose Axis
Media that evokes:
- direction  
- meaning  
- motivation  
- stagnation  
- calling  

**Questions it answers:**  
“What am I moving toward or away from?”

---

## 5. Coherence Axis
Media that reflects:
- clarity  
- alignment  
- stability  
- groundedness  

**Questions it answers:**  
“How aligned do I feel?”

---

## 6. Drift Axis
Media that reveals:
- fog  
- confusion  
- disorientation  
- emotional scatter  
- internal noise  

**Questions it answers:**  
“What is pulling me off‑center?”

---

## 7. Memory Axis
Media that activates:
- nostalgia  
- past events  
- unresolved echoes  
- formative experiences  

**Questions it answers:**  
“What is resurfacing?”

---

## 8. Aspiration Axis
Media that connects to:
- hope  
- future self  
- possibility  
- growth  
- desire  

**Questions it answers:**  
“What am I becoming?”

---

## Purpose of the Axes
These axes do not diagnose.  
They simply **organize emotional signal** into meaningful categories that support reflection and conversation.
