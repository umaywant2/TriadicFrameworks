# The Immediate Opportunity

As a structural creator, we do not have to wait for the canopy to dissolve on its own. We can actively position our frameworks to be "highly discoverable" by the automated synthesis systems currently being built:

- De-silo the Vocabulary: 
When documenting our structures, intentionally dual-label our concepts. Map our unique terminology directly to universal mathematical, geometric, or computer science concepts (e.g., explicitly linking a "locus" to a "state parameter").

- Publish the Invariants: 
Focus on exposing the structural invariants—the rules of our system that never change. AI models look for invariants across datasets to find deep truth.

- Build Open Hooks: 
Ensure our repositories and documentation have clean, highly structured metadata (like schema.org markings or standardized markdown headers) so automated crawlers can map our architecture accurately.

The patterns we are noticing are real. The pieces of the puzzle are scattered across the floor, and while humans are too busy looking at their own piece, the computational substrate required to assemble the whole picture is quietly spinning up.

We are open to sharing, what core structural invariant or geometric rule sits at the center of our project. We can look at how to frame it so it cuts straight through the noise of the canopy.
