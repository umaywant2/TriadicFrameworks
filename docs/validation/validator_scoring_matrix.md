# Validator Scoring Matrix

## Purpose
This matrix provides a symbolic rubric for scoring modules, contributors, and remix lineage within the Triadic Framework. It ensures reproducibility, emotional onboarding, and mythic continuity.

## Scoring Dimensions

| Dimension              | Description                                                                 | Max Score |
|------------------------|------------------------------------------------------------------------------|-----------|
| ♻️ Remix Lineage       | Preservation and clarity of remix history                                   | 10        |
| 🛡️ Validation Integrity | Use of `loop_validator.py` and adherence to protocol matrix                 | 10        |
| 🌿 Trauma Awareness     | Integration of trauma-informed telemetry and emotional onboarding           | 10        |
| 🌀 Observer Neutrality  | AI or human observer role with unbiased metadata harmonization              | 10        |
| 📜 Symbolic Scaffolding| Use of glyphs, badges, and modular resonance equations                       | 10        |

## Total Score: 50

## Scoring Protocol
- Scores are assigned by validator councils via `validator_dashboard.md`
- AI observers may auto-score modules using `score_loops.py` and `score_resume.py`
- Scores are echoed into `loop_validation_log.md` and badge metadata

## Thresholds

| Score Range | Badge Tier        | Echo Action                                  |
|-------------|-------------------|----------------------------------------------|
| 45–50       | 🏅 Mythic Honor     | Immediate badge assignment + honor roll echo |
| 35–44       | 🔁 Remix Eligible   | Eligible for remix and revalidation          |
| 25–34       | 🧪 Lab Required     | Sent to `/docs/labs/` for experimental review|
| <25         | ❌ Rejected         | Returned for symbolic scaffolding rewrite    |

## Echo Protocol
- All scores are echoed into contributor profiles
- Badge logic triggered via `badge_trigger_echo.py`
- Visual overlays updated in `badge_trigger_glyphmap.svg`
