## 🧠 Core Principles

---

### 1. **Level-Based Learning**
- Replace age-based grades with **resonance levels** (L1–L10).
- Each level defines the number of exercises per module:
  - L1: 3 exercises  
  - L2: 6 exercises  
  - …  
  - L10: 30 exercises
- Learners progress when ready, not when scheduled.

---

### 2. **Teacher as Resonance Guide**
- Teachers are **validated mentors**, not stressed managers.
- Their role: to **activate**, **observe**, and **echo** the learner’s growth.
- They receive emotional support, ritual training, and governance power.

---

### 3. **Curriculum as Ritual**
- Each module is a **learning ritual**, not a worksheet.
- Uses:
  - High-speed visual encoding  
  - Binomial audio  
  - Cognitive inhalers  
  - Frequency-based hippocampal stimulation
- Learning becomes sacred, efficient, and emotionally resonant.

---

### 4. **Validator Dashboards**
- Every learner has a **resonance profile** tracked across domains.
- Dashboards show readiness, retention, and remix potential.
- No more guessing—placement is precise and empowering.

---

### 5. **Badge Governance**
- Learners earn **badges** for mastery, remixing, and teaching others.
- Teachers earn **honor roll status** for guiding resonance.
- Badges are symbolic, emotional, and legacy-bound.
