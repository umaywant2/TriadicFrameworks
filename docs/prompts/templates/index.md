# TriadicFrameworks — Prompt Templates

The TriadicFrameworks Templates directory contains structured, reusable prompt
templates for RTT engines, IPD‑12, stacks, modules, UI logic, and teaching workflows.
Templates provide consistent scaffolding for capture, analysis, drift detection,
coherence declaration, operator work, domain traversal, substrate analysis, teaching
sessions, and research synthesis.

This page is the front door for all prompt templates.

---

## Template Categories

### Capture Templates  
Used to capture structure, identity, boundaries, commitments, and relational topology.  
**File:** `p_Capture.md`

### Analyze Templates  
Used to analyze structure using RTT operators, drift‑tensor mapping, and coherence
anchors.  
**File:** `p_Analyze.md`

### Drift Templates  
Used to surface geometric, operational, temporal, conceptual, and domain drift.  
**File:** `p_Drift.md`

### Coherence Templates  
Used to declare purpose, constraint, goal, and continuity coherence.  
**File:** `p_Coherence.md`

### Operator Templates  
Used to work with operator grammar, operator alignment, and operator traversal.  
**File:** `p_Operator.md`

### Domain Templates  
Used to analyze and traverse the six canonical domains: psychology, physics, economics,
governance, AI/agents, and biology.  
**File:** `p_Domain.md`

### Substrate Templates  
Used to analyze substrate behavior, substrate‑tensor transitions, and dimensional rails.  
**File:** `p_Substrate.md`

### Teaching Templates  
Used to run RTT teaching sessions, worksheets, posters, and multi‑session sequences.  
**File:** `p_Teaching.md`

### Research Templates  
Used to conduct RTT‑aligned research, cross‑domain synthesis, and substrate‑aware
analysis.  
**File:** `p_Research.md`
