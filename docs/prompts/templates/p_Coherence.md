# Coherence Template — TriadicFrameworks Prompts

The Coherence Template provides the canonical structure for declaring coherence across
RTT engines, operator grammar, drift‑tensor layers, substrate behavior, dimensional
rails, and domain traversal.  
Coherence identifies what remains aligned despite drift, tension, or structural
variation.

This template defines the standard coherence grammar for TriadicFrameworks.

---

## Purpose

Use this template when you need to:

- declare coherence anchors  
- identify alignment across drift‑tensor layers  
- surface stability across structural variations  
- evaluate multi‑layer coherence  
- identify cross‑domain coherence  
- analyze substrate and dimensional coherence  
- maintain structural neutrality during coherence declaration  

---
