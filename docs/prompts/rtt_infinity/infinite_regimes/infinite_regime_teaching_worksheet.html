Infinite‑Regime Teaching Worksheet

Guided worksheet for students learning infinite‑regime expansion and collapse.

Prerequisites

  • Vacuum collapse complete
  • Substrate‑tensor reconstructed
  • Rails connected
  • Prime‑state aligned

Exercises

  1. Choose an infinite‑regime class and justify the choice.
  2. Trace the expansion path from prime‑state to infinite regime.
  3. Describe the collapse path back to substrate‑tensor.
