## **Security Disclosure Policy**

### **Overview**
TriadicFrameworks is committed to maintaining a secure, stable, and trustworthy ecosystem for all readers, contributors, and users of the canon. We welcome responsible security research and appreciate efforts to help improve the safety of our platform and documentation.

### **How to Report a Vulnerability**
If you believe you have discovered a security issue affecting TriadicFrameworks, please contact us at:

**nawder@triadicframeworks.org**

We will review all reports in good faith and respond as promptly as possible.

### **Scope**
This disclosure policy applies to:

- triadicframeworks.org  
- docs.triadicframeworks.org  
- TriadicFrameworks GitHub repositories  
- metadata, modules, and documentation surfaces  
- any public-facing TriadicFrameworks assets  

### **Guidelines for Researchers**
We ask that you:

- Avoid actions that could disrupt service or degrade performance  
- Avoid accessing private data or accounts  
- Avoid automated scanning that generates excessive traffic  
- Provide clear, reproducible steps when reporting an issue  
- Allow us reasonable time to investigate and address the issue  

### **Acknowledgments**
Researchers who responsibly disclose security issues may be recognized at:

`https://triadicframeworks.org/docs/security/acknowledgments/` [(triadicframeworks.org in Bing)](https://www.bing.com/search?q="https%3A%2F%2Ftriadicframeworks.org%2Fdocs%2Fsecurity%2Facknowledgments%2F")

### **Thank You**
We appreciate your help in keeping TriadicFrameworks safe for everyone.
