# **RTT Medical Module — Instructor Rubric**  
### *RTT/1‑Aligned • Zero Drift • Operator‑First • Print‑Optimized*

---

## **Purpose**

This rubric evaluates student mastery of the **six medical operators**:

1. MEDICAL_METADATA_OPERATOR  
2. MEDICAL_DRIFT_OPERATOR  
3. MEDICAL_SUBSTRATE_OPERATOR  
4. MEDICAL_REGIME_OPERATOR  
5. MEDICAL_CONTINUITY_OPERATOR  
6. MEDICAL_SYNTHESIS_OPERATOR  

Students must demonstrate:

- structural reasoning  
- drift awareness  
- substrate literacy  
- regime literacy  
- continuity extraction  
- patient‑safe synthesis  

Students **must not** produce medical advice.

---

# **1. MEDICAL_METADATA_OPERATOR (8 Points)**  
### *Substrate • Noise • Translation • AI • Commercial Layers*

| Level | Criteria |
|-------|----------|
| **8 — Mastery** | Correctly identifies substrate type, stability, noise, translation layers, AI overlays, commercial overlays, accessibility stability, and regime hint for all three regimes. No false positives. |
| **6 — Proficient** | Minor errors in one regime; correctly identifies major overlays and substrate type. |
| **4 — Developing** | Misses 2–3 key metadata elements (e.g., translation drift, AI overlays). |
| **0–2 — Needs Support** | Treats all regimes as similar; fails to detect overlays or substrate differences. |

**Instructor Notes:**  
Students must treat Ping An as **high‑noise, translation‑unstable, AI‑augmented**.

---

# **2. MEDICAL_DRIFT_OPERATOR (8 Points)**  
### *Template • Semantic • Translation • AI • Commercial • Regime Drift*

| Level | Criteria |
|-------|----------|
| **8 — Mastery** | Produces accurate drift maps for all regimes; identifies all drift types; assigns correct drift scores; flags drift sources. |
| **6 — Proficient** | Minor misclassification of drift severity. |
| **4 — Developing** | Misses AI or translation drift; underestimates Ping An drift. |
| **0–2 — Needs Support** | Treats all regimes as low drift; fails to identify drift sources. |

**Instructor Notes:**  
Students must distinguish **semantic drift** from **translation drift**.

---

# **3. MEDICAL_SUBSTRATE_OPERATOR (6 Points)**  
### *Stability • Noise • Interference • Accessibility*

| Level | Criteria |
|-------|----------|
| **6 — Mastery** | Correctly evaluates substrate stability, noise, interference layers, accessibility, and substrate score for all regimes. |
| **4 — Proficient** | Minor errors in stability or interference classification. |
| **2 — Developing** | Misses accessibility or interference layers. |
| **0 — Needs Support** | Treats mobile_html as equivalent to html; ignores interference. |

**Instructor Notes:**  
Students must identify **layer interference** in Ping An.

---

# **4. MEDICAL_REGIME_OPERATOR (6 Points)**  
### *Regime Logic • Signals • Coherence • Shifts*

| Level | Criteria |
|-------|----------|
| **6 — Mastery** | Correctly classifies all regimes; identifies regime signals; evaluates coherence; detects regime shifts. |
| **4 — Proficient** | Minor errors in coherence or signals. |
| **2 — Developing** | Misclassifies one regime; misses regime signals. |
| **0 — Needs Support** | Treats all regimes as clinical; ignores regime logic. |

**Instructor Notes:**  
Students must recognize **public‑health framing** in NHS.

---

# **5. MEDICAL_CONTINUITY_OPERATOR (12 Points)**  
### *Stable Symptoms • Risk Factors • Red Flags • Actions • Differentials*

| Level | Criteria |
|-------|----------|
| **12 — Mastery** | Extracts a correct, regime‑invariant continuity kernel; excludes drift‑sensitive elements; identifies stable symptoms, risks, red flags, actions, and differentials. |
| **9 — Proficient** | Minor inclusion of regime‑specific elements. |
| **6 — Developing** | Includes AI‑generated or commercial elements; misses stable red flags. |
| **0–4 — Needs Support** | Fails to extract continuity kernel; collapses regimes. |

**Instructor Notes:**  
Students must **exclude** AI triage suggestions and commercial prompts.

---

# **6. MEDICAL_SYNTHESIS_OPERATOR (10 Points)**  
### *Stable vs Unstable • Regime‑Specific • Drift‑Sensitive • Patient‑Safe Summary*

| Level | Criteria |
|-------|----------|
| **10 — Mastery** | Produces a drift‑bounded, regime‑aware synthesis; separates regime‑specific elements; identifies substrate risks; includes uncertainty + escalation indicators; summary is patient‑safe. |
| **8 — Proficient** | Minor issues with uncertainty or regime separation. |
| **5 — Developing** | Collapses regimes; misses drift‑sensitive elements; weak uncertainty disclosure. |
| **0–3 — Needs Support** | Produces medical advice; ignores drift; unsafe summary. |

**Instructor Notes:**  
The summary must be **structural**, not prescriptive.

---

# **Instructor Guidance**

- Emphasize **regime literacy**: differences are structural, not contradictory.  
- Emphasize **drift awareness**: especially translation + AI drift in Ping An.  
- Emphasize **substrate literacy**: mobile_html ≠ html.  
- Emphasize **patient safety**: no advice; only structure, uncertainty, escalation.  
- Encourage students to use the example JSON packets as their “data.”
