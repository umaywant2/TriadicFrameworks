# State and Transition Primitives

This document defines primitives governing persistence and change.

---

## Primitive: Persistence

The ability of internal state
to remain stable across time.

Persistence enables continuity
but does not prevent adaptation.

---

## Primitive: Transition

A bounded change in internal state,
context, or regime.

Transitions are expected
and are not treated as errors.

---

## Primitive: Regime

A set of assumptions under which
internal state and behavior remain valid.

Regimes may change without invalidating the system.

---

## Primitive: Boundary

A defined limit separating regimes,
states, or operational domains.

Boundaries enable safe transition
and prevent uncontrolled collapse.
