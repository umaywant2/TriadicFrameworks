# Red Team Questions

The following questions are intended
to challenge assumptions and expose weaknesses.

---

- Can the same outcomes be achieved
  without any internal substrate?

- Does the WR‑SADC core ever become
  an implicit controller?

- Are regime boundaries clearly defined,
  or inferred post‑hoc?

- Does added dimensionality
  improve clarity or obscure it?

- What failure modes remain silent
  until collapse?

Negative answers are not disqualifying.
They inform refinement or rejection.
