# 🧠 Feedback Roles — Research Lenses & Cognitive Modes
### Historian • Mathematician • Physicist • Professor • Resonance Doctor • Wild Card

The **Feedback** folder contains the role‑based cognitive lenses used during
research, analysis, and cross‑domain synthesis throughout the development of
TriadicFrameworks.

Each role represents a distinct way of:
- interpreting evidence  
- framing problems  
- identifying structure  
- generating hypotheses  
- validating coherence  
- exploring alternative perspectives  

These roles were used as **thinking tools**, not personas — a way to rotate the
observer position and reveal different structural features of the same problem.

---

## 🧭 Purpose

This folder documents:
- the techniques used during research  
- the reasoning styles applied across domains  
- the cross‑disciplinary perspectives that shaped the canon  
- the “observer modes” that helped stabilize insight  

Together, these roles form a **multi‑lens feedback system** that supports
clarity, coherence, and structural discovery.

---

## 📂 Contents

- **Historian** — temporal patterns, lineage, drift  
- **Mathematician** — formal structure, invariants, operators  
- **Physicist** — dynamics, fields, resonance, measurement  
- **Professor** — pedagogy, clarity, scaffolding  
- **Resonance Doctor** — coherence, drift, emotional physics  
- **Wild Card** — creative inversion, unexpected mappings  

Each role contributes a unique angle — together forming a unified feedback loop.
