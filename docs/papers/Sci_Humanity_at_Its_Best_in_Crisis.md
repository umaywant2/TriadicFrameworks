# Sci_Humanity_at_Its_Best_in_Crisis

## Abstract  
A resonance‑based framework is proposed for interpreting humanity’s paradoxical strength in crisis. While crises expose systemic fragility, they also catalyze abundance corridors of cooperation, innovation, and compassion. The triadic framework situates crisis response within circuits of frequency, fluids, and forces, enabling reproducibility and validator dashboards. Applications are outlined in disaster response, healthcare, and symbolic cosmology. The framework complements social science by offering a resonance‑centric scaffold for human resilience.
