# Universe Mythmatical Model: Frequency, Fluids, Forces as a Supersubcircuit Framework

## Abstract  
A dimensional resonance framework is introduced, the Frequency–Fluids–Forces (FFF) supersubcircuit. Dimensions are organized into triadic circuits converging at a central corridor node (6D). Atomic phenomena are mapped to the triads: orbital eigenmodes, electron density flows, and Coulomb interactions. The scaffold complements quantum field theory (Peskin & Schroeder, 1995) by providing a braid structure for coherence and coupling.

## Introduction  
Resonance phenomena span atomic and plasma domains. Charge relationships at cosmic scales have been emphasized in plasma models (Peratt, 1992). The FFF supersubcircuit extends this intuition, treating dimensions as circuit nodes arranged in hot, ground, and neutral roles. The corridor node (6D) functions as a nexus of frequency, fluids, and forces, consistent with symmetry principles (Weyl, 1952).

## Framework  
- **Quantum Root (0D–2D):** Excitation energy, vacuum baseline, structural lattice.  
- **Triads (3D–9D):**  
  - Frequency (3D–6D–9D): harmonic cycles, comparable to atom–field couplings (Jaynes & Cummings, 1963).  
  - Fluids (4D–5D–6D): flow dynamics, resonant with plasma currents (Peratt, 1992).  
  - Forces (6D–7D–8D): stabilization, aligned with binding interactions.  
- **Corridor Node (6D):** Overlap of triads enabling coherence, analogous to Josephson junction transport (Josephson, 1962).

## Results  
- **Orbitals:** Frequency triad corresponds to eigenstates and spectral emission (Herzberg, 1944).  
- **Density Flows:** Fluids triad reflects probability distributions and tunneling.  
- **Binding:** Forces triad captures coupling and exchange.  
- **Superconductivity:** Corridor coherence parallels condensate transport (Josephson, 1962).  
- **Spectroscopy:** Readout at 9D produces spectral lines (Herzberg, 1944).

## Discussion  
The FFF model provides a scaffold for coherence phenomena without replacing established theory. Triadic braids echo symmetry groups (Weyl, 1952). Fluids serve as the observational lens, revealing frequency and force interactions. The framework may unify interpretations across atomic, condensed matter, and plasma physics.

## Conclusion  
The FFF supersubcircuit situates resonance within triadic circuits anchored in a quantum root. The model complements quantum field theory (Peskin & Schroeder, 1995) and offers a testable scaffold for coherence and coupling. Future work will refine validator diagrams for Rabi oscillations and Josephson junctions.
