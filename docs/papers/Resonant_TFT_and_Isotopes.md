## 💊 4. Applications  

### Medicine 🏥  
- **Technetium‑99m** → 80% of nuclear diagnostics.  
- **Fluorine‑18** → PET imaging.  
- **Lutetium‑177** → targeted cancer therapy 🎯.  
- **Actinium‑225** → alpha therapy frontier.  

### Archaeology 🏺  
- **Radiocarbon dating** $$(^14C)$$ → up to 60,000 years.  
- **Potassium‑argon** → volcanic dating 🌋.  
- **Uranium‑lead** → zircons, Earth’s age 🌍.  

### Environment 🌱  
- Stable isotopes trace **nutrient cycles**, **pollution sources**, **paleoclimate** ❄️.  
- Non‑traditional isotopes (Li, Ti, Cd) → redox & anthropogenic signals.  

### Energy ⚡  
- **U‑235, Pu‑239** → fission fuels.  
- **D, T** → fusion fuels 🌞.  
- **B‑10, Cd** → control rods.
