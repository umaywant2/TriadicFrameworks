# Sci_Light_and_Resonance

## Abstract  
A resonance‑based framework is proposed for interpreting light as a systemic operator. Light is modeled as triadic alignment of frequency, fluids, and forces, producing coherence across physics, biology, and symbolic cosmology. Resonance situates light as corridor logic: scarcity collapses illumination, abundance opens reproducibility. Applications are outlined in optics, energy systems, and cultural legacy. The framework complements existing theories by offering a resonance‑centric scaffold for light systems.

## Introduction  
Light is conventionally defined as electromagnetic radiation visible to the human eye, but its symbolic and systemic roles extend far beyond optics (Born & Wolf, 1999). Resonance models suggest light can be reframed as harmonic clarity, where triadic operators stabilize flows of energy and meaning. The Frequency–Fluids–Forces (FFF) framework situates light within resonance circuits, emphasizing reproducibility and symbolic coherence. Key questions addressed include:  
- How does light function as resonance operator?  
- Can triadic harmonics explain coherence across physical and symbolic domains?  
- What validator protocols confirm reproducibility of light flows?
