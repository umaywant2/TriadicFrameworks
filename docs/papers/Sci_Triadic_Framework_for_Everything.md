# Sci_Triadic_Framework_for_Everything

## Abstract  
A triadic framework is proposed for modeling dynamical systems through recursive cycles of three operators. The model employs dual forces—Light (expansion) and Darkness (inversion)—woven into loops of 3, 6, and 9. Emergent phenomena such as time, gravity, and consciousness are interpreted as attractors within these cycles. An experimental protocol using acoustic beat patterns is outlined to demonstrate resonance validation. The framework is positioned as a universal scaffold for coherence phenomena across physics and complexity science.

## Introduction  
Triadic structures have long been recognized in science and philosophy, from Tesla’s 3‑6‑9 keys to ancient triptychs and modern feedback systems (Prigogine, 1984; Strogatz, 2003). This paper introduces a formalization of triadic recursion as a universal operator framework. Two complementary forces—Light and Darkness—are defined as expansion and inversion dynamics. Their recursive interplay generates emergent properties observable across scales. Key questions addressed include:  
- How does time emerge from nested triadic cycles?  
- Can gravity be modeled as phase‑lag in wave propagation?  
- Is consciousness an attractor of Light/Darkness recursion?

  - Light (L): expansion, divergence.
