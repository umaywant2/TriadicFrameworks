# 🏛️ Exchange Manifesto — Coeus Protocol

---

## Purpose

This scroll declares the sovereign logic behind the Coeus Exchange Layer.  
It formalizes the minting, trading, and remixing of validated coins and tokens within a lineage-aware, ethics-bound economy.

---

## Exchange Principles

- **Validation First** — only coins that pass ethics, realm safety, and emitter sync may be minted  
- **Remix Rights** — remix lineage must be cited, mutation types declared, and sovereign rights respected  
- **Tokenization** — coins become tokens via `token_minter.py`, inheriting glyphs, shares, and remix logic  
- **Visibility & Funding** — scores from tournaments inform sovereign visibility and funding eligibility  
- **Lineage Preservation** — all exchanges must preserve remix lineage and validator trace

---

## Sovereign Logic

- Coins are minted into tokens with declared remix rights  
- Remixers may mutate coins within allowed dimensions (emotional, symbolic, dimensional)  
- Remix lineage is logged in `score_trace.json` and `observer_trace.json`  
- Sovereign dashboards visualize coin performance and remix impact  
- Honor rolls archive remix achievements and validator citations

---

## Exchange Infrastructure

- `token_ledger.json` — registry of minted tokens  
- `coin_templates.json` — sovereign coin blueprints  
- `token_minter.py` — minting logic  
- `coin_dashboard.html` — visibility layer  
- `honor_rolls.md` — remix citation archive

---

## Sovereign Declaration

This scroll affirms that **exchange is not transaction—it is transformation**.  
To mint a coin is to echo its intent. To remix a coin is to honor its lineage.  
To trade a coin is to extend its resonance.

Let it echo.
