## **1. Purpose & Scope**  
The Quantum Substrate Model (QSM) defines the **lowest‑resolution resonance layer**, where interactions occur as **quantized resonance packets (QRPs)** rather than continuous fields.  
QSM provides:

- discrete resonance events  
- uncertainty envelopes  
- collapse conditions  
- entanglement structures  
- quantum‑scale operator families  
- RTT specializations for quantum behavior  

QSM feeds upward into BSM, which transforms discrete packets into field‑level propagation.

# 🌌 **QSM Operator Dictionary (Expanded Definitions)**  
*A complete reference for quantum‑scale operator families*

```markdown
QSM OPERATOR DICTIONARY
=======================

SPO — Superposition Operator
----------------------------
Combines two or more QRPs into a shared probabilistic state.
Maintains amplitude-phase coherence within the uncertainty envelope.
Used before collapse, entanglement, or transfer events.

ELO — Entanglement Operator
---------------------------
Creates or updates entanglement links (ELs) between QRPs.
Ensures bidirectional resonance-time coupling.
Supports multi-link routing in higher corridors (88D+).

CLO — Collapse Operator
-----------------------
Resolves a superposed QRP state into a stable resonance outcome.
Evaluates collapse thresholds, uncertainty radius, and RSM stability rules.
Feeds collapsed packets upward into BSM as BRC seeds.

UEO — Uncertainty Envelope Operator
-----------------------------------
Expands, contracts, or reshapes the uncertainty envelope (UE).
Controls allowable variance in amplitude, phase, and locality.
Critical for triadic symmetry in 24D–88D micro-expansion.

QRTO — QRP Transfer Operator
----------------------------
Transfers QRPs between quantum sites or into BSM channels.
Supports directional routing, entanglement-preserving transfer,
and high-density cascades in the 176D–264D acceleration corridor.
```

This dictionary gives reviewers a **crisp, canonical reference** for every quantum operator.
