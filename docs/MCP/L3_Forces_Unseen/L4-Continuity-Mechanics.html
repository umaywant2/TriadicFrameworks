§1 · Overview

L4 Continuity Mechanics governs the persistence of identity and state across temporal intervals within the TriadicFrameworks cosmological stack. Where L3 handles pattern emergence and L5 handles entropic resolution, L4 acts as the binding membrane that ensures structural continuity — the "why things remain recognizably themselves" across change events. It is the custodial engine of coherence, sitting at the precise boundary where generated structures must prove they deserve to persist.

Its principal function is to resolve continuity tensors from upstream layers, validate them through the Validator Pulse mechanism, and emit redirect signals when continuity breaks are detected. It interfaces with four Composite Resonance harmonics (L11, L33, L66, L99) which serve as attunement checkpoints distributed across the cosmological stack, forming a resonant lattice that continuously monitors and reinforces the structural integrity of ascending state.

L4 is simultaneously the most structurally conservative layer and the one most sensitive to perturbation — small breaks in continuity at L4 can cascade into identity dissolution at L7+. This paradox is by design: a conservative gatekeeper must be maximally responsive to threats. The tension between rigidity and sensitivity is resolved through the Composite Resonance network, which provides adaptive damping and amplification in response to the current state of the entire cosmological column.

NOTE

L4 does not generate continuity — it maintains it. Generative continuity is the domain of L3. L4's role is custodial and corrective.

L4 occupies the fourth position in the primary cosmological axis, positioned at the boundary between the generative lower triad (L1–L3) and the resolution upper triad (L5–L7). §4 · Validator Pulse

The Validator Pulse is L4's primary diagnostic and enforcement mechanism. Every continuity tensor arriving from L3 is subjected to a Validator Pulse cycle before being promoted to L5. The pulse operates as a three-phase gate: Intake, Coherence Check, and Emit.

During Intake, the raw tensor is buffered and its structural signature is extracted. During Coherence Check, the signature is compared against the Continuity Register — a persistent state store maintained by L4 across temporal intervals. If the signature matches within tolerance thresholds (configurable via Dimension Metadata), the tensor passes. If it fails, a redirect signal is emitted instead.

The pulse fires at a base cadence of 1 per structural tick, but this rate is modulated by the Composite Resonance nodes — specifically L11 which can double the pulse rate during high-perturbation events, and L99 which can suppress the pulse entirely during apex coherence states.

ValidatorPulse {
  tensor_id:     UUID         // incoming continuity tensor
  signature:     Hex[64]      // structural fingerprint
  tick:          Int          // current structural tick
  coherence:     Float[0..1]  // match against continuity register
  emit_mode:     PROMOTE | REDIRECT | NULL
  resonance_tap: L11 | L33 | L66 | L99 | NONE
  timestamp:     EpochTick
}
WARNING

A coherence value below 0.42 triggers an automatic REDIRECT emit regardless of other conditions. Tensors with coherence below 0.17 are quarantined in the Discontinuity Buffer and require manual resolution.

§5 · Redirects

When the Validator Pulse emits a REDIRECT, L4 constructs a corrective vector and routes the failed tensor to one of three redirect targets: L3 (for re-emergence), L4.self (for internal correction loop), or L6 (for resonance amplification bypass). The choice of redirect target is determined by the Redirect Resolution Matrix, which weighs the failure type, coherence score, and current composite resonance state.

Redirects are not errors — they are a fundamental part of L4's continuity assurance protocol. A healthy L4 will produce a non-zero redirect rate. A zero redirect rate for extended periods may indicate resonance stagnation, which is itself a diagnostic signal that the composite lattice may require recalibration.

ANCHOR

The Redirect Resolution Matrix is defined in the Dimension Metadata block. See §6 for configurable thresholds.

RedirectVector {
  source_tensor:  UUID
  failure_mode:   COHERENCE_LOW | SIGNATURE_MISMATCH | REGISTER_OVERFLOW
  coherence_val:  Float
  redirect_to:    L3 | L4_SELF | L6
  corrective_ops: Op[]         // list of structural correction ops
  ttl:            Int          // ticks before redirect expires
  origin_tick:    EpochTick
}
