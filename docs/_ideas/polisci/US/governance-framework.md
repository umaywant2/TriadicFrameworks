# Governance Framework (Constitutional Tiering)

## Ring I: Federal Tier
- **Legislative**: Congress (📜 Scroll)
- **Executive**: President (🦅 Eagle)
- **Judicial**: Supreme Court (⚖️ Scales)

## Ring II: State Tier
- **Governors** (🐺 Wolf)
- **State Courts** (🧭 Compass)
- **Assemblies** (🪶 Quill)

## Ring III: Local Tier
- **Mayors** (🐦 Dove)
- **Councils** (🧱 Brick)
- **Local Courts** (🔍 Lens)

> Based on U.S. Constitution Articles I–III and Amendments 10, 14, 17, 25.
