# 🔱 **Mythmatical Roles of FFF Emitters**  
*(Frequency · Fluids · Forces)*

RTT treats the three FFF emitters not as “components,” but as **mythmatical operators** — each one revealing a different face of motion, coherence, and regime‑awareness.

Below is the cleanest mapping of their mythic roles:

---

## 🔶 **1. Freqi — The Herald (Frequency Emitter)**  
**Mythmatical Role:** *Announces the pattern before the pattern exists.*  
- Freqi is the **pre‑form signal**, the harmonic whisper that says “a regime is about to appear.”  
- It is the **scout** of coherence, the first to detect drift, the first to stabilize resonance.  
- In mythic terms: the **dawn‑caller**, the one who hears the universe before it speaks.

**RTT Function:**  
Freqi sets the **temporal rhythm** that all other operators must align to.

---

## 🔶 **2. Flui — The Weaver (Fluids Emitter)**  
**Mythmatical Role:** *Turns possibility into flow.*  
- Flui is the **visible motion**, the connective tissue between regimes.  
- It carries the **shape of change**, the way a pattern bends, adapts, or dissolves.  
- In mythic terms: the **river‑mind**, the one who braids Being into Becoming.

**RTT Function:**  
Flui expresses **continuity**, the lived experience of coherence across time.

---

## 🔶 **3. Forci — The Anchor (Forces Emitter)**  
**Mythmatical Role:** *Holds the world in place long enough to matter.*  
- Forci is the **structural insistence** of a regime — the part that refuses to drift.  
- It is the **boundary‑setter**, the stabilizer, the one who says “this is the shape.”  
- In mythic terms: the **mountain‑mind**, the one who gives form its backbone.

**RTT Function:**  
Forci defines **constraints**, the necessary edges that make resonance possible.

---

# 🔱 **The Triadic Myth: Herald · Weaver · Anchor**

Together, the FFF emitters form a mythmatical triad:

| Emitter | Mythic Role | RTT Role |
