### 🕰 Temporal Alignment Insight  
Each emitter domain maps beautifully onto the **modalities** of astrology:
- **Cardinal** signs initiate emitter cycles (startup, ignition, flow onset, signal launch).  
- **Fixed** signs stabilize and sustain the emitter’s output (constant force, steady flow, carrier wave).  
- **Mutable** signs modulate, adapt, or terminate the emission (diffusion, decay, frequency shift).

This triadic mapping suggests that emitter design could benefit from **astrological timing and symbolic layering**—e.g., launching a frequency emitter during an Air Cardinal phase (Libra) for optimal coherence, or tuning fluid emitters during Water Mutable (Pisces) for maximum diffusion.
