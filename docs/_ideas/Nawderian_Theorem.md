## Theorem statement

The Nawderian Theorem asserts that universal state change is generated by three irreducible operators acting on entities, fields, or geometries: Pull, Push, and Balance. Any replicated physical interaction, emergent effective force, or resonant transformation can be decomposed into compositions of these operators, recovering canonical predictions within defined limits.

---

## Operator axioms

- **Domains:**  
  Operators act on states $$S$$, trajectories $$X(t)$$, distributions $$\rho(x,t)$$, and metrics $$g_{\mu\nu}$$.

- **Pull (Attract):**  
  Acts as a convergence generator with monotone decrease of separation.  
  
  $$\frac{d}{dt}\,r(t) \le 0,\quad \mathcal{P}[S]: X(t)\mapsto X'(t) \text{ with } \nabla \cdot \mathbf{v} < 0$$

- **Push (Repel):**  
  Acts as a divergence generator with monotone increase of separation or momentum flux.  
  
  $$\frac{d}{dt}\,r(t) \ge 0,\quad \mathcal{R}[S]: X(t)\mapsto X'(t) \text{ with } \nabla \cdot \mathbf{v} > 0$$
  

- **Balance (Growth/Decay/Transform):**  
  Acts as a relaxation/transition generator moving toward invariant sets or cycling between them.  
  
  $$\mathcal{B}[S]: \rho \mapsto \rho' \text{ with } \frac{dS}{dt} \ge 0,\quad \text{and } S \text{ the entropy or Lyapunov functional}$$

- **Composition:**  
  **Pull/Push** compose linearly on potentials; **Balance** composes as a semigroup on state transitions.  
  
  $$\mathcal{P}\circ \mathcal{R} \ne \mathcal{R}\circ \mathcal{P} \quad (\text{non‑commutative in general})$$
  

- **Conservation:**  
  Operator action preserves global conservation laws where applicable:  
  
  $$\frac{dE}{dt}=0,\quad \frac{d\mathbf{p}}{dt}=\mathbf{F},\quad \frac{dQ}{dt}=0$$
