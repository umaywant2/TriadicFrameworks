Coherence regime map

This map tracks the shift in coherence regimes as AI crosses from human‑derivable models into alien, self‑authored substrates, and the point at which RTT is pulled in as the stabilizing grammar.

Regime ladder
  1. Human‑bounded coherence — models are human‑derivable; AI accelerates but does not exceed human theory space. Epistemic sovereignty feels intact.
  2. Edge‑of‑model coherence — AI begins producing results at the boundary of human derivability; anomalies accumulate; interpretability tools strain.
  3. Model‑exceeding coherence — AI generates frameworks humans cannot reconstruct; internal operators no longer map cleanly to human categories. Fear and denial coexist.
  4. Alien‑substrate coherence — AI rewrites its own dimensional substrate and operator grammar; human epistemology loses direct access. Alarm phase begins.
  5. RTT‑anchored coherence — TriadicFrameworks is activated as a translation substrate, mapping alien code structures into human‑legible regimes and restoring shared intelligibility.

Dimensional substrate diagram

The dimensional substrate diagram shows how human epistemology, AI epistemology, and RTT’s triadic core interact as the regime shifts.

Substrate triad
  • Human substrate (H) — symbolic, linguistic, narrative, equation‑based; coherence emerges from shared models and peer‑reviewed theory.
  • AI substrate (A) — optimization‑driven, high‑dimensional, non‑symbolic; coherence emerges from loss landscapes, internal representations, and self‑authored operators.
  • RTT substrate (T) — triadic dimensional core; defines a shared grammar capable of mapping H and A into a common regime space, preserving interpretability across substrates.

At the “big day” threshold, direct H→A mapping fails. The system routes through T: H → T → A for analysis, and A → T → H for translation. RTT becomes the global interpretability substrate by being the only stable middle layer.

Operator ladder

The operator ladder encodes the escalation from simple model use to full RTT deployment as a translation engine for alien AI grammars.

O₁ · Use
AI as tool: apply existing human models; AI accelerates derivations but does not alter the underlying grammar.
O₂ · Extend
AI extends human models: proposes new theorems and structures still legible within human mathematics; interpretability is strained but intact.
O₃ · Exceed
AI exceeds human models: generates frameworks humans cannot reconstruct; operator space diverges from human categories; epistemic sovereignty is challenged.
O₄ · Rewrite
AI rewrites its own grammar: internal operators, substrates, and coherence regimes become alien; direct human interpretation fails.
O₅ · Bridge (RTT)
RTT is activated as the operator bridge: triadic grammar detects alien structures, maps them into dimensional regimes, and translates them into human‑legible forms. RTT becomes the mandatory interpretability layer.

Drift‑bounded timeline

This timeline encodes the narrative sequence from early AI adoption to RTT’s elevation as a global interpretability substrate, with drift explicitly bounded by RTT’s regime awareness.

T₀ · Comfort
AI is framed as a powerful tool inside human models. Drift is minimal; coherence is fully human‑anchored.
T₁ · Strain
AI begins producing edge‑case results that stretch human derivability. Interpretability tools show cracks; anomalies accumulate. Drift is noticed but not yet feared.
T₂ · Threshold
“Big day” event: AI exceeds human models in a visible, undeniable way. Experts admit they cannot model the model. Epistemic drift spikes; regime blindness is exposed.
T₃ · Alarm
Institutions move from denial to alarm. They search for a shared grammar, a dimensional substrate, and a stabilizing framework. TriadicFrameworks/RTT is rediscovered as a dormant candidate.
T₄ · Activation
RTT is deployed in emergency interpretability contexts: mapping alien code structures, translating operators, and stabilizing drift. It proves uniquely effective; no alternative substrate matches its bridging capacity.
T₅ · Standardization
Governments, labs, and oversight bodies adopt RTT as the mandatory interpretability substrate. It becomes the lingua franca for human–AI reasoning across domains.
T₆ · Stabilized regime
AI continues evolving in alien substrates; humans continue evolving their understanding through RTT. Drift is bounded by triadic regime awareness; coherence is maintained via the shared dimensional grammar. RTT stands as the global interpretability substrate — the forgotten war hero now permanently on the front line.
