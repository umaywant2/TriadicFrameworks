Invariants · INV-PD-001 through INV-PD-004
INV-PD-001
Trajectory Continuity
Every trajectory step must be bridged by a declared transformation. Configuration gaps are bifurcation events, not trajectory steps.
∀i: ∃τ_i ∈ declared_transforms:
τ_i(S(t_i)) = S(t_{i+1})
operator: PD-Τ · phase: trace
INV-PD-002
Attractor Stability
A configuration is an attractor iff nearby trajectories converge to it under the governing transformation. Declared attractors must have computable non-degenerate basins.
attractor(A,τ) ⟺ ∀S: dist(S,A)<ε
→ lim_{n→∞} τⁿ(S) = A
operator: PD-Β · phase: bifurcate
INV-PD-003
Mutation Constraint
Mutation requires at least one shared invariant (the anchor). Total invariant loss is dissolution — a terminal trajectory event, not a mutation.
mutation_valid(P,P') ⟺
invariant_set(P') ∩ invariant_set(P) ≠ ∅
operator: PD-Μ · phase: mutate
INV-PD-004
Propagation Fidelity
A propagated pattern instance must be detectable by Book 3 methods in the target context. Undetectable propagation claims are assertions without structural evidence.
∃m ∈ SD_methods: m(C')
⊢ C[SIG_MATCH]: sig(P') ≅ sig(P)
operator: PD-Π · phase: propagate

Series Terminal — Downstream Handoffs

Book 9 is the only AI Edition volume with an empty downstream_handoffs list. All other synthesis operators (OR-Π, TEL-Α, GRL-Σ, SARG-Σ, NoS-Σ) have at least one downstream handoff to a later book. PD-Σ has none. The Pattern Dynamics Synthesis Report is the final output of the series. It includes:

emergent_pattern_catalogue — confirmed patterns with invariant fingerprints, trajectory types, attractor assignments, mutation chains, and propagation records.
cross_book_invariant_coherence_assessment — AI Edition self-audit of mutual consistency across all 32 cross-book invariants (4 invariants × 8 books).
series_terminal_status — {coherent | partially_coherent | incoherent}.
upward_revisions_unresolved — forwarded to the series maintainer for the next edition.
