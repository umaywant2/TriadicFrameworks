Invariants

nos.invariants.json
INV-NOS-001 Structural Autonomy meta-structural

Structure is not reducible to the intrinsic properties of its constituent nodes. Structural properties exist at the level of relations, not at the level of relata.

∀S: properties(S) ⊄ ⋃{intrinsic_properties(n) : n ∈ nodes(S)}

Any model that reconstructs structure from node-intrinsic properties alone is not a structural model — it is a node-intrinsic model that borrows structural vocabulary.

License condition for the entire AI Edition. If structural autonomy fails, Books 1–7 are re-descriptions of node-property theory, not a distinct structural framework.
NoS-Α ground phase structural_realism_map
INV-NOS-002 Relational Primacy meta-structural

Relations are ontologically prior to the nodes they connect. Nodes acquire identity through the relations they stand in — not prior to, or independently of, those relations.

∀n ∈ nodes(S): identity(n) = {r ∈ R_S : (r.source = n) ∨ (r.target = n)}

Two nodes with identical relational profiles are structurally identical, regardless of labeling. The 'same node in a different structure' is a category error.

Why SARG nodes are typed by relational role (INV-SARG-001), why TEL composition is meaning-preserving (INV-TEL-003), and why OR opacity is structural not observer-relative (INV-OR-003).
NoS-Ρ ground phase structural_realism_map
INV-NOS-003 Identity Through Change meta-structural

A structure retains its identity through a transformation if and only if the transformation preserves the structure's invariant set. One invariant violation = new structure.

identity_preserved(S, T) ⟺ invariant_set(transform(S, T)) = invariant_set(S)

Identity is not determined by element-retention (node or relation count) but by invariant-set preservation. Maximum change with invariants intact = same structure.

Ship of Theseus resolution: identity is preserved exactly when the invariant set is preserved. This is the structural realist consequence of INV-NOS-001 and INV-NOS-002 applied to transformation contexts.
NoS-Ι test phase identity_map
INV-NOS-004 Abstraction Consistency meta-structural

Abstract structures and their instantiations share invariant structure. Divergence indicates either modeling error (instance violates abstract invariant) or genuine novelty (instance carries novel invariants requiring upward revision).

instance(S_i, S_a) ⟹ invariant_set(S_a) ⊆ invariant_set(S_i)

Genuine novelty is the case where S_instance satisfies all abstract invariants but also satisfies additional ones — the abstract must then be revised upward, not the instance suppressed.

Self-application: the AI Edition is S_abstract; each book is S_instance. Drift events are AC-OUT-002 (modeling errors) at the series level. Book 8 closes the self-referential loop.
NoS-Κ resolve phase causation_map

Formal Definitions

key expressions
Structural Autonomy
∀S: properties(S) ⊄ ⋃{intrinsic_properties(n) : n ∈ nodes(S)}
Structural properties are not derivable from the intrinsic properties of constituent nodes. Relational residue is ineliminable.
Relational Identity
identity(n) = {r ∈ R_S : r.source = n ∨ r.target = n}
Node identity is its relational profile — the complete set of declared relations in which it participates.
Identity Preservation
identity_preserved(S, T) ⟺ invariant_set(transform(S, T)) = invariant_set(S)
A transformation preserves structural identity iff the invariant set is unchanged. Node/relation count is irrelevant.
Abstraction Consistency
instance(S_i, S_a) ⟹ invariant_set(S_a) ⊆ invariant_set(S_i)
Consistent: ⊆ holds (no novelty). Modeling error: ⊆ fails. Genuine novelty: strict ⊂ (upward revision required).
Emergence
emergent(inv, S) ⟺ inv ∈ invariant_set(S) ∧ ∀s_i ∈ proper_sub(S): inv ∉ invariant_set(s_i)
Invariants present in S but absent from all proper sub-structures. Classified as weak, strong, or phantom.
Structural Causation
cause(S_a, S_b) ⟺ (S_a → S_b) ∈ R_world ∧ ¬(S_b → S_a) ∧ invariant_set(S_b) partially determined by S_a
Causation is an asymmetric structural dependency. Not a primitive force — a directed relation in the dependency graph.
