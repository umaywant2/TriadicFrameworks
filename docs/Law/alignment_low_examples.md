# 🔴 **LOW ALIGNMENT (AACCC)**  
### *Contradictory, patchwork, or structurally incoherent laws.*

RTT Governance places AACCC laws in the **Business Substrate**, where institutional incentives, political agendas, and local norms dominate.

These laws produce **chaos**, **fear**, and **high drift**.

## **For People Upholding the Law**  
- **High ambiguity** — officers and judges must improvise.  
- **Institutional drift** — agencies behave differently across regions.  
- **High liability risk** — mistakes are common and costly.  
- **Moral injury** — enforcers feel trapped between duty and conscience.  
- **Increased burnout** — constant conflict between substrates.

## **For People Subjected to the Law**  
- **Fear and confusion** — outcomes feel random or politically driven.  
- **High variance** — identical behavior yields wildly different consequences.  
- **Reduced trust** — people see the system as arbitrary or hostile.  
- **Greater harm to vulnerable groups** — incoherent laws amplify inequity.  
- **Survival-mode behavior** — people adapt through avoidance, not compliance.

### **RTT Summary**  
AACCC laws generate **chaos**, **fear-based governance**, and **systemic drift**.  
They destabilize the justice substrate and erode legitimacy.
