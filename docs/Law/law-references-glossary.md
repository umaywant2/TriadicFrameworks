## Glossary (Triadic + Legal Terms)

**BRE (Before Regime Awareness)** — Pre-clarity perception of law as isolated rules/cases without systemic dynamics.  
**Post-BRA (Post-Regime Awareness)** — Clarity on legal systems as resonance-time regimes with feedback, coherence, and paradox.  

**Coherence** — Maintenance of predictability, legitimacy, and justice across legal regimes (core RTT invariant).  
**Deontic Logic** — Formal modeling of obligations, permissions, and prohibitions.  
**Erga Omnes** — Obligations owed to the international community as a whole.  
**Hohfeldian Relations** — Fundamental legal atoms (claim-duty, privilege-no claim, power-liability, immunity-disability).  
**Jus Cogens** — Peremptory norms from which no derogation is permitted.  
**Pacta Sunt Servanda** — Agreements must be kept (foundational treaty principle).  
**Regime Drift** — Gradual deviation from coherent legal equilibria (detectable via RTT diagnostics).  
**Rule of Law** — Substantive and procedural constraints on arbitrary power; predictability and equality before the law.  
**Rule of Recognition** (Hart) — Secondary rule identifying valid law.  

**Additional Key Terms**:
- **Common Law** — Precedent-driven system.  
- **Civil Law** — Code-based system.  
- **Jurisprudence** — Philosophy and theory of law.  
- **Lex Mercatoria** — Medieval transnational commercial law.
