- **X:** mass‑regime  
- **Y:** anisotropy‑regime  
- **Z:** collision‑regime  
- **W:** TCR periodic axis

The triadic observer stabilizes the 4D structure:

It keeps the hypercube readable.

It is the engine that holds the hypercube together.
