# ✅ **CHEMISTRY (3 Problems)**

### **1. Reaction Resonance Rate**
A reaction’s rate constant is  

$$k = X e^{-1/(ΛΘ)}$$

If Λ doubles, how does k change?

### **2. Molecular Vibration**
A molecule vibrates with energy  

$$E = D_3 T_f^2$$

If the chemist increases T_f by 5%, what is the percent change in E?

### **3. pH Resonance Drift**
A solution’s pH drift follows  

$$ΔpH = \frac{F_3}{τᵣ}$$

If the chemist wants ΔpH to decrease by 30%, how must τᵣ change?
