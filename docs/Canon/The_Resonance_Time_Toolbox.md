
# ⭐ **THE RESONANCE‑TIME TOOLBOX**  
### *The seven universal operators, assembled into a functional, minimal, triadic kit.*

Each operator is presented in a **triadic spec**:

- **What it does** (function)  
- **How it behaves** (mechanism)  
- **Where it applies** (scope)  

And each one is **scale‑invariant** across 3D–9D.

---

## **1. Relation‑Op**  
### **Function:** Generates structure  
### **Mechanism:** Establishes relational tension (self ↔ other ↔ between)  
### **Scope:** Every dimension, every scale  

Relation‑Op is the *ignition spark* of any system.  
Nothing exists until relation exists.

---

## **2. Boundary‑Op**  
### **Function:** Shapes structure  
### **Mechanism:** Defines regime shifts (inside ↔ outside ↔ threshold)  
### **Scope:** All coherence domains  

Boundary‑Op turns relation into *form* by creating the first distinction.

---

## **3. Rhythm‑Op**  
### **Function:** Animates structure  
### **Mechanism:** Establishes recurrence (cycle ↔ beat ↔ return)  
### **Scope:** All temporal and resonance systems  

Rhythm‑Op turns form into *behavior*.

---

## **4. Transition‑Op**  
### **Function:** Evolves structure  
### **Mechanism:** Reconfigures states (phase ↔ shift ↔ new regime)  
### **Scope:** All systems undergoing change  

Transition‑Op turns behavior into *becoming*.

---

## **5. Lineage‑Op**  
### **Function:** Persists structure  
### **Mechanism:** Carries coherence (inherit ↔ echo ↔ propagate)  
### **Scope:** All systems across time and scale  

Lineage‑Op turns becoming into *story*.

---

## **6. Envelope‑Op**  
### **Function:** Contains structure  
### **Mechanism:** Defines coherence domains (scope ↔ limit ↔ possibility)  
### **Scope:** All worlds, from atomic orbitals to supspheres  

Envelope‑Op turns story into *world*.

---

## **7. Coherence‑Op**  
### **Function:** Aligns structure  
### **Mechanism:** Maintains identity (align ↔ stabilize ↔ integrate)  
### **Scope:** All systems that remain intelligible as themselves  

Coherence‑Op turns world into *self*.

---
