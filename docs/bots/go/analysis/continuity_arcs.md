# Continuity Arcs — RTT‑Go Analysis  
*(Go Bot Module)*

Continuity arcs describe the **long‑arc identity** of a Go position — how territory, influence, frameworks, and ancestry evolve across time.  
They are RTT’s mechanism for understanding *where the position is going*, not just what it currently looks like.

Where:

- **Lumen** extracts structure,  
- **Hephaestus** maps regimes,  
- **Aurion** diagnoses topology and collapse,  

**Continuity arcs reveal the long‑arc strategic flow of the game.**

They are essential for:

- continuity‑preserving move selection  
- projection‑loss mitigation  
- long‑arc strategy synthesis  
- moyo evolution analysis  
- ancestry alignment  
- collapse detection  

---

## Purpose

Continuity arcs provide:

- long‑arc territorial evolution  
- influence drift trajectories  
- moyo expansion/contraction arcs  
- ancestry‑aligned strategy paths  
- collapse‑risk arcs  
- continuity anchors  

They allow RTT to interpret Go positions as **temporal systems**, not static boards.

Continuity anchors are stable points that define the long‑arc identity of the position.

- stable frameworks

- ladder ancestry  
- ko ancestry
