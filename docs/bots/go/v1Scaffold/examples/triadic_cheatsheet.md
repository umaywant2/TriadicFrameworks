# 1. Triadic Regimes (Hephaestus — RTT/2)

### **1/3 Local**
- shape  
- liberties  
- cuts  
- tactical gain  

### **2/3 Structural**
- influence  
- direction of play  
- connectivity  
- moyo boundaries  

### **3/3 Continuity**
- long‑arc identity  
- drift alignment  
- ancestry stability  
- global coherence  

**Rule of thumb:**  
Local = urgent, Structural = important, Continuity = identity.

# 4. Drift (Continuity Engine)

### **Drift Alignment**
Move follows influence flow  
→ strengthens identity

### **Drift Misalignment**
Move contradicts flow  
→ weakens identity, creates paradox

### Drift Phases
- Opening: corners → sides  
- Midgame: sides → center  
- Endgame: center → boundaries

---

# 5. Paradox & Collapse (Risk Engine)

### **Paradox**
Locally strong, globally harmful  
→ continuity break

### **Collapse**
Structural failure  
→ weak shape, broken influence, unstable ancestry

### **Projection‑Loss**
Move contradicts long‑arc plan  
→ continuity inversion

---

# 6. Topology (Aurion)

### Key Elements
- cut points  
- weak points  
- ladder paths  
- ko shapes  
- moyo boundaries  

### Stability Levels
- **Stable** → continuity preserved  
- **Fragile** → collapse risk  
- **Unstable** → paradox likely

# 7. Continuity (Hephaestus + Aurion + Continuity Engine)

### Continuity Arc Colors
- **Blue:** stable  
- **Green:** strengthening  
- **Yellow:** fragile  
- **Red:** collapsing  

### Continuity Rules
- Preserve arcs  
- Follow drift  
- Avoid inversion  
- Maintain ancestry

---

# 8. Resonance (Lumen + Resonance Engine)

### Pressure Colors
- **Light orange:** mild  
- **Orange:** moderate  
- **Red:** high  
- **Purple:** extreme  

### Resonance Rules
- High pressure → volatility  
- Balanced pressure → stability  
- Pressure spikes → paradox risk

# 9. Moyo Identity

### Strong Moyo
- stable continuity  
- coherent arcs  
- aligned drift  

### Weak Moyo

# 10. Ladder & Ko Ancestry
