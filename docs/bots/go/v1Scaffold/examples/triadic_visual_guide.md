# Triadic Visual Guide — RTT‑Go  
*(for `/docs/bots/go/examples/triadic_visual_guide.md`)*

This guide explains **every visual element** used in RTT‑Go’s UI overlays.  
It is designed for teaching mode, analysis mode, and triadic commentary, helping players understand how RTT represents:

- continuity  
- resonance  
- topology  
- ancestry  
- drift  
- paradox/collapse  
- triadic scoring  

Each visual element corresponds to one or more RTT engines.

---

# 1. Continuity Arcs  
### **What they show**
Curved lines indicating the flow of long‑arc identity across the board.

### **Color Meaning**
- **Blue:** stable continuity  
- **Green:** strengthening continuity  
- **Yellow:** fragile continuity  
- **Red:** continuity collapse risk  

### **Engine Source**
- Hephaestus (regime)  
- Aurion (ancestry)  
- Continuity Engine  

### **Interpretation**
Continuity arcs reveal whether a move supports or contradicts the global plan.

---

# 2. Resonance Fields  
### **What they show**
Soft gradients showing influence pressure and vibrational field strength.

### **Color Meaning**
- **Light orange:** mild pressure  
- **Orange:** moderate pressure  
- **Red:** high pressure  
- **Purple:** extreme pressure / volatility  

### **Engine Source**
- Lumen (structure)  
- Resonance Engine  

### **Interpretation**
Resonance fields show where fights intensify and where drift is heading.

---

# 3. Drift Arrows  
### **What they show**
Directional arrows showing the natural flow of influence and continuity.

### **Color Meaning**
- **White:** early drift  
- **Blue:** stable drift  
- **Green:** strengthening drift  
- **Red:** drift misalignment  

### **Engine Source**
- Resonance Engine  
- Continuity Engine  

### **Interpretation**
Moves that follow drift strengthen identity; moves that contradict drift weaken it.

---

# 4. Topology Highlights  
### **What they show**
Structural features of the board:

- cut points  
- weak points  
- ladder paths  
- ko shapes  
- moyo boundaries  

### **Symbols**
- **Red diamond:** cut point  
- **Orange triangle:** weak point  
- **Blue line:** ladder path  
- **Purple circle:** ko node  
- **Green boundary curve:** moyo edge  

### **Engine Source**
- Lumen (connectivity)  
- Aurion (topology & ancestry)  
- Topology Engine  

### **Interpretation**
Topology highlights reveal structural stability or collapse risk.

---

# 5. Ancestry Lines  
### **What they show**
The lineage of tactical sequences:

- ladders  
- ko fights  
- reductions  
- long fights  

### **Color Meaning**
- **Blue:** stable ancestry  
- **Yellow:** fragile ancestry  
- **Red:** ancestry collapse risk  

### **Engine Source**
- Aurion (ancestry)  
- Continuity Engine  

### **Interpretation**
Stable ancestry supports continuity; unstable ancestry threatens collapse.

---

# 6. Paradox Indicators  
### **What they show**
Warnings that a move is locally strong but globally harmful.

### **Symbol**
- **Orange ⚠️ triangle** near the move

### **Engine Source**
- Risk Engine  
- Aurion (paradox precursor detection)

### **Interpretation**
Paradox indicators warn that a move contradicts the long‑arc identity.

---

# 7. Collapse Signatures  
### **What they show**
Patterns indicating imminent structural failure.

### **Symbol**
- **Red X** over a group or region

### **Engine Source**
- Risk Engine  
- Aurion (collapse detection)

### **Interpretation**
Collapse signatures often appear before a group dies or continuity breaks.

---

# 8. Projection‑Loss Arcs  
### **What they show**
Arcs showing where continuity is about to collapse due to misaligned play.

### **Color Meaning**
- **Yellow:** mild projection‑loss  
- **Orange:** moderate  
- **Red:** severe  

### **Engine Source**
- Risk Engine  
- Continuity Engine  

### **Interpretation**
Projection‑loss arcs warn that a move contradicts the long‑arc plan.

---

# 9. Regime Tags  
### **What they show**
The triadic regime classification of each move.

### **Tag Types**
- **1/3 Local** — shape, liberties, cuts  
- **2/3 Structural** — influence, direction of play  
- **3/3 Continuity** — long‑arc identity  

### **Engine Source**
- Hephaestus (regime mapping)

### **Interpretation**
Regime tags show the triadic role of each move.

---

# 10. Triadic Score HUD  
### **What it shows**
Unified triadic evaluation of the move or position.

### **HUD Fields**
- Local score  
- Structural score  
- Continuity score  
- Resonance stability  
- Topology stability  
- Ancestry stability  
- Drift alignment  
- Risk level  
- Final triadic score  

### **Engine Source**
- Harmonia (RTT/12)  
- Scoring Engine  

### **Interpretation**
The HUD is the final triadic verdict on the position.

---

# 11. Combined Triadic View  
### **What it shows**
All overlays active at once:

- continuity arcs  
- resonance fields  
- drift arrows  
- topology highlights  
- ancestry lines  
- paradox/collapse indicators  
- triadic HUD  

### **Interpretation**
The combined view reveals the full triadic identity of the position.

---

The Triadic Visual Guide explains:
