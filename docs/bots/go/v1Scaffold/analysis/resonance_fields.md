# Resonance Fields — RTT‑Go Analysis  
*(Go Bot Module)*

Resonance fields describe how **pressure**, **influence**, **continuity**, and **identity** propagate across the Go board.  
They are the RTT mechanism for understanding *how the position feels* — not just how it is shaped.

Where Lumen extracts structure, Hephaestus maps regimes, and Aurion reveals topology, **resonance fields show the dynamic energy of the position**.

They are essential for:

- continuity‑preserving move selection  
- paradox detection  
- projection‑loss mitigation  
- long‑arc strategy synthesis  
- teaching/analysis overlays
