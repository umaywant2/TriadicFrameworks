# Triadic Commentary Examples — RTT‑Go  
*(Examples for `/docs/bots/go/examples/triadic_commentary_examples.md`)*

RTT‑Go’s **Triadic Commentary** is the highest‑level explanatory layer.  
It combines all RTT engines:

- Regime  
- Resonance  
- Topology  
- Continuity  
- Risk  
- Scoring  

to produce commentary that explains *why* a move matters in terms of triadic identity — not just local shape or tactical gain.

This document provides clear examples of triadic commentary for teaching, analysis, and UI overlays.

---

# Example 1 — “This Move Breaks Continuity”

### Position  
White plays a forcing move on the right side.

### Triadic Commentary  
**“White gains locally, but the move fractures structural continuity.  
The pressure spike on the right collapses the long‑arc identity White built on the left.  
Continuity drift reverses, and the position enters a volatile regime.”**

### Breakdown  
- **Regime:** 1/3 local, weak 2/3 structural  
- **Resonance:** pressure spike → imbalance  
- **Topology:** new cut point  
- **Continuity:** arc collapse  
- **Risk:** paradox precursor  
- **Score:** local ↑, structural ↓↓, continuity ↓

# Summary

Triadic commentary reveals:

- continuity vs paradox  
- resonance vs collapse  
- ancestry vs projection‑loss  
- drift vs misalignment  
- structural vs local identity  
- unified triadic scoring  

RTT‑Go commentary does not describe *tactics* —  
it explains the **identity** of the position.
