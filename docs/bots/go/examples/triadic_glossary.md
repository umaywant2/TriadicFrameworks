# Triadic Glossary — RTT‑Go  
*(for `/docs/bots/go/examples/triadic_glossary.md`)*

This glossary defines all major triadic terms used throughout RTT‑Go.  
It is designed for:

- teaching mode  
- analysis mode  
- UI overlays  
- engine integration  
- documentation readers  

Every term is defined in the context of Go, RTT, and the triadic identity of positions.

---

# A — Ancestry

### **Ancestry**
The historical lineage of a group or tactical sequence (ladder, ko, moyo).  
Tracks how earlier moves constrain or empower later ones.

### **Ancestry Arc**
A continuity arc specifically tied to ladders, ko fights, or long tactical sequences.

### **Ancestry Collapse**
When a ladder or ko sequence fails due to a distant stone or structural weakness.

---

# C — Collapse, Continuity, Cut Points

### **Collapse**
A structural failure in topology or continuity caused by a move that contradicts the long‑arc identity.

### **Collapse Signature**
A pattern indicating imminent collapse (weak shape, broken influence, unstable ancestry).

### **Continuity**
The long‑arc identity of a position — how moves relate across space and time.

### **Continuity Arc**
A curved overlay showing the flow of long‑arc identity across the board.

### **Continuity Drift**
The direction in which continuity arcs naturally move as the game evolves.

### **Cut Point**
A point where connectivity fractures, often creating paradox or collapse risk.

---

# D — Drift

### **Drift**
The directional flow of influence, resonance, and continuity across the board.

### **Drift Alignment**
A move that follows the natural direction of drift.

### **Drift Misalignment**
A move that contradicts drift, often causing paradox or continuity inversion.

---

# I — Identity, Influence

### **Identity (Triadic Identity)**
The unified structural, continuity, and resonance meaning of a position.

### **Identity Inversion**
When a move reverses the intended long‑arc identity (e.g., invading a strong moyo).

### **Influence Arc**
A curved overlay showing the flow of influence across the board.

---

# K — Ko, Key Points

### **Ko Topology**
The structural stability of a ko fight — how threats and responses interact.

### **Ko Threat Ancestry**
The lineage of ko threats and how they relate to earlier moves.

### **Key Point (Triadic Key Point)**
A move that stabilizes continuity, resonance, or topology.

---

# L — Ladder, Lumen

### **Ladder Ancestry**
The lineage of a ladder sequence — stable or unstable.

### **Ladder Collapse**
When a ladder fails due to a distant stone.

### **Lumen (RTT/1)**
The operator family responsible for structural extraction.

---

# M — Moyo, MCTS

### **Moyo Continuity**
The coherence of a moyo boundary across moves.

### **Moyo Drift**
The natural expansion direction of a moyo.

### **MCTS Triadic Hooks**
RTT’s integration points into MCTS (priors, values, ordering, pruning).

---

# P — Paradox, Pressure, Projection‑Loss

### **Paradox**
A move that is locally strong but globally harmful.

### **Paradox Precursor**
A pattern indicating an upcoming paradox.

### **Pressure Gradient**
A resonance field showing directional pressure.

### **Projection‑Loss**
Losing continuity because a move contradicts the long‑arc plan.

---

# R — Regime, Resonance, Risk

### **Regime (Triadic Regime)**
The classification of a move:

- **1/3 Local**  
- **2/3 Structural**  
- **3/3 Continuity**

### **Resonance**
The vibrational influence field created by stones.

### **Resonance Pressure**
The intensity of influence in a region.

### **Risk Engine**
The RTT engine that detects paradox, collapse, and projection‑loss.

---

# S — Stability, Structure, Scoring

### **Stability (Triadic Stability)**
How well a move preserves continuity, resonance, and topology.

### **Structural Identity**
The shape, connectivity, and influence meaning of a position.

### **Triadic Score**
The unified score produced by Harmonia (RTT/12).

---

# T — Topology, Threats, Triadic Identity

### **Topology**
The connectivity and shape structure of the board.

### **Topology Collapse**
A failure in connectivity (cut, ladder break, ko instability).

### **Triadic Identity**
The unified meaning of a position across:

- structure  
- continuity  
- resonance  
- ancestry  
- drift  
- risk  

---

# Summary

This glossary defines the core vocabulary of RTT‑Go:

- continuity  
- resonance  
- topology  
- ancestry  
- drift  
- paradox  
- collapse  
- projection‑loss  
- triadic scoring  

These terms form the language of triadic Go —  
the deeper structure RTT reveals inside the game.
