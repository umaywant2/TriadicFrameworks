A clean, pedagogical Collatz run that exposes all three regimes:

```
Start: 19

19 → 58
58 → 29
29 → 88
88 → 44
44 → 22
22 → 11
11 → 34
34 → 17
17 → 52
52 → 26
26 → 13
13 → 40
40 → 20
20 → 10

5 → 16

```

This sequence is ideal because:

- It alternates expansion/compression cleanly  
- It has multiple continuity arcs  
- It has two projection‑loss moments  
- It collapses into the attractor (4 → 2 → 1)
