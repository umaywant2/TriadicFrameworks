# ✅ `/docs/bots/hanabi/golden/example_input.txt`

A compact, readable 5‑turn cooperative sequence:

```
HANABI SESSION — 5 TURNS

TURN 1:
Player A gives a color hint to Player B (Blue cards).
Notes: establishing cooperative intent.

TURN 2:
Player B plays the hinted Blue 1.
Notes: continuity arc begins.

TURN 3:
Player A discards a Red 5.
Notes: discard topology shift; potential collapse signature.

TURN 4:
Player B gives a number hint to Player A (2s).
Notes: resonance field forming.

TURN 5:
Player A misplays a Yellow 2 instead of the intended Blue 2.
Notes: projection-loss event.
```

This sequence is ideal because:

- It shows **hint → play → discard → hint → misplay**  
- It contains **continuity**, **collapse**, **projection‑loss**  
- It is small enough to be readable but rich enough for RTT
