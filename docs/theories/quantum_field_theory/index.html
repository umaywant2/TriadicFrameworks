🌀 QFT Module

Quantum Field Theory is the substrate‑level excitation grammar that unifies quantum amplitudes, relativistic geometry, symmetry groups, operator algebra, vacuum structure, and renormalization flow.

Module Summary

QFT is treated in TriadicFrameworks as a substrate‑level excitation grammar, not a particle ontology. It defines:

  • fields and their excitation modes
  • operator algebra (creation, annihilation, propagators)
  • symmetry geometry (gauge + global)
  • vacuum stability surfaces
  • renormalization flow across scales
  • RTT regime behavior (R1 → R4)
