## 0. How to Read This Document

This matrix maps all eleven Triadic Pantheon profiles against **ten universal operator slots** — functional archetypes that appear in every system, though they are named, narrativized, and weighted differently in each tradition. The operator slots are not invented categories imposed from outside; they emerge inductively from the overlap of all eleven profiles.

**Three tiers, ten operator slots:**

| Tier | Slot | Function Class | Core Question |
|---|---|---|---|
| I — Celestial | C1 | Primordial Container | What holds everything? |
| I — Celestial | C2 | Sovereign / Law-Giver | Who rules the ordered cosmos? |
| I — Celestial | C3 | Creative Breath / Scale-Setter | What animates and measures form? |
| II — Civilizational | V1 | Connector / Messenger | Who transmits across realms? |
| II — Civilizational | V2 | Force of Consequence | Who enforces cosmic law? |
| II — Civilizational | V3 | Craftsman / Unifier | Who builds the structure of the world? |
| II — Civilizational | V4 | Keeper of Record | Who holds knowledge, memory, and fate? |
| III — Chthonic | H1 | Primordial Destabilizer | What threatens the order from below? |
| III — Chthonic | H2 | Shapeshifter / Daemon | Who governs permitted transformation? |
| III — Chthonic | H3 | Threshold / Mirror Deity | Who stands between worlds? |

**Confidence markers** appear in syncretism tables:
- ✦ Strong alignment (structural + narrative + functional match)
- ◈ Moderate alignment (functional match; narrative divergence)
- ◇ Weak alignment (surface resemblance only; use with caution)
- ✕ Conflict (structural incompatibility; syncretism forbidden)
