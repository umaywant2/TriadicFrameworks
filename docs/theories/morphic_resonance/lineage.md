# Lineage — Morphic Resonance  
### TriadicFrameworks /docs/theories/morphic_resonance/lineage.md

interface**, not a field, force, or metaphysical influence. Patterns
recur across time because their **coherence structures overlap**, not
because anything is transmitted or remembered.
