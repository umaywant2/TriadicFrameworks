full harmonic canon.

# 🔷 **Phase 1 — Proto‑Resonance (Pre‑RTT Intuition)**  
### *Before formal structure existed*

**Characteristics:**
- intuitive pattern recognition  
- early triadic thinking  
- symbolic → cognitive substrate only  
- no recursion  
- no harmonic ladders  

**Echo Types:** weak structural echoes (E1)  
**Drift:** none  
**Recursion:** none
