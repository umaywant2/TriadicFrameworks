# 🌄 **GLOBAL ANIMATION RULES**

## **1. Gravity Resonance Rules**
- Always subtle  
- Begins with micro‑tremble  
- Air distortion is soft, never sharp  
- Lift is slow, intentional, relational  
- Drop is gentle, cushioned  

## **2. Cloak Behavior**
### Little Gravity
- Responds to emotion  
- Micro‑flutter during resonance  
- Settles slowly after emotional peaks  

### The Master
- Cloak remains still  
- Symbol of internal stillness  
- Only moves when he walks  

## **3. Environmental Motion**
- Mist curls and parts around resonance  
- Leaves respond to emotional shifts  
- Light flickers subtly during breakthroughs

# 🌅 **SCENE‑BY‑SCENE ANIMATION BEATS**

---

# **Scene 1 — Arrival at the Apprentice Yard**

### **Primary Motion Beats**
- Little Gravity enters frame slowly  
- Boots disturb mist in soft curls  
- Master stands perfectly still  

### **Resonance Beats**
- None yet — world is calm  

### **Cloak + Environmental Beats**
- Little Gravity’s cloak catches morning light  
- Mist drifts across courtyard tiles  

### **Emotional Timing**
- Curiosity → anticipation  

---

# **Scene 2 — The Meeting**

### **Primary Motion Beats**
- Little Gravity shifts weight nervously  
- Master turns only his head at first  
- Subtle eye‑level alignment  

### **Resonance Beats**
- Faint shimmer in air when Master asks foundational questions  

### **Cloak + Environmental Beats**
- Master’s cloak remains still  
- Little Gravity’s cloak moves with each breath  

### **Emotional Timing**
- Uncertainty → respect  

---

# **Scene 3 — The First Lesson**

### **Primary Motion Beats**
- Master raises hand slowly  
- Stone lifts with micro‑tremble  
- Stone drifts toward Master’s palm  

### **Resonance Beats**
- Air distortion between Master and stone  
- Mist parts around floating stone  

### **Cloak + Environmental Beats**
- Little Gravity’s cloak flutters with awe  
- Master’s cloak unmoving  

### **Emotional Timing**
- Confusion → revelation  

---

# **Scene 4 — The Listening Stone**

### **Primary Motion Beats**
- Little Gravity attempts lift (no movement)  
- Master adjusts posture gently  
- Little Gravity closes eyes → breath slows  
- Stone rises a finger’s width  

### **Resonance Beats**
- Leaves rustle in synchronized pulse  
- Air thickens between hands and stone  
- Micro‑tremble → lift → soft drop  

### **Cloak + Environmental Beats**
- Cloak responds to emotional vulnerability  
- Spiral trees sway subtly  

### **Emotional Timing**
- Frustration → vulnerability → breakthrough  

---

# **Scene 5 — The Weight of Kindness**

### **Primary Motion Beats**
- Little Gravity kneels beside creature  
- Branch lifts slowly, gently  
- Creature scurries free  

### **Resonance Beats**
- Warm, soft resonance glow  
- Air distortion minimal — kindness over power  

### **Cloak + Environmental Beats**
- Cloak settles around knees  
- Clearing light warms during lift  

### **Emotional Timing**
- Empathy → responsibility → warmth  

---

# **Scene 6 — The Apprentice’s Oath**

### **Primary Motion Beats**
- Master and apprentice face each other  
- Oath gesture: hands open, palms forward  
- Master places hand on Little Gravity’s shoulder  

### **Resonance Beats**
- Subtle shimmer around both characters  
- Air stills as oath completes  

### **Cloak + Environmental Beats**
- Cloaks catch last rays of dusk  
- Shadows lengthen across courtyard  

### **Emotional Timing**
- Pride → resolve → beginning of mastery
