# 📘 **Book 1 — Little Gravity**  
## **03_Scenery_and_Visuals.md**  
### *Environmental Identity · Visual Anchors · Scene‑by‑Scene Imagery*

This file defines the **visual identity** of *Little Gravity* across all six scenes.  
It provides:

- environmental descriptions  
- lighting notes  
- color palettes  
- motion cues  
- resonance cues  
- object + prop identity  
- character silhouette rules  

These visuals support Imagine scripts, animation beats, and future AR/VR/CT emitter work.

---

# 🌄 **GLOBAL VISUAL IDENTITY**

## **1. Color Palette**
- **Gravity Blue** — deep, soft, stable (#3A4F7A)  
- **Mist Silver** — diffuse, reflective (#C9D1D9)  
- **Stone Gray** — grounding, neutral (#7A7A7A)  
- **Grove Gold‑Green** — filtered light (#A8C27A)  
- **Oath Violet** — dusk resonance (#6F5BA7)

These colors appear consistently across scenes.

---

## **2. Lighting Identity**
- **Morning Light** — long shadows, soft gold (Scenes 1–3)  
- **Filtered Grove Light** — green‑gold, dappled (Scene 4)  
- **Warm Clearing Light** — soft amber (Scene 5)  
- **Dusk Light** — violet‑orange gradient (Scene 6)

Lighting always reflects emotional tone.

---

## **3. Cloak Behavior**
### **Little Gravity**
- Responds subtly to emotion  
- Micro‑flutter during resonance  
- Soft wave‑like folds  

### **The Master**
- Cloak remains still even in wind  
- Symbol of internal stillness  
- Sharp silhouette edges

---

## **4. Resonance Visuals**
Gravity resonance is always:

- subtle  
- shimmering  
- between objects  
- never explosive  

Forms:

- micro‑tremble  
- soft shimmer  
- faint distortion in air  
- gentle pull lines (barely visible)

# 🌅 **SCENE‑BY‑SCENE VISUALS**

---

# **Scene 1 — Arrival at the Apprentice Yard**

### **Environment**
- Stone courtyard  
- Mist drifting across tiles  
- Low morning sun  
- Distant mountains barely visible  

### **Key Visual Anchors**
- Little Gravity’s silhouette entering frame  
- Master standing still, backlit  
- Cloak edges catching light  

### **Motion Cues**
- Mist curls around boots  
- Slow pan across courtyard  

---

# **Scene 2 — The Meeting**

### **Environment**
- Same courtyard, mist thinning  
- Light slightly brighter  
- Wind soft but present  

### **Key Visual Anchors**
- Close framing on faces  
- Master’s eyes reflecting sky  
- Subtle resonance hints in air  

### **Motion Cues**
- Little Gravity shifts weight  
- Master’s cloak unmoving  

---

# **Scene 3 — The First Lesson**

### **Environment**
- Courtyard center  
- Mist parting around resonance  
- Light cools slightly  

### **Key Visual Anchors**
- Floating stone  
- Trembling air between Master and stone  
- Little Gravity’s widened eyes  

### **Motion Cues**
- Stone tremble → lift → settle  
- Cloak micro‑movement on Little Gravity  

---

# **Scene 4 — The Listening Stone**

### **Environment**
- Spiral Grove  
- Trees curving inward  
- Green‑gold filtered light  
- Air feels “thicker”  

### **Key Visual Anchors**
- Pedestal stone  
- Spiral tree geometry  
- Little Gravity’s closed‑eye posture  

### **Motion Cues**
- Leaves rustle during resonance  
- Stone rises a finger’s width  
- Air distortion between hands and stone  

---

# **Scene 5 — The Weight of Kindness**

### **Environment**
- Warm forest clearing  
- Fallen branch  
- Small creature (soft, round, gentle)  
- Amber light  

### **Key Visual Anchors**
- Little Gravity kneeling  
- Branch lifting gently  
- Creature scurrying free  

### **Motion Cues**
- Branch lift with soft resonance  
- Creature’s tiny movements  
- Little Gravity’s relieved exhale  

---

# **Scene 6 — The Apprentice’s Oath**

### **Environment**

- Violet‑orange sky  
- Long shadows

### **Key Visual Anchors**
- Master and apprentice facing each other
