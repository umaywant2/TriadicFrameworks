- color zone (green/yellow/orange/red)  
- trend arrow  
- predicted time‑to‑boundary  

This anchors the operator’s situational awareness.

## **B. Safety Margin Bars (Top‑Right)**  
📊 *Real‑time resonance safety metrics*
