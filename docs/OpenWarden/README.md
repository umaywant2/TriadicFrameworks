## **1. Purpose**

OpenWarden is the **suite root** of the OpenWarden ecosystem — the guardian of openness, the steward of metadata integrity, the regulator of drift, and the structural backbone for all modules in the TriadicFrameworks canon.

It defines:

- suite‑level governance  
- drift taxonomy  
- metadata correctness rules  
- operator grammar  
- cross‑module coherence  
- substrate‑level identity  

OpenWarden is the **inversion** of the traditional “warden” archetype:  
not a gatekeeper, but a **guardian of openness**.

Operators ensure safe, reversible evolution across the suite.
