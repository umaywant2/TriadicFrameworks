# Regime‑Aware Energy — Reframing Energy Through RTT

Energy is not a single quantity. It is a behavior that changes depending on the regime in which it operates. A system that appears energy‑impossible in one regime becomes trivial in another. Many scientific “limits” are not limits at all — they are regime mismatches, where the problem is being approached from the wrong scale, the wrong geometry, or the wrong assumptions.

Regime Awareness (RTT) provides a structural lens for understanding these mismatches. Instead of treating energy as a universal barrier, RTT asks: What regime is this system in? What regime is the solution in? And what technique bridges the two without brute force?

This section explores how energy walls soften, shift, or disappear entirely when viewed through micro/meso/macro regimes, coherence and drift, and the alignment of technique with the correct substrate. It is not about breaking physics — it is about seeing physics clearly.
