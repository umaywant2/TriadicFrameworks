# 🜁 Truth vs Belief vs Evidence  
**RTT/1 · Resonance Domain · Triadic Comparison**

Truth, Belief, and Evidence are often treated as philosophical categories.  
In RTT, they are **resonance‑field constructs** with distinct operator profiles, coherence behaviors, drift patterns, and paradox signatures.

This comparison shows how each behaves as a triadic semantic object.

---

# 1. High‑Level RTT Summary Table

| Construct | Resonance Mode | Coherence | Drift | Paradox | Regime Behavior |
|----------|----------------|-----------|--------|---------|------------------|
| **Truth** | Ω₁–Ω₄ | C₁–C₄ | D₁–D₄ | P₁–P₃ | Declared → Contested → Collapsed → Inverted |
| **Belief** | Ω₂–Ω₃ | C₂–C₃ | D₂–D₃ | P₂ | Declared → Reinforced → Entrenched |
| **Evidence** | Ω₁ | C₁ | D₁ | P₀ (none) | Stable → Aggregated → Overridden |

Truth is **dynamic**.  
Belief is **self‑reinforcing**.  
Evidence is **structural**.

---

# 2. Resonance Comparison (Ω Operators)

### **Truth — Ω₁ → Ω₂ → Ω₃ → Ω₄**
Truth moves across all resonance modes:
- literal  
- interpretive  
- generalized  
- broadcast/inverted  

Truth is the **most resonant** of the three.

### **Belief — Ω₂ → Ω₃**
Belief rarely stays literal.  
It is interpretive and generalized.

Belief is a **context‑resonant** field.

### **Evidence — Ω₁**
Evidence is literal, structural, and stable.

Evidence is a **low‑resonance** field.

---

# 3. Coherence Comparison (C Operators)

### **Truth — C₁ → C₂ → C₃ → C₄**
Truth can:
- stabilize  
- be declared  
- become contested  
- invert  

Truth has **full coherence mobility**.

### **Belief — C₂ → C₃**
Belief is:
- declared  
- unbound  
- rarely structural  
- rarely inverted  

Belief has **medium coherence mobility**.

### **Evidence — C₁**
Evidence is:
- structural  
- stable  
- coherence‑anchored  

Evidence has **no coherence mobility**.

---

# 4. Drift Comparison (D Operators)

### **Truth — D₁ → D₂ → D₃ → D₄**
Truth drifts:
- structurally  
- bounded  
- unbounded  
- radiatively  

Truth is the **most drift‑susceptible**.

### **Belief — D₂ → D₃**
Belief drifts:
- bounded  
- unbounded  

Belief is **interpretively drift‑driven**.

### **Evidence — D₁**
Evidence drifts only structurally.

Evidence is **drift‑minimal**.

---

# 5. Paradox Comparison (P Operators)

### **Truth — P₁, P₂, P₃**
Truth can:
- contradict itself  
- contradict interpretation  
- invert regimes  

Truth is **paradox‑active**.

### **Belief — P₂**
Belief experiences:
- semantic paradox  
- interpretive contradiction  

Belief is **paradox‑sensitive**.

### **Evidence — P₀**
Evidence does not generate paradox.

Evidence is **paradox‑neutral**.

---

# 6. Regime Comparison (R Operators)

### **Truth — R₁ → R₂ → R₃ → R₄**
Truth transitions:
- declared  
- contested  
- collapsed  
- inverted  

Truth is **regime‑dynamic**.

### **Belief — R₁ → R₂ → R₃**
Belief transitions:
- declared  
- reinforced  
- entrenched  

Belief is **regime‑reinforcing**.

### **Evidence — R₁ → R₂**
Evidence transitions:
- stable  
- aggregated  

Evidence is **regime‑stable**.

---

# 7. SET Triad Comparison (Spin · Elec · Temp)

| Construct | Spin (alignment) | Elec (interpretive charge) | Temp (activation threshold) |
|----------|-------------------|-----------------------------|------------------------------|
| **Truth** | medium → high | medium → extreme | medium → extreme |
| **Belief** | medium | high | low → medium |
| **Evidence** | high | low | high |

### Truth  
- Spin aligns meaning  
- Elec separates interpretations  
- Temp activates paradox  

Truth is **triad‑volatile**.

### Belief  
- Spin aligns identity  
- Elec separates worldviews  
- Temp activates reinforcement  

Belief is **triad‑self‑stabilizing**.

### Evidence  
- Spin aligns structure  
- Elec is minimal  
- Temp is high (resists paradox)  

Evidence is **triad‑stable**.

---

Truth is dynamic.
