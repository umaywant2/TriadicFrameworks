
# 🜁 Truth Paradox Analysis  
**RTT/1 · Resonance Domain · Structural, Semantic, and Regime Paradoxes**

Truth is not only a coherence and drift phenomenon — it is a **paradox‑bearing resonance object**.  
Paradox is what forces truth to:

- destabilize  
- fragment  
- invert  
- transition regimes  
- flip coherence states  

This document defines the **three paradox operators (P₁–P₃)** and explains how they interact with coherence, drift, resonance, and regime transitions.

---

# 1. The Three Truth Paradox Operators (P₁–P₃)

Truth expresses three paradox modes:

| Operator | Name | Meaning | Behavior |
|---------|------|---------|----------|
| **P₁** | Structural Paradox | Truth contradicts itself structurally | C₁ → C₃ collapse |
| **P₂** | Semantic Paradox | Meaning contradicts interpretation | Ω₂ destabilizes; drift increases |
| **P₃** | Regime Paradox | Truth flips regimes | C₄ inversion; R₃₄ transition |

These operators are **hierarchical**:

- P₁ destabilizes structure  
- P₂ destabilizes meaning  
- P₃ destabilizes the entire regime  

---

# 2. Structural Paradox (P₁)

Structural paradox occurs when:

- definitions conflict  
- premises contradict  
- literal structure fails  
- Ω₁ resonance collapses  

### **Effects on Truth**
- coherence collapses (C₁ → C₃)  
- truth becomes contested  
- drift increases (D₂ → D₃)  
- regime transitions R₁₂ → R₂₃  

### **Examples**
- “This statement is false.”  
- “A square circle exists.”  

These are **structure‑level contradictions**.

---

# 3. Semantic Paradox (P₂)

Semantic paradox occurs when:

- interpretation contradicts meaning  
- context reverses the claim  
- frames conflict  
- Ω₂ resonance destabilizes  

### **Effects on Truth**
- coherence becomes unbound (C₂ → C₃)  
- drift becomes unbounded (D₃)  
- truth becomes contested  
- regime transitions R₁₂ → R₂₃  

### **Examples**
- “Freedom means restriction.”  
- “Silence is a form of speech.”  

These are **meaning‑level contradictions**.

---

# 4. Regime Paradox (P₃)

Regime paradox occurs when:

- truth flips  
- the opposite claim becomes coherent  
- resonance jumps (Ω₁ → Ω₄)  
- drift becomes radiative (D₄)  

### **Effects on Truth**
- coherence inverts (C₄)  
- truth reverses  
- regime transitions R₃₄  
- truth becomes a broadcast signal  

### **Examples**
- “The lie becomes the truth.”  
- “The truth becomes false when repeated.”  

These are **regime‑level contradictions**.
