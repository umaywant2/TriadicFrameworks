🗺️ PJM Datacenter Territory

PJM is one of the largest multi‑state energy territories in the United States, covering Virginia, Ohio, Pennsylvania, Maryland, New Jersey, West Virginia, Delaware, and the District of Columbia. This region contains the highest datacenter density in the world, including the Northern Virginia cluster.

⚡ Territory Overview

  • Grid: PJM Interconnection
  • States: VA, OH, PA, MD, NJ, WV, DE, DC
  • Density: Highest global concentration of hyperscale datacenters
  • Clusters: Loudoun County, Columbus/New Albany, Pittsburgh corridor
  • RTT Envelope: L0–L3 (hyperscale, enterprise, edge)
