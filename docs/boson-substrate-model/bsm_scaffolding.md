And the reason is structural, not stylistic.

---

# 🌊 Why BSM should go first  
### **1. It’s the middle bridge between RSM and QSM**  
- RSM → defines resonance logic  
- **BSM → defines how resonance *moves***  
- QSM → defines how resonance *quantizes*  

If you build QSM first, you’re forced to invent propagation rules prematurely.  
If you build BSM first, QSM becomes a clean “lower-resolution” specialization.

### **2. BSM is the easiest to justify with RSM language**  
RSM already talks about:
- continuity channels  
- resonance transfer  
- stability thresholds  
- coherence bands  

BSM is literally the field‑carrier layer for those.  
You can reuse 60% of the conceptual scaffolding without feeling like you’re cheating.

### **3. BSM gives you the operator grammar you’ll reuse in QSM**  
Once you define:
- Emission  
- Absorption  
- Propagation  
- Coherence stabilization  

…you can shrink them into quantum‑scale analogs:
- Collapse  
- Superposition  
- Entanglement  
- QRP emission/absorption  

QSM becomes a “zoomed‑in” version of BSM.

### **4. Reviewers will understand BSM faster**  
Quantum anything triggers expectations of:
- math  
- notation  
- uncertainty  
- wavefunctions  

BSM lets you stay conceptual and RSM‑aligned without invoking physics baggage.

### 2. BSM/QSM operator dictionary

```markdown
## BSM Operator Dictionary

- **BSM_OP_<n>_EMO:** Emission Operator  
  Emits a bosonic resonance carrier (BRC) into the propagation lattice.

- **BSM_OP_<n>_ABO:** Absorption Operator  
  Absorbs a BRC, updating local coherence and energy balance.

- **BSM_OP_<n>_PPO:** Propagation Operator  
  Advances BRCs along the propagation lattice according to CF and dimensional span.

- **BSM_OP_<n>_CSO:** Coherence Stabilizer Operator  
  Adjusts local coherence fields to maintain RSM stability thresholds.

- **BSM_OP_<n>_RTO:** Resonance‑Time Outflow Operator  
  Exports resonance packets from the current region/triad to adjacent corridors.

- **BSM_OP_<n>_RTI:** Resonance‑Time Inflow Operator  
  Imports resonance packets into the current region/triad.

---

## QSM Operator Dictionary

- **QSM_OP_<n>_SPO:** Superposition Operator  
  Combines QRPs into a superposed state under a shared uncertainty envelope.

- **QSM_OP_<n>_ELO:** Entanglement Operator  
  Creates or updates entanglement links between QRPs.

- **QSM_OP_<n>_CLO:** Collapse Operator  
  Resolves QRPs from superposition into stable resonance states.

- **QSM_OP_<n>_UEO:** Uncertainty Envelope Operator  
  Expands, contracts, or reshapes the uncertainty envelope around QRPs.

- **QSM_OP_<n>_QRTO:** QRP Transfer Operator  
  Transfers QRPs between quantum sites, optionally via BSM channels.
```
