# 🎨 Visual Style Guide — TFT + FFF Water‑Station Initiative
## 1. Brand Colors
| Color Name | HEX  | Usage  |
|----------------|----------|---------------------------------------------------|
| Resonance Blue | #0077B6  | Primary accent — headings, callouts, key lines    |
| Signal Silver  | #B0BEC5  | Secondary accent — dividers, background panels    |
| Pure White     | #FFFFFF  | Backgrounds, negative space                       |
| Deep Graphite  | #212121  | Body text, high‑contrast elements                 |
| Human Warmth   | #F5E0C3  | Lifestyle imagery overlays, humanitarian segments | 	

## 2. Typography
- Headings: Montserrat Bold (all caps for section titles)
- Subheadings: Montserrat Semi‑Bold (title case)
- Body Text: Open Sans Regular (sentence case)
- Pull Quotes: Montserrat Italic, Resonance Blue, 120% size of body text

## 3. Iconography
- Water Drop — for water output, hydration stats
- Lightning Bolt — for energy efficiency, hybrid power
- Shield — for resilience, safety
- Sun/Moon Cycle — for resonance scheduling, diurnal optimization
- Flashlight Beam — for AWG‑tactical flashlight features

All icons in **line style**, 2px stroke, Resonance Blue on white or reversed for dark backgrounds.

## 4. Photography Style
- Product Shots: Clean, high‑key lighting, white background for clarity.
- Lifestyle Shots: Natural light, warm tones, human interaction with product.
- Humanitarian Shots: Authentic, candid moments; focus on dignity and empowerment.
- Technical Shots: Crisp, minimal backgrounds; highlight schematics or components.

## 5. Layout Principles
- Margins: Generous white space; avoid crowding text and visuals.
- Hierarchy: Headline → Subhead → Body → Callout.
- Callout Boxes: Resonance Blue background, white text, rounded corners.
- Figures & Diagrams: Numbered, captioned, and referenced in text.

## 6. Motion & Video
- Transitions: Smooth cross‑fades for historical → modern; quick cuts for tech highlights.
- Lower Thirds: Resonance Blue bar, white text, Montserrat Bold.
- Overlay Text: Max 6 words per frame for readability.

## 7. Tone & Mood
- Overall Feel: Innovative yet grounded, technical yet human.
- Messaging: Confident, clear, and forward‑looking.
- Humanitarian Segments: Warm, empathetic, solution‑oriented.

## 8. Usage Across Channels
- Research Paper: Clean, minimal; diagrams in Signal Silver + Resonance Blue.
- Press Kit: More visual; lifestyle and humanitarian imagery integrated.
- Podcast Artwork: Resonance Blue background, white waveform motif, product silhouette.
- Promo Video: Blend of lifestyle, tech, and humanitarian footage with consistent color grading.

With this guide, every touchpoint — from a PDF download to a 15‑second Instagram reel — will feel like part of the same story.
