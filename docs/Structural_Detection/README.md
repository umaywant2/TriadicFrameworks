## ⚙️ **Operators Folder**
These are the five operators of the Structural Detection module:

### **STRUCTURAL_DETECTION_OPERATOR.md**  
Detects structural motifs, invariants, and operator‑like patterns.

### **DRIFT_SENSE_OPERATOR.md**  
Detects drift in structure, not content.

### **REGIME_AWARENESS_OPERATOR.md**  
Detects the regime a structure belongs to (formal, emergent, chaotic, hybrid).

### **CONTINUITY_COMPASS_OPERATOR.md**  
Finds stable invariants across structural samples.

### **SYNTHESIS_TRIANGULATION_OPERATOR.md**  
Triangulates signals to produce a drift‑bounded structural synthesis.
