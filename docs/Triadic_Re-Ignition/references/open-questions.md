# Triadic Re-Ignition: Open Questions

> **An open question preserves uncertainty without turning uncertainty into a conclusion.**

Triadic Re-Ignition begins with observations that may become meaningful before their mechanisms are clear.

The purpose of this document is to preserve unresolved questions across the module without requiring premature answers.

An open question may identify:

```text
missing evidence

missing structure

missing observer variables

competing interpretations

unknown mechanisms

representation limits

untested predictions

or unresolved paradox
```

An open question should not silently contain its preferred answer.

For example:

```text
GOOD:

"What changed when the pattern
became recognizable?"
```

is structurally different from:

```text
LOADED:

"What hidden mechanism restored
the pattern?"
```

The second question has already assumed:

```text
hidden mechanism

restoration

and prior persistence
```

before those claims have been established.

The discipline of this document is therefore:

> **Keep the question as open as the evidence actually requires.**

# Open-Question Discipline

Every open question should ideally distinguish between:

```text
WHAT IS OBSERVED?

WHAT IS UNKNOWN?

WHAT HYPOTHESES EXIST?

WHAT COULD DISTINGUISH THEM?
```

The preferred structure is:

```text
Observation
     ↓
Open Question
     ↓
Candidate Hypotheses
     ↓
Possible Discriminator
     ↓
Evidence
     ↓
Revision
```

Not every question will immediately have a discriminator.

That is acceptable.

Sometimes the next task is simply:

```text
make the question operational
```

---

# Question States

Questions may be tagged conceptually as:

```text
OPEN
```

No adequate answer currently exists.

```text
PARTIALLY BOUNDED
```

Some interpretations have been excluded or constrained.

```text
OPERATIONALIZED
```

The question has been translated into something observable or testable.

```text
UNDER TEST
```

Evidence is actively being compared.

```text
PROVISIONALLY RESOLVED
```

A working answer currently fits the evidence but remains revisable.

```text
CLOSED
```

The module no longer considers the question meaningfully unresolved.

Closed questions should normally retain their reasoning history rather than simply disappearing.

# Avoiding Question Drift

Questions themselves can drift.

Example:

```text
START:

"What changed during recognition?"
```

may silently become:

```text
"How was the hidden pattern restored?"
```

Notice what happened.

The second version introduced:

```text
hidden

pattern persistence

restoration mechanism
```

without first establishing them.

When this occurs:

```text
RETURN TO OBSERVATION
        ↓
REMOVE ASSUMPTIONS
        ↓
RESTORE OPEN QUESTION
```

This is drift control applied to inquiry itself.

# Working Summary

Triadic Re-Ignition should accumulate questions more carefully than it accumulates answers.

The process is:

```text
OBSERVATION
     ↓
QUESTION
     ↓
HYPOTHESES
     ↓
INVARIANTS
     ↓
DISCRIMINATORS
     ↓
TESTING
     ↓
REVISION
```

Not:

```text
OBSERVATION
     ↓
QUESTION
     ↓
PREFERRED ANSWER
     ↓
CERTAINTY
```

An unresolved question is not a defect in the framework.

Sometimes it is the most accurate representation currently available.

The central discipline is:

> **Do not hide an answer inside the question.  
> Do not fill uncertainty simply because vocabulary exists.  
> Preserve competing explanations.  
> Search for invariants.  
> Build discriminators where possible.  
> Let questions become narrower as evidence becomes stronger.**

In the shortest form:

```text
OPEN
  ↓
BOUND
  ↓
OPERATIONALIZE
  ↓
TEST
  ↓
REFINE
```

And when the evidence still does not decide:

```text
UNKNOWN
```

is a valid result.

The goal of the Open Questions registry is not to make uncertainty disappear.

**It is to make uncertainty structured enough that someone else can continue the investigation.**
