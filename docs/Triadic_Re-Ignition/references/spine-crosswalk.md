> **A crosswalk identifies structural correspondence. It does not silently convert an exploratory module into established canon.**

This document maps the working concepts of Triadic Re-Ignition to the analytical overlays of the TriadicFrameworks Spine.

The crosswalk supports:

```text
structural navigation

terminology alignment

operator comparison

drift detection

module interoperability

future review
```

It does not establish that Triadic Re-Ignition has been incorporated into the canonical Spine.

The relationship is:

```text
Triadic Re-Ignition
        ↓
exploratory module

TriadicFrameworks Spine
        ↓
structural reference
```

The crosswalk records correspondence between them while preserving their different statuses.

# 1. Structural Overlay

The Structural Overlay provides the strongest initial connection between the Spine and Triadic Re-Ignition.

Re-Ignition examines transitions such as:

```text
inaccessible
     ↓
accessible
```

```text
unrecognized
     ↓
recognized
```

```text
fragmented
     ↓
reintegrated
```

```text
unstable
     ↓
stabilized
```

These can be analyzed as structural transitions without first deciding their mechanism.

---

## Structural Objects

The primary structural objects in Re-Ignition include:

```text
observer

pattern

relationship

context

trigger

representation

timeline

projection

invariant

regime
```

Their working relationships can be represented as:

```text
PATTERN
   +
OBSERVER
   +
CONTEXT
   ↓
OBSERVATION
   ↓
RECOGNITION
   ↓
REPRESENTATION
```

This is a structural model.

It does not yet specify a physical or neurological process.

---

## Structural Transition

The core Re-Ignition sequence is:

```text
DORMANT / INACCESSIBLE
          ↓
       TRIGGER
          ↓
      RESONANCE
          ↓
     RECOGNITION
          ↓
    REINTEGRATION
          ↓
    STABILIZATION
```

The Structural Overlay asks:

```text

What relationships connect them?

Where does the transition occur?

What remains invariant?

What new structure becomes accessible?
```

## Structural Questions

The Structural Overlay contributes the following questions:

1. What are the relevant states?

2. What relationship changes?

3. Where is the observer located?

4. What is preserved across the transition?

5. What changes in accessibility?

6. Does the structure change, or does access to it change?

7. Can the transition be modeled without deciding ontology first?

# 2. Drift Overlay

The Drift Overlay is essential because Re-Ignition events can feel compelling before their mechanisms are understood.

A common drift path is:

```text
observation
    ↓
recognition
    ↓
interpretation
    ↓
mechanism
    ↓
ontology
    ↓
certainty
```

Each transition strengthens the claim.

The Drift Overlay makes those transitions observable.

---

## Re-Ignition Drift Risks

Primary drift risks include:

```text
interpretation drift

narrative lock

binary collapse

form lock

timeline inflation

observer omission

resonance-causation collapse

recognition-verification collapse

premature ontology
```

These risks do not make Re-Ignition observations invalid.

They identify where claim strength can exceed available support.

---

## Drift Crosswalk

```text
Re-Ignition Risk          Drift Function
------------------------------------------------
Interpretation Drift      explanation replaces observation

Narrative Lock            preferred story resists revision

Binary Collapse           paradox reduced to A or B

Form Lock                 representation resists correction

Timeline Inflation        representation becomes ontology

Observer Omission         access conditions disappear

Resonance Inflation       correspondence becomes causation

Recognition Inflation     familiarity becomes verification

Premature Ontology        model becomes reality claim
```

---

## Bounded Drift Path

Triadic Re-Ignition uses:

```text
OBSERVATION
     ↓
CAPTURE
     ↓
RELATIONSHIP
     ↓
INTERPRETATION
     ↓
HYPOTHESIS
     ↓
ALTERNATIVES
     ↓
TESTING
     ↓
REFINEMENT
```

The Drift Overlay monitors movement between these layers.

Its purpose is not to stop interpretation.

Its purpose is to keep interpretation visible as interpretation.

---

## Drift Questions

1. What was directly observed?

2. What was added during interpretation?

3. When did the claim become stronger?

4. Were alternative explanations preserved?

5. Did resonance become causation without a discriminator?

6. Did recognition become verification?

7. Did a representational difference become a physical claim?

8. What evidence would require revision?

# 3. Coherence Overlay

The Coherence Overlay examines whether relationships remain stable, compatible, and revisitable across transitions.

Within Re-Ignition:

```text
resonance
    ↓
recognition
    ↓
reintegration
    ↓
stabilization
```

can be examined as a coherence-building sequence.

---

## Coherence in Re-Ignition

A re-ignited pattern may initially appear as:

```text
partial

fragmented

unstable

difficult to represent
```

Reintegration attempts to connect it with the observer's current model.

Stabilization asks whether the relationship remains accessible across repeated investigation.

```text
initial recognition
        ↓
repeated access
        ↓
comparison
        ↓
model revision
        ↓
stabilized coherence
```

Stabilized coherence does not automatically establish truth.

It establishes that the model or relationship can be examined consistently.

---

## Coherence Crosswalk

```text
Re-Ignition Term          Coherence Function
------------------------------------------------
Resonance                 initial correspondence

Recognition               explicit relationship access

Reintegration             connection to current model

Stabilization             repeated coherent accessibility

Invariant                  relation surviving transition

Continuity                persistence across states

Paradox                    unresolved coherence boundary

Revision                   correction toward better fit
```

---

## Coherence Test

A candidate Re-Ignition model can be examined through:

```text
internal coherence

cross-observer coherence

temporal coherence

representational coherence

experimental coherence
```

### Internal Coherence

```text
Does the model contradict itself?
```

### Cross-Observer Coherence

```text
Can another observer identify
the same relationship?
```

### Temporal Coherence

```text
Does the relationship remain stable
when revisited later?
```

### Representational Coherence

```text
Do different forms preserve
the same invariant?
```

### Experimental Coherence

```text
Does testing support the expected
relationship?
```

---

## Coherence Questions

1. What remains stable across recognition?

2. Can the relationship be revisited?

3. Can it be represented in more than one form?

4. What remains invariant across those forms?

5. Does the model preserve the original observation?

6. Does reintegration improve coherence or merely strengthen narrative?

7. What contradiction remains unresolved?

# 4. Substrate Overlay

The Substrate Overlay introduces a more cautious relationship.

Re-Ignition frequently asks:

```text
What carries the pattern?

What supports continuity?

Where does the relationship persist?

What makes renewed access possible?
```

These are substrate questions.

However, the module does not assume that a specific substrate has already been identified.

---

## Substrate Boundary

A useful distinction is:

```text
functional substrate question
```

versus:

```text
physical substrate claim
```

The functional question is:

```text
What must persist or recur
for renewed access to occur?
```

The stronger physical claim is:

```text
A specific physical carrier
has been identified.
```

Triadic Re-Ignition can ask the first without claiming the second.

---

## Candidate Substrate Locations

Depending on the domain, candidate support may involve:

```text
memory

documents

environment

practice

physical state

computational state

social transmission

symbolic representation

shared structure

unknown carrier
```

The candidate set should remain open until evidence distinguishes among them.

---

## Substrate Crosswalk

```text
Re-Ignition Term          Substrate Question
------------------------------------------------
Dormant Pattern           where does continuity reside?

Trigger                   what couples to the carrier?

Recognition               how does access become available?

Representation             what supports communicable form?

Reintegration             where is the pattern re-embedded?

Stabilization             what supports repeated access?

Continuity                what persists across the interval?
```

---

## Substrate Drift Risk

A common drift path is:

```text
relationship persists
        ↓
something must support it
        ↓
candidate substrate
        ↓
specific hidden substrate
        ↓
ontological certainty
```

The bounded path is:

```text
observed continuity
        ↓
candidate support conditions
        ↓
alternative substrate models
        ↓
discriminator
        ↓
testing
```

---

## Substrate Questions

1. What must persist for Re-Ignition to occur?

2. Is continuity stored, reconstructed, regenerated, or distributed?

3. What evidence supports each candidate substrate?

4. Could multiple substrate layers contribute?

5. What substrate claim is actually required by the observation?

6. What finding would falsify the preferred carrier model?

```text
analytical dimension
        ≠
```

An analytical dimension may be:

```text

projection regime
```

These can function as independent variables or axes within a model without being claimed as additional physical dimensions.

## Domain Translation

A term should not be transferred between domains without checking what it means operationally.

For example:

```text
resonance in learning
```

may describe:

```text
recognizable conceptual correspondence
```

while:

```text
resonance in a physical model
```

may refer to:

```text
measurable dynamical behavior
```

The shared word does not establish identical mechanisms.

# 7. Clarity Overlay

The Clarity Overlay provides the strongest methodological connection between the Spine and Re-Ignition.

Re-Ignition depends upon separating:

```text
observation

relationship

representation

interpretation

hypothesis

mechanism

ontology
```

Clarity increases when those layers remain visible.

---

## Clarity Sequence

A working clarity sequence is:

```text
CAPTURE
   ↓
SEPARATE
   ↓
LOCATE
   ↓
COMPARE
   ↓
IDENTIFY INVARIANTS
   ↓
GENERATE ALTERNATIVES
   ↓
DEFINE DISCRIMINATORS
   ↓
TEST
   ↓
REFINE
```

This sequence prevents the first compelling interpretation from silently becoming the final claim.

---

## Clarity Crosswalk

```text

------------------------------------------------

Revision                   improves model correspondence
```

---

## Claim Clarity

The Re-Ignition claim ladder is:

```text
OBSERVATION
     ↓
RELATIONSHIP
     ↓
INTERPRETATION
     ↓
HYPOTHESIS
     ↓
MECHANISM
     ↓
ONTOLOGY
```

Clarity requires identifying the current level.

For example:

```text
"I recognized a pattern."
```

is not equivalent to:

```text
"I identified the mechanism
that preserved the pattern."
```

And neither is equivalent to:

```text
"The mechanism reveals the
underlying nature of reality."
```

---

## Clarity Questions

1. What is the smallest supported claim?

2. Which layer does the statement occupy?

3. What is observation versus interpretation?

4. What remains unknown?

5. Which alternatives remain viable?

6. What evidence would strengthen the claim?

7. What evidence would weaken it?

8. Has a metaphor become a mechanism?

9. Has an analytical model become ontology?
