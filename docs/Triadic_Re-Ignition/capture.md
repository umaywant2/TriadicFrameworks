# Triadic Re-Ignition — Capture

> **Capture the event before explaining the event. Preserve the first signal, the observer, the sequence, and the unknowns.**

This document is the rapid-capture front end for Triadic Re-Ignition.

Use it when:

```text
something becomes familiar

an old ability becomes accessible

a relationship suddenly appears

a contradiction becomes visible

a past event acquires new significance

a representation changes

or a recognition event occurs
```

The purpose is not to determine immediately:

```text
what happened physically

what mechanism produced it

whether something was stored

whether something was reconstructed

or what the event ultimately means
```

The purpose is simpler:

```text
CAPTURE
WHAT HAPPENED
BEFORE THE STORY HARDENS
```

---

```text
RTT: 1
```

# Core Capture Principle

A Re-Ignition event may move quickly:

```text
TRIGGER
   ↓
FIRST SIGNAL
   ↓
RESONANCE
   ↓
RECOGNITION
   ↓
INTERPRETATION
   ↓
NARRATIVE
```

The nearer capture occurs to:

```text
FIRST SIGNAL
```

and:

```text
RECOGNITION
```

the easier it may be to preserve the difference between:

```text
what appeared
```

and:

```text
what was later inferred
```

---

# The First Rule

```text
CAPTURE FIRST.

EXPLAIN SECOND.
```

Do not suppress interpretation.

Simply place it in a different field.

---

# Minimum Capture

If there is time for nothing else, record:

```text

________________________________

________________________________

________________________________

________________________________
```

# Minimum Claim

After the initial capture, ask:

```text
What is the smallest statement
that preserves what happened
without assuming why?
```

Example:

```text
Experience:

"An old skill suddenly came back."
```

Minimum claim:

```text
"A skill that had not been
functionally accessible became
accessible again."
```

Then preserve stronger interpretations separately.

# Never Overwrite the First Capture

Later understanding may improve dramatically.

Do not silently rewrite:

```text
CAPTURE v1
```

into:

```text
CAPTURE v5
```

Instead preserve:

```text
v1
 ↓
v2
 ↓
v3
```

with:

```text
what changed

why it changed

which evidence appeared
```

Revision history is part of the observation.


Do not silently merge them.

# Working Summary

`capture.md` is the intake layer for the entire Triadic Re-Ignition module.

Its first responsibility is:

```text
DO NOT LOSE
THE ORIGINAL EVENT
```

The working sequence is:

```text
EVENT
  ↓
FIRST SIGNAL
  ↓
RESONANCE?
  ↓
RECOGNITION
  ↓
FIRST FORM
  ↓
INTERPRETATION
```

Then the capture expands:

```text
LOCATE THE OBSERVER
        ↓
ANCHOR TIME
        ↓
COMPARE EARLIER ACCESS
        ↓
MAP DORMANCY
        ↓
FIND INVARIANTS
        ↓
IDENTIFY DIFFERENCES
        ↓
PRESERVE PARADOX
        ↓
GENERATE ALTERNATIVES
        ↓
AUDIT DRIFT
        ↓
WRITE MINIMUM CLAIM
        ↓
DEFINE DISCRIMINATOR
        ↓
TEST
        ↓
REVISIT
```

The deepest discipline is simple:

> **Do not make the first flash carry the weight of the final theory.  
> Preserve what happened.  
> Preserve what you thought it meant.  
> Preserve the difference between those two things.  
> Preserve what changed later.  
> And preserve enough unknown that the evidence still has somewhere to go.**

In its smallest form:

```text
CAPTURE THE CLICK.

CAPTURE THE STORY
THAT FOLLOWED.

DO NOT CONFUSE THEM.
```

That gives everything downstream something clean to work with.

**Before Re-Ignition becomes a model, `capture.md` makes sure it remains an observation.**


Main writing choice: I made capture.md the pre-experimental intake layer, sitting upstream of the Observation Protocol. It preserves both the flash and the story that followed, plus version history, so later Drift analysis can actually trace where interpretation changed instead of trying to reconstruct that boundary after the fact.
