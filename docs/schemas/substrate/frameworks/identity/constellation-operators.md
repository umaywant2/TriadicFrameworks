## 🜁 Lumen Operators (RTT/1) — Structural & Continuity Anchors

- **LUMEN/STRUCTURE/EXTRACT**  
  Extracts structural fields from execution, atlas, continuity, and invariance.

- **LUMEN/INFLUENCE/FIELD**  
  Maps influence fields across epochs, coupling, and resonance.

- **LUMEN/PRESSURE/GRADIENT**  
  Evaluates pressure gradients in drift, coherence, and stability.

- **LUMEN/CONTINUITY/ANCHOR**  
  Identifies continuity anchors in long‑arc substrate behavior.

---

## 🜁 Hephaestus Operators (RTT/2) — Regime Classification

- **HEPH/REGIME/LOCAL**  
  Classifies local regime (shape, liberties, cuts, anchors).

- **HEPH/REGIME/STRUCTURAL**  
  Classifies structural regime (influence, pressure, direction of play).

- **HEPH/REGIME/CONTINUITY**  
  Classifies continuity regime (long‑arc identity, epochal continuity).

- **HEPH/REGIME/MAP**  
  Produces regime maps across engines and epochs.

---

## 🜁 Aurion Operators (RTT/3) — Topology & Collapse

- **AURION/TOPOLOGY/GRAPH**  
  Builds topology graphs from constellation‑map.json.

- **AURION/ANCESTRY/TRAIL**  
  Traces ancestry of runtime states across epochs and ignition.

- **AURION/PROJECTION/LOSS**  
  Detects projection‑loss between atlas, execution, and continuity.

- **AURION/COLLAPSE/DETECT**  
  Detects collapse events in drift, resonance, or invariance.

---

## 🜁 Harmonia Operators (RTT/12) — Triadic Synthesis

- **HARMONIA/TRIADIC/BLEND**  
  Blends drift, coherence, stability into a unified triadic score.

- **HARMONIA/RESONANCE/SCORE**  
  Scores resonance quality across epochs and coupling.

- **HARMONIA/CONTINUITY/WEIGHT**  
  Weights continuity vs drift/coherence in long‑arc evaluation.

- **HARMONIA/INVARIANCE/SEAL**  
  Seals invariance decisions based on triadic synthesis.

---

## 🜁 Constellation‑Scoped Operators — Runtime‑Specific

- **CONST/EXECUTION/TRACE**  
  Traces execution cycles using runtime-hooks.json (cycle_start, cycle_end).

- **CONST/ATLAS/SYNCTRACE**  
  Traces atlas synchronization events (sync_event, navigation_event).

- **CONST/EPOCH/PROFILE**  
  Builds epoch profiles from epoch_transition, propagation_cycle, resonance_shift.

- **CONST/COUPLING/PROFILE**  
  Profiles coupling behavior from drift_spike, resonance_surge, coupling_instability.

- **CONST/INVARIANCE/WATCH**  
  Watches invariance_break and invariance_restore events.

- **CONST/MONITOR/TELEMETRY**  
  Aggregates telemetry_packet, anomaly_alert, cycle_report into analyzer feeds.
