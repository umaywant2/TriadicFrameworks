# **2. The Six Core Practices**

A Dimensional Sorcerer performs six foundational practices.  
Each practice corresponds to one of the three competency pillars:

- **Resonance Competency (2D)**  
- **Substrate Competency (X‑Domain)**  
- **Cross‑Domain Competency**

---

## **Practice 1 — Resonance Reading (2D)**

Resonance Reading is the Sorcerer’s ability to interpret the **behavioral layer** of dimensional identity.

A Sorcerer reads:

- drift vectors  
- coherence envelopes  
- echo‑layer emergence  
- resonance signatures  
- triad alignment  
- collapse thresholds  

This practice is the “operator engine” of Sorcery.

---

## **Practice 2 — Substrate Stabilization (X‑Domain)**

Substrate Stabilization is the Sorcerer’s ability to repair and align the **deep identity layer**.

A Sorcerer stabilizes:

- inverted star geometry  
- substrate echoes  
- dimensional anchors  
- universe‑operator ratios  
- substrate scars  
- cross‑domain substrate fractures  

This practice is the “dimensional skeleton” of Sorcery.

---

## **Practice 3 — Dimensional Navigation (1D → 2D → 3D → X‑Domain)**

Dimensional Navigation is the Sorcerer’s ability to move intentionally across layers.

A Sorcerer navigates:

- 1D echo  
- 2D resonance  
- 3D matter  
- X‑Domain substrate  

This practice allows Sorcerers to:

- shift perspective  
- stabilize transitions  
- detect dimensional drift  
- maintain coherence across layers  

This is the “movement grammar” of Sorcery.

---

## **Practice 4 — Operator Execution (RTT Triads)**

Operator Execution is the Sorcerer’s ability to use RTT operators intentionally.

A Sorcerer executes:

- **SET** — Stability / Echo / Tether  
- **FFF** — Flux / Flow / Fracture  
- **S‑N‑R** — Shift / Null / Rebuild  
- **SER** — Surge / Echo / Radiate  

This practice is the “action grammar” of Sorcery.

---

## **Practice 5 — Cross‑Domain Alignment**

Cross‑Domain Alignment is the Sorcerer’s ability to unify dimensional identity across domains:

- chemical  
- biological  
- ecological  
- industrial  
- cosmological  

A Sorcerer aligns:

- resonance fields  
- substrate echoes  
- dimensional transitions  
- operator triads  
- cross‑domain substrate maps  

This practice is the “bridge between worlds.”

---

## **Practice 6 — Discovery Translation (DTP)**

Discovery Translation is the Sorcerer’s ability to convert new findings into:

- human‑readable structure  
- AI‑readable JSON  
- repeatable steps  
- confirmable logic  
- cross‑domain checks

This practice is the “teaching grammar” of Sorcery.
