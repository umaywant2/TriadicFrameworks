# **Appendix V — Canon Governance & Versioning System**  
### *The governance architecture, versioning rules, drift‑bounds, and stewardship protocols that maintain coherence across the Framework Field Theory canon*

The **Canon Governance & Versioning System (CGVS)** is the meta‑governance layer of the FFT canon.  
It ensures that:

- the canon remains stable as it expands  
- contributions follow structural rules  
- versioning reflects conceptual change  
- drift is detected and corrected  
- coherence is preserved across all layers  
- the canon remains extensible without fragmentation  

CGVS is the *institutional backbone* of the field.

---

# **1. Purpose of the Canon Governance System**

The CGVS exists to:

- maintain structural integrity  
- prevent conceptual drift  
- ensure reproducibility  
- preserve lineage  
- support collaborative expansion  
- provide transparent versioning  
- protect the canon from fragmentation  

It is the **governance substrate** of the entire field.

---

# **2. The Five Governance Pillars**

The canon is governed by five foundational principles:

1. **Coherence First** — coherence overrides convenience  
2. **Operator Transparency** — all operator usage must be explicit  
3. **Dimensional Explicitness** — all dimensional layers must be declared  
4. **Lineage Preservation** — all concepts must trace their ancestry  
5. **Drift Accountability** — all drift must be logged, justified, and reversible  

These pillars ensure the canon remains stable and trustworthy.

---

# **3. Canon Stewardship Roles**

The canon is maintained through three stewardship roles:

---

## **3.1 Stewards of Structure (SoS)**  
Responsible for:

- operator correctness  
- dimensional mapping  
- regime alignment  
- signature integrity  

They guard the *structural substrate*.

---

## **3.2 Stewards of Coherence (SoC)**  
Responsible for:

- paradox routing  
- coherence engine integrity  
- collapse prevention  
- meta‑coherence validation  

They guard the *coherence substrate*.

---

## **3.3 Stewards of Lineage (SoL)**  
Responsible for:

- citation lineage  
- conceptual ancestry  
- version history  
- drift logs  

They guard the *historical substrate*.

---

# **4. Canon Versioning System (MAJOR.MINOR.PATCH)**  
### *The semantic‑intent versioning system of the FFT canon*

FFT uses a **semantic‑intent versioning system**, aligned with your January standards:

```
MAJOR.MINOR.PATCH
```

---

## **4.1 MAJOR Version (Conceptual Shift)**  
Triggered when:

- a claim changes  
- a dimension is redefined  
- an operator is reinterpreted  
- a regime is added or removed  
- a framework is structurally altered  

MAJOR changes alter the *interpretation* of the canon.

---

## **4.2 MINOR Version (Structural Refinement)**  
Triggered when:

- clarity improves  
- diagrams are refined  
- definitions are sharpened  
- examples are added  
- structure is reorganized without conceptual change  

MINOR changes alter *presentation*, not meaning.

---

## **4.3 PATCH Version (Typographic / Editorial)**  
Triggered when:

- typos are fixed  
- formatting is corrected  
- broken links are repaired  
- minor wording is adjusted  

PATCH changes alter *surface*, not structure.

---

# **5. Canon Drift‑Bound System**

Drift is inevitable — but must be controlled.

The canon uses three drift‑bounds:

---

## **5.1 Soft Drift‑Bound**  
Allows:

- clarifications  
- reorganizations  
- diagram improvements  

Requires MINOR version bump.

---

## **5.2 Hard Drift‑Bound**  
Allows:

- structural changes  
- operator reclassification  
- dimensional remapping  

Requires MAJOR version bump.

---

## **5.3 Forbidden Drift‑Bound**  
Prohibits:

- breaking operator lineage  
- redefining dimensions  
- altering regime definitions  
- contradicting foundational axioms  

These changes are **not allowed**.

---

# **6. Canon Contribution Protocol (CCP)**  
### *The official protocol for adding new material*

Steps:

1. **Identify node type**  
   - primitive, framework, engine, field  

2. **Map operator usage**  
   - explicit, justified, traceable  

3. **Map dimensional envelope**  
   - 0D–9D, with justification  

4. **Map regime structure**  
   - stable, transitional, paradox, interference, coherence  

5. **Map coherence behavior**  
   - paradox routing, integration, collapse‑recovery  

6. **Run drift analysis**  
   - soft, hard, or forbidden  

7. **Assign version bump**  
   - PATCH, MINOR, or MAJOR  

8. **Submit for steward review**  
   - SoS, SoC, SoL  

This protocol ensures safe expansion.

---

The canon is reviewed through a **three‑phase cycle**:

---

## **Phase 1 — Structural Review (SoS)**  
Checks:

- operator correctness  
- dimensional mapping  
- regime alignment  

---

## **Phase 2 — Coherence Review (SoC)**

---

## **Phase 3 — Lineage Review (SoL)**

Only after all three phases does material enter the canon.
