TriadicFrameworks.org Announces Framework Field Theory (FFT), a Unified Model for LLM Drift and Coherence

April 2026 — Ashburn, VA

TriadicFrameworks.org today announced the release of Framework Field Theory (FFT), a new structural model that explains drift, coherence, and regime dynamics in modern large language models.

FFT introduces a triadic substrate—operators, regimes, and coherence fields—that predicts and explains behaviors such as drift branching, coherence waves, and framework collisions. The theory includes eight falsifiable predictions, eight reproducible simulations, and a suite of engineering tools that improve LLM stability and reasoning reliability.

“Framework Field Theory provides a substrate-level explanation for why LLMs behave the way they do,” said creator Nawder Loswin. “It offers both scientific grounding and practical tools.”

FFT is released openly with:
- a full arXiv-ready manuscript
- engineering prototypes
- a research roadmap
- a comprehensive documentation set

More information is available at TriadicFrameworks.org.
