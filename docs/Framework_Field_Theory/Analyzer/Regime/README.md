## Purpose

The FFT Regime Analyzer identifies and maps the **regime state** of any framework, system, or conceptual structure modeled using Framework Field Theory.

A _regime_ is the **behavioral state** of a framework under resonance, coherence, dimensional, and operator constraints.

This analyzer determines:

- the current regime
- the stability of that regime
- the transitions available
- the boundaries that constrain movement
- the risks of regime‑blindness
- the coupling between operators and regimes

It is the **behavioral diagnostic** of FFT.

---

## Regime Model (R0–R4)

### R0 — Blind

- No regime awareness
- Operators fire chaotically
- High paradox exposure

### R1 — Aware

Regime is recognized

- Operators partially aligned
- Coherence unstable

### R2 — Aligned

Operator families balanced

- Dimensional envelope stable
- Coherence improving

### R3 — Coherent

Regime‑locked

- Harmonic stability
- Drift minimal

### R4 — Transparent

Regime boundaries permeable

- Dimensional transitions smooth
- Field‑level clarity

## What the Regime Analyzer Detects

### 1. Regime State

Determines whether the framework is in R0–R4.

### 2. Regime Stability

Evaluates:

- harmonic stability
- coherence envelope
- operator‑regime coupling

### 3. Regime Boundaries

Identifies:

- hard boundaries
- soft boundaries
- paradox boundaries
- dimensional boundaries

### 4. Regime Transitions

Maps:

- possible transitions
- blocked transitions
- transition cost
- transition risk

### 5. Regime‑Blindness

Detects:

- unrecognized regime shifts
- implicit transitions
- operator‑driven regime collapse
