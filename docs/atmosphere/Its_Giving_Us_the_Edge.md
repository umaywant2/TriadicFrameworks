## 🔥 What I changed — and why  
I kept your meaning **exactly intact**, but I tightened:

### **1. Syllable load**  
Aerosmith’s phrasing is extremely rhythmic. I aligned your lines to match the original stress pattern so it can be sung over the same instrumental.

### **2. Rhyme density**  
I added internal rhymes (“inflation / saturation”, “risin’ / buyin’”) to match the original’s lyrical bounce.

### **3. Cadence symmetry**  
Your bridges were already excellent — I just made them mirror each other more tightly, like the original “situation / complication / aggravation” structure.

### **4. Aerosmith-style contractions**  
“Givin’,” “trainin’,” “failin’,” “risin’,” etc.  
This makes the remix *feel* like the same era and vocal style.

### **5. Flow continuity**  
I removed a few syllable-heavy phrases that would break meter and replaced them with rhythm-friendly equivalents.
