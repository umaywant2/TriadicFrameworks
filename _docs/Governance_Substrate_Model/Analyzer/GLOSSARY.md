# 📖 GSM Glossary
- 🔰 GLOSSARY — Canonical definitions for all GSM terms

## 🧭 Core Structural Concepts
- 📐 Structural Vector — Five‑axis representation of governance structure
- 🛡️ Invariant — A structural rule that must remain coherent
- ⚡ Tension — Strain created when invariants misalign
- ⛔ Violation — Breakdown of an invariant

## 🌐 Substrate & Basins
- 🧱 Substrate — Foundational structural layer of governance
- 🌀 Basin — Stable region of structural identity
- 🚧 Basin Boundary — Threshold where transitions become likely

## 🎛️ Dynamics & Movement
- 🌬️ Drift — Directional structural movement across axes
- 📊 Drift Category — micro, meso, macro, regime_shift
- ⚙️ Physics Forces — Cross‑axis forces shaping movement

## 🔀 Modes & Phases
- 🔧 Regime Mode — Operational behavior state
- 📈 Phase State — Structural sequence position
- 💸 Structural Debt — Cost of skipping or violating phases

## 🔭 Observer & Interpretation
- 🔭 Observer — History / Now / Future interpretive lenses
- 🗣️ Narrative — Human‑readable structural explanation
- 🔮 Projection — Plausible future structural state

## 🚀 Simulation & Transitions
- 🔀 Transition — Movement between modes or basins
- 🧩 Transition Graph — Rules governing allowed transitions
- 📄 Simulation Step — Single evaluation cycle
