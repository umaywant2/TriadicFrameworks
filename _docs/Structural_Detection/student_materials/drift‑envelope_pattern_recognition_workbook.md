# HOW TO USE THIS WORKBOOK

For each exercise:

1. Identify the **pattern family** (A/B/C/D/O/I)  
2. Identify the **pattern ID** (e.g., A1, C3, D2)  
3. Identify the **drift profile**  
4. Identify the **envelope geometry**  
5. Identify the **deformation class**  
6. Identify the **continuity behavior**  
7. Identify the **regime alignment**  
8. Identify the **coherence‑break type**  
9. Produce a **PATTERN_PACKET**  

This workbook is designed for **students**, but structured at **instructor‑grade clarity**.
