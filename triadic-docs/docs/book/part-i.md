# Part I — Foundational Theory

Part I introduces the foundational principles of Resonance‑Time Theory (RTT).  
It establishes the conceptual base upon which all other modules are built.

---

## Core Concepts

### **Resonance‑Time Theory (RTT)**
RTT describes how structural patterns evolve, stabilize, and interact across time.

### **Structural Resonance**
The tendency of patterns to reinforce or amplify each other within a structural field.

### **Temporal Logic**
The rules governing how structures behave across time intervals.

---

## Purpose of Part I

Part I exists to:

- define the theoretical foundation of RTT  
- establish the rules of structural resonance  
- introduce temporal logic  
- prepare readers for analyzer layers and operators
