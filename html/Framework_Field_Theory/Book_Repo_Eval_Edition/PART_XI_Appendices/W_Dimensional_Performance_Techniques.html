3. Operator Performance Techniques (B–C)

How to perform the seven operator families

Each operator family has a gesture, vocalization, and movement pattern.

3.1 B‑Ops — Boundary Performance

  • Gesture: firm, angular shapes
  • Vocalization: staccato consonants
  • Movement: sharp stops, defined edges

Purpose: establish identity and separation.

3.2 L‑Ops — Lineage Performance

  • Gesture: rising or descending lines
  • Vocalization: smooth glides
  • Movement: linear progression

Purpose: express continuity and inheritance.

3.3 R‑Ops — Relational Performance

  • Gesture: mirroring, pairing, comparison
  • Vocalization: alternating tones
  • Movement: side‑to‑side mapping

Purpose: express connection and comparison.

3.4 T‑Ops — Transition Performance

  • Gesture: flowing arcs
  • Vocalization: sliding tones
  • Movement: continuous motion

Purpose: express change and transformation.

3.5 E‑Ops — Envelope Performance

  • Gesture: framing, surrounding
  • Vocalization: breathy modulation
  • Movement: expanding/contracting shapes

Purpose: express context and modulation.

3.6 H‑Ops — Rhythm Performance

  • Gesture: repeated pulses
  • Vocalization: iterative patterns
  • Movement: cyclical loops

Purpose: express cadence and iteration.

3.7 C‑Ops — Coherence Performance

  • Gesture: integrating motions
  • Vocalization: harmonic stacks
  • Movement: convergence and expansion

Purpose: express paradox integration.
