The canonical framework for evaluating operator stability, paradox behavior, dimensional drift, and coherence response under stress

1. The Five Stress Conditions

Every operator is tested under five canonical stress conditions:

  1. Contradiction Stress — direct logical or structural conflict
  2. Overload Stress — too much input, complexity, or demand
  3. Dimensional Drift Stress — forced upward or downward movement
  4. Interference Stress — collision with another operator
  5. Paradox Saturation Stress — paradox exceeds coherence capacity

Each condition reveals different failure modes and evolution triggers.
