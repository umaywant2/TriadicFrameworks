🧭 Structural Notes

Facilities Root

Contains canonical, cross‑domain substrate artifacts: - lifecycle - scoring - propagation - intervention - modernization timing - strategy - glossary

These files are referenced, not duplicated, by domain extensions.

RTT‑AGERI/

The first fully instantiated Facilities domain extension.

It: - Inherits Facilities substrate definitions - Extends scoring, standards, and governance - Provides city‑ and resident‑facing materials - Does not redefine shared Facilities concepts

Future Facilities domains will follow this same pattern.
