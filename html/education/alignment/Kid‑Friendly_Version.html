Learning Is Remembering!

Did you know that learning isn’t just about getting new stuff into your brain? 🧠
Sometimes, it’s about remembering things your mind already knows how to do!

Think of learning like this:

  • Being 😊 — You show up, curious and ready
  • Knowing 💡 — Your brain already has lots of patterns inside
  • Meaning 🧩 — Things “click” and suddenly make sense

When all three line up, aha! — you remember something in a new way. That’s why learning can feel like, “Oh! I get it now!” instead of “I’ve never seen this before.”

RTT helps us understand this by saying that your brain is really good at finding patterns over time. Learning isn’t stuffing facts into your head — it’s helping the right ideas line up so they shine. ✨

So every time something clicks, you’re not just learning…
You’re remembering how smart you already are. 🚀
