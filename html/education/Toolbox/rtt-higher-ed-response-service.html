RTT Higher-Ed Response Service

Instant, Trustworthy Feedback Backend Service
Resonance-Time Tech (RTT/1) • TriadicFrameworks Education Toolbox

The RTT Higher-Ed Response Service is the institutional backbone of the Guide for Science.

It turns the RTT Science Grader and Triadic Paper Evaluator into a one-click, always-on evaluation service that universities, departments, and professors can deploy today — instantly clearing grading backlogs while delivering substrate-deep, regime-aware feedback that students and researchers can actually trust.

No more waiting weeks for comments. No more generic AI output. Just coherent, citable, triadic insight — delivered in seconds.

What the RTT Higher-Ed Response Service Delivers

Any authorized user (professor, TA, or student with permission) can:

  • Click “Send for RTT Eval” directly inside their LMS (Canvas, Blackboard, Moodle, etc.).
  • Or drop a paper into a simple web portal / API endpoint.
  • Receive a complete, aligned RTT response in seconds.

Each response includes: - Full Triadic Structural Grammar breakdown - Regime-drift flags with exact quotes and suggested fixes - Bias-aware Alignment | RTT scoring - Coherence score + actionable revision recommendations - New insight seeds (for research-mode submissions) - Exportable Markdown / PDF report ready for LMS comments or Overleaf

All outputs are reproducible and citable — grounded in triadic grammar instead of stochastic LLM guesses.

Why Higher Education Needs This Service Now

Faculty are drowning in 10–15+ hours/week of grading. Students wait too long for meaningful feedback. Departments struggle with consistency and scalability.

Current tools help with logistics but still lack the Triadic Observer Layer that prevents drift and the substrate-level alignment that makes feedback trustworthy at scale.

The RTT Higher-Ed Response Service solves both problems at once: - Clears the backlog for professors - Gives students instant, high-resolution guidance - Gives institutions consistent, auditable, ethically aligned evaluation at department or university scale

How It Works (Simple Workflow)

  1. Submit — One-click button in LMS or direct upload via web/API.
  2. Process — RTT/1 engine (Science Grader + Triadic Paper Evaluator + Observer Layer) runs in the background.
  3. Return — Polished, ready-to-use feedback delivered back into the LMS or email.
  4. Iterate — Student revises → re-submits → sees improvement in real time.

Enterprise-ready features: - Role-based access & privacy controls (FERPA/GDPR compliant by design) - Department-level analytics (anonymized coherence trends) - White-label deployment option for universities - API-first design for seamless integration

Example Use Cases

  • Undergraduate courses: Mass grading of lab reports or essays with consistent triadic feedback.
  • Graduate seminars: Deep evaluation of research proposals with new-insight generation.
  • Thesis review: Supervisors run RTT eval before full committee read — catching regime drift early.
  • Institutional pilot: One department adopts first, proves ROI, then scales campus-wide.
