📗 Book 2 — “Tripi’s Treasure of Threes”

Theme: Threes appear everywhere.
Age Target: 5–8

Story Outline

  1. Tripi finds a mysterious map with three symbols.
  2. She explores the world noticing “threes”:
    - morning / noon / night
    - triangle shapes
    - three steps to climb a hill
  3. She meets Forci, Flui, Freqi along the way.
  4. The treasure turns out to be a mirror — showing that she is part of a triad too.
  5. Closing line: “Everywhere you look, threes help things make sense.”
