📄 III. Publisher‑Ready Manuscript Templates

These are the formats publishers expect.

Template A — Picture Book Manuscript (Standard)

TITLE: [Book Title]
AUTHOR: [Your Name]
ILLUSTRATOR: [Illustrator Name]

PAGE 1–2 (Spread)
[Text here — 1–2 lines max]

Illustration Notes:
- Describe scene
- Character positions
- Mood / color

PAGE 3–4 (Spread)
[Text here]

Illustration Notes:
...

Rules:
- One spread per section
- Minimal text
- Clear illustration notes
- Triadic motifs noted where needed

Template B — Character‑Driven Manuscript

PAGE 1
Nawderia sits under the triad tree.

Illustration: Warm sunset, triad leaves glowing.

PAGE 2
“Forci, Flui, Freqi — come say hello!”

Illustration: Characters entering from three directions.
