📘 Book 1 — “Nawderia and the Three Little Forces”

Theme: The world is made of gentle pushes, flows, and hums.
Age Target: 4–7

Story Outline

  1. Opening: Great‑Aunt Nawderia sits under a glowing triad tree.
  2. She introduces three tiny friends: Forci, Flui, Freqi.
  3. Each one demonstrates their “way of being”:
    - Forci nudges a pebble.
    - Flui wiggles through grass.
    - Freqi hums and makes leaves dance.
  4. A small problem appears: a stuck wagon.
  5. Each character tries their method:
    - Forci pushes.
    - Flui wiggles the wheels free.
    - Freqi vibrates the mud loose.
  6. Together, they solve it.
  7. Nawderia smiles: “Everything works better in threes.”
  8. Closing illustration: the triad symbol glowing softly.

Concept Seeds

  • Force, flow, frequency
  • Collaboration
  • Triads as natural patterns
