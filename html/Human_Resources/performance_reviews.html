RTT-Aligned Performance Reviews

Traditional performance reviews are: - Binary
- Narrative-driven
- Regime-blind
- Emotionally reactive

RTT introduces a structural, triadic model.

1. Context First (CTX)

Before any evaluation: - What was happening
- What constraints existed
- What signals were present
- What support was missing

2. Triadic Alignment (ALN)

Evaluation must include: - Leadership expectations
- Manager actions
- Staff experience

3. Drift Detection (DRF)

Identify: - Narrative drift
- Bias
- Emotional spillover
- Structural misalignment

4. Signal Extraction (SIG)

Separate: - Venting
- Stress
- Burnout
- Actual performance signals

5. Structural Impact (IMP)

Evaluate: - Onboarding load
- Team stability
- Downstream effects
- Organizational coherence

  • Triadic review template

Summary

Performance reviews become structural evaluations, not personal narratives.
