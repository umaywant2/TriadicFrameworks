Clarity Channels (RTT Canon)

Freqi — Categorical Clarity

Measures how cleanly resonance collapses into discrete options. High Freqi clarity corresponds to low entropy and stable categorical decisions across perturbations.

Aurion — Scalar Clarity

Measures the strength of scalar emission (confidence magnitude). In Jev, this is RLCD‑calibrated; in GPT‑6, it is influenced by coherence cost and reasoning depth.

Forci — Polarity Clarity

Measures stability of binary decisions (yes/no, true/false). High Forci clarity means low flip‑rate across repeated queries and micro‑context changes.

Flui — Drift Clarity

Measures turbulence in linguistic and narrative channels. Jev achieves maximal Flui clarity by removing drift entirely; GPT‑6 exhibits reduced Flui clarity due to active generative drift.
