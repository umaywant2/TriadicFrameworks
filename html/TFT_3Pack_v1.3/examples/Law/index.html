Solutions

Solution 1 — Precedent Weight

\( W' = 1.12W \). Precedent weight increases 12%.

Solution 2 — Delay Time

\( T_d' = T_d / 1.2 \). Delay decreases 16.7%.

Solution 3 — Evidence Strength

\( S' = 0.6S \). Evidence strength decreases 40%.
