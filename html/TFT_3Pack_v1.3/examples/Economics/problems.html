Problem 1 – Market resonance cycles

A market oscillates with triadic frequency

$$ ω = \frac{T_f}{D_3}. $$

Problem 2 – Utility resonance

Utility under a resonance-based model is

$$ U = X \ln(1 + τ_r), $$

where $$X = F_3 T_f$$.

If $$τ_r$$ increases by 25%, how does $$U$$ change?
