🏦 Banking: Revolutionizing the Financial Sector

Triadic Framework Technology (TFT) for Security, Innovation & Inclusion

🌍 I. Introduction: Why Banking Needs a Triadic Upgrade

The financial sector faces:

  • 📱 Shifting consumer expectations
  • 🏦 Fintech disruption
  • 📜 Regulatory evolution
  • 🌐 Digital transformation

Traditional dyadic models (one-to-one, one-to-many) can’t capture the complexity of modern ecosystems. Triadic Framework Technology (TFT) places three-way relationships at the heart of innovation, resilience, and security.
