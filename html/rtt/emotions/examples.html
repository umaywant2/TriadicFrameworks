1. Coherence Example

Compassion Restoring Stability

Emotion: Compassion
Triad: (+1, +1, +1)
Equation:

$$C = k_c \cdot \sigma \cdot \frac{R}{1 + \Delta O}$$

Given:
- $$k_c = 1.2$$
- $$\sigma = 0.8$$
- $$R = 0.9$$
- $$\Delta O = 0.2$$
- $$D = 0.4$$

Compute:

$$C = 1.2 \cdot 0.8 \cdot \frac{0.9}{1.2} = 0.72$$

Interpretation:
- $$C = 0.72 > D = 0.4$$
- Drift decreases
- System moves toward coherence
