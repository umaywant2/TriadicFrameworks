🟢 Resonance Truth

RTT/1 · Semantic Resonance · Operational Truth · Coherence · Drift · Paradox · SET Triad

Truth is not a static fact.

In Resonance-Time Theory (RTT), a truth claim is treated as a structured object whose operational meaning depends on:

  • what is being claimed
  • what kind of Truth is involved
  • what Evidence exists
  • where that Evidence came from
  • which Regime governs interpretation
  • what remains coherent
  • what has Drifted
  • what remains unresolved
  • what may presently be accepted
  • what must later be Revalidated

The Resonance Truth module therefore operates on two connected planes:

The governing idea is:

Operational Truth is not merely a value. It is a qualified relationship embedded in Evidence, Regime, Lineage, and lifecycle.

3. Core RTT Concepts

Truth moves among literal, interpretive, generalized, and radiative modes.

Resonance describes how Claims propagate, align, destabilize, broaden, or transform through interpretive systems.

9. Truth-State Principle

The Truth Service does not attempt to produce:

universal truth = true

Instead it produces a bounded operational result.

Conceptually:

Truth State:
  operationally_accepted

domain result:
  domain-native value

permitted uses:
  [...]

prohibited uses:
  [...]

limitations:
  [...]

Revalidation:
  [...]

This allows the system to say:

This is the result presently supported for this use under this Regime.

That is intentionally weaker than omniscience and stronger than an unqualified guess.

10. Negative Results Are First-Class

A successful Evaluation may produce a negative domain result.

Examples include:

DNS:
  absent

Authorization:
  deny

Transaction:
  rolled back

Intent:
  converged = false

These may still be:

operationally_accepted

because the service successfully established the negative result.

Therefore:

Truth qualification concerns whether the answer is structurally supported, not whether the answer is positive.

11. Indeterminate Is a Valid Result

The Truth Service must be capable of saying:

  null

when available Evidence cannot support one operational answer.

This is not necessarily service failure.

It may be the correct bounded result.

The initial NTP fixtures explicitly test this behavior.

25. Evaluation Discipline

The service should preserve several reasoning rules.

Preserve Dissent

Do not delete inconvenient Evidence merely because it does not join the dominant cluster.

Preserve Gaps

Do not convert missing Evidence into positive absence without qualified coverage.

Preserve Lineage

Do not treat copied or derived Evidence as independent solely because it has another identifier.

Preserve History

Do not rewrite prior Truth States merely because a later Evaluation changes the current result.

Preserve Regime

Do not treat a result qualified for one use as universally qualified.

Preserve Null

Do not invent a value merely because a downstream consumer expects one.

26. Architecture Anti-Patterns

The operational Truth Service should resist:

majority = Truth

Source = Authority

valid signature = operational trust

identity = authorization

desired state = current state

committed = materialized

missing Evidence = absence

copied Evidence = corroboration

difference = Paradox

old accepted value = current accepted value

confidence scalar = universal sufficient explanation

These shortcuts erase the structure the module is designed to preserve.

33. Canonical Module Summary

The original Resonance Truth module begins with a semantic proposition:

Truth is not a static semantic object. It moves through resonance, Coherence, Drift, Paradox, and Regime.

The operational extension asks what happens when that idea must survive contact with real systems.

Those questions converge on one architectural lesson:

The useful unit of operational Truth is not an isolated value. It is a qualified relationship with visible Evidence and Lineage.

That relationship may change.

Its Evidence may age.

Its Regime may change.

Its Sources may disagree.

Its Lineage may reveal dependency.

Its current answer may become:

contested

degraded

expired

rejected

indeterminate

None of those cases require the system to pretend certainty.

That is the purpose of Resonance Truth.
