Triadic Operators

The Egyptian mythological system expresses a natural triadic structure through primordial emergence, solar‑cycle continuance, and underworld dissolution.

Nephthys Mourning, protection, boundary mediation

Structural Notes

  • Genesis is primordial + solar + artisanal, not ex nihilo
  • Continuance is cyclical + sovereign + restorative, tied to rebirth and rulership
  • Dissolution is chaos + desert + underworld, not moral evil
  • Liminality is judicial + underworld, emphasizing balance (Ma’at)
  • Egyptian cosmology is cyclical, with daily solar rebirth and underworld passage
