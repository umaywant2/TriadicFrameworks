Triadic Operators

The Greek mythological system expresses a natural triadic structure through primordial emergence, Olympian continuance, and chthonic dissolution.

Structural Notes

  • Genesis is primordial + emergent, not artisanal
  • Continuance is sovereign + civic + agricultural, emphasizing order and stability
  • Dissolution is chthonic + ecstatic, tied to death, madness, and abyssal containment
  • Liminality is threshold‑dominant, with multiple figures bridging realms
  • Greek cosmology is stratified, with clear vertical layering (Protogenoi → Olympians → Underworld)
