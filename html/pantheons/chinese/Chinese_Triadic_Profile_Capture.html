Chinese Triadic Profile Capture

(TriadicFrameworks Canon Module — Chinese Mythological System)

I. Genesis Operators (Creation / Emergence / Ordering)

Chinese genesis emphasizes cosmic separation, primordial beings, and ordering of Heaven and Earth.

  • Pangu — Primordial giant; genesis through cosmic separation of Heaven and Earth; world‑structuring.
  • Nüwa — Genesis through creation of humanity; repair of cosmic order; mother‑creator.
  • Fuxi — Genesis through civilization; invention of trigrams, writing, and societal structure.
  • Yuanshi Tianzun — Primordial Heavenly Lord; genesis through divine emanation and cosmic hierarchy.

Genesis Pattern:
Chinese genesis is cosmic + civilizational + reparative — creation emerges from separation, ordering, and restoration.

II. Continuance Operators (Maintenance / Harmony / Prosperity)

Continuance in Chinese myth is agriculture, governance, cycles, harmony, and protection.

  • Huangdi (Yellow Emperor) — Continuance through governance, medicine, technology, and cultural order.
  • Guanyin — Continuance through compassion, mercy, protection, and emotional harmony.
  • Shennong — Continuance through agriculture, herbal medicine, and sustenance.
  • Chenghuang (City Gods) — Continuance through civic protection, justice, and social stability.

Continuance Pattern:
Chinese continuance is harmonious + civilizational + protective — stability emerges from governance, compassion, and cycles.

III. Dissolution Operators (Chaos / Disorder / Punishment / Underworld)

Chinese dissolution is chaos, imbalance, punishment, demons, and underworld governance.

  • Gonggong — Dissolution through cosmic imbalance; floods, destruction, and conflict with Heaven.
  • Taowu — Dissolution through chaos, malevolence, and moral disorder.
  • King Yama (Yanluo Wang) — Dissolution through judgment, punishment, and underworld governance.
  • Huli Jing (malevolent form) — Dissolution through deception, imbalance, and spiritual corruption.

Dissolution Pattern:
Chinese dissolution is moral + cosmic + judicial — endings arise from imbalance, corruption, or violation of harmony.

IV. Liminal Operators (Threshold / Spirit‑World / Mediation)

Chinese liminality is spirit‑world navigation, immortals, boundary guardians, and cosmic mediation.

  • Zhong Kui — Liminal operator of demon‑slaying; guardian of thresholds and spiritual boundaries.
  • The Eight Immortals (Bāxiān) — Collective liminal operators; mediate between mortal world and spirit realms.
  • Meng Po — Liminal operator of memory erasure; threshold between life → reincarnation.
  • White Snake (Bai Suzhen) — Liminal operator of transformation, love, and boundary crossing.

Liminal Pattern:
Chinese liminality is spirit‑centric + transformative + judicial — thresholds define identity, reincarnation, and spiritual balance.

V. Structural Notes (Canon‑Aligned)

  • Genesis is cosmic separation + civilizational invention, not ex nihilo.
  • Continuance is harmonious + protective, emphasizing governance and compassion.
  • Dissolution is moral + cosmic, tied to imbalance and corruption.
  • Liminality is spirit‑world dominant, with strong emphasis on reincarnation and mediation.
  • Chinese cosmology is hierarchical + cyclical, with Heaven, Earth, and Underworld interlocking.
