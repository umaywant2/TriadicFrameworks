Capture Template — TriadicFrameworks Prompts

The Capture Template provides the canonical structure for capturing any system using RTT engines, operator grammar, structural identity, boundaries, commitments, drift signals, coherence anchors, substrate behavior, dimensional rails, and domain context.

This template defines the standard capture grammar for TriadicFrameworks.

Purpose

Use this template when you need to:

  • capture structure before analysis
  • declare identity, boundaries, commitments, and topology
  • surface early drift signals
  • identify coherence anchors
  • capture substrate behavior and dimensional context
  • map domain relevance
  • prepare material for RTT/1 → RTT∞ analysis
  • maintain structural neutrality during capture

Capture Template

1. Identity

Capture the identity of the system.

Prompt:
- Declare the identity of this system. What is its form, purpose, and boundary?

2. Structural Form

Capture the structure, commitments, and relational topology.

Prompt:
- Describe the structural form of this system (identity, boundary, commitment, topology).

3. Operators Present

Capture the operators already visible.

Prompt:
- Identify the operators present in this system (structural, drift, coherence, regime, dimensional, domain).

4. Early Drift Signals

Capture any initial drift indicators.

Prompt:
- Surface early drift signals across geometric, operational, temporal, conceptual, and domain layers.

5. Coherence Anchors

Capture what appears aligned.

Prompt:
- Identify coherence anchors (purpose, constraint, goal, continuity).

6. Substrate Context

Capture the substrate behavior and medium identity.

Prompt:
- Describe the substrate context (physical, cultural, cognitive, civic, biological, computational).

7. Dimensional Context

Capture dimensional rails and transitions.

Prompt:
- Identify dimensional rails and any visible transitions (rail, substrate‑transition, prime‑state, collapse, integration).

8. Domain Context

Capture domain relevance across the six canonical domains.

Prompt:
- Identify domain context (psychology, physics, economics, governance, AI/agents, biology).

9. Session Notes

Capture any additional context, anomalies, or structural observations.

Prompt:
- Record any additional notes relevant to structure, drift, coherence, substrate, dimensions, or domains.
