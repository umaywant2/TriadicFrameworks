TriadicFrameworks — Prompt Templates

The TriadicFrameworks Templates directory contains structured, reusable prompt templates for RTT engines, IPD‑12, stacks, modules, UI logic, and teaching workflows. Templates provide consistent scaffolding for capture, analysis, drift detection, coherence declaration, operator work, domain traversal, substrate analysis, teaching sessions, and research synthesis.

This page is the front door for all prompt templates.
