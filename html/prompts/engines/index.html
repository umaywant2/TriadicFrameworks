TriadicFrameworks — Engine Modules

The TriadicFrameworks engine modules provide structured prompt sets for all RTT engines and IPD‑12. Each engine module includes explainers, prompts, diagnostics, worksheets, posters, curriculum material, and cross‑links to the Docsbook and S3 Spine Visualizer.

Engine modules define the core behavior of the TriadicFrameworks canon.
They are used for structural analysis, drift detection, coherence restoration, teaching, and deep‑layer traversal.

This page is the front door for all engine‑level prompt modules.

Introduces advanced drift patterns, coherence restoration, and multi‑regime structural synthesis. Used for complex analysis and teaching.
