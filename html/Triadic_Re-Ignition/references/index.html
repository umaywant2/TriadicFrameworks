Triadic Re-Ignition: References

References preserve lineage, vocabulary, boundaries, and unresolved questions so exploration can continue without losing track of what came from where.

The References layer supports the rest of Triadic Re-Ignition.

Its purpose is not to provide another theory layer.

Instead, it maintains the structures needed to keep the module:

traceable

consistent

bounded

cross-referenceable

reviewable

and open to revision

The References layer contains four primary artifacts:

Terminology

What do our terms mean?

RTT Crosswalk
    ↓
How does Re-Ignition relate
to its reference foundation?

Spine Crosswalk
    ↓
How does Re-Ignition relate
to the broader structural architecture?

Open Questions
    ↓
What remains unresolved?

Together, these documents help prevent exploratory language from silently becoming canonical language.

Why Terminology Matters

A word may begin as an exploratory description and gradually become treated as an explanation.

For example:

"projection"

might begin as:

a model for how structure
becomes representation

and later drift into:

an established physical mechanism

without any intermediate evidence.

Terminology boundaries help prevent that transition.

The rule is:

Define the term at the claim level actually supported by the module.

2. RTT Crosswalk

File: ./RTT-crosswalk.md

The RTT Crosswalk maps Triadic Re-Ignition concepts to Resonance-Time Theory.

Its relationship is:

RTT
    ↓
reference foundation

Re-Ignition
    ↓
exploratory application

The crosswalk asks:

Where does terminology overlap?

Where does operator structure correspond?

Where is the resemblance only conceptual?

Where is formalization possible?

Where should no mapping exist?

RTT Boundary

The crosswalk does not make Re-Ignition part of RTT merely by identifying similarities.

The progression is:

shared vocabulary
       ↓
structural similarity
       ↓
operational correspondence
       ↓
mathematical correspondence
       ↓
mechanistic equivalence
       ↓
canonical identity

These levels should not be collapsed.

Most Re-Ignition mappings currently belong at the earlier levels.

Therefore:

similar structure
      ≠
same operator
      ≠
same mechanism
      ≠
canonical identity

Strong RTT Relationships

Some of the strongest areas for continued comparison include:

RESONANCE

TIME

COHERENCE

TRANSITION

STABILIZATION

REGIME

Candidate relationships also exist around:

activation

recognition thresholds

resonance-time gradients

S-N-R

local resonant behavior

Those mappings should become stronger only through operationalization and testing.

RTT No-Mapping Rule

An important result of a crosswalk can be:

UNMAPPED

Not every Re-Ignition concept needs an RTT equivalent.

Not every RTT operator needs a Re-Ignition application.

This protects both structures from operator inflation.

3. Spine Crosswalk

File: ./spine-crosswalk.md

The Spine Crosswalk maps Re-Ignition into the broader TriadicFrameworks structural reference architecture.

The working relationship is:

Triadic Re-Ignition
        ↓
exploratory module

TriadicFrameworks Spine
        ↓
structural reference

The crosswalk uses the Spine as a set of analytical lenses rather than as automatic authority for new claims.

Spine Overlay Questions

The crosswalk organizes Re-Ignition through seven analytical questions:

STRUCTURAL
What changed?

DRIFT
Where did interpretation
become stronger than support?

COHERENCE
What remains stable?

SUBSTRATE
What supports continuity?

DIMENSIONAL
Across what layers is
the relationship represented?

DOMAIN

CLARITY
What can actually be justified?

This is especially useful when a Re-Ignition observation begins crossing conceptual layers.

Crosswalk Versus Canon

A crosswalk means:

relationship identified

not:

canonical inclusion approved

The distinction should remain explicit:

CROSSWALK
    =
structural relationship map

CROSSWALK
    ≠
automatic canon

A module may remain useful indefinitely without requiring canonical promotion.

4. Open Questions

File: ./open-questions.md

The Open Questions registry preserves unresolved problems.

It asks:

What do we still not know?

Questions may concern:

Re-Ignition

resonance

recognition

observer state

form / formless

local time

resonance time

timeline projection

apparent multiplicity

paradox

continuity

substrate

domains

experiments

canon

An unresolved question is not considered a defect.

It is an explicit boundary of current understanding.

Questions Must Stay Open

A question can contain hidden assumptions.

For example:

"What changed during recognition?"

is relatively open.

But:

"What hidden mechanism restored
the dormant structure?"

already assumes:

hidden mechanism

persistent dormant structure

restoration

The References layer therefore applies drift control to questions themselves.

LOADED QUESTION
      ↓
IDENTIFY ASSUMPTIONS
      ↓
REMOVE UNSUPPORTED CLAIMS
      ↓
RESTORE OPEN QUESTION

Question Lifecycle

Open questions may evolve through:

OPEN
  ↓
PARTIALLY BOUNDED
  ↓
OPERATIONALIZED
  ↓
UNDER TEST
  ↓
PROVISIONALLY RESOLVED
  ↓
CLOSED

Not every question will reach the end.

Some may remain:

UNKNOWN

for a long time.

That is acceptable.

Source Versus Application

The References layer maintains another important distinction:

SOURCE DEFINITION
        ≠
MODULE APPLICATION

For example:

RTT term
      ↓
RTT source meaning

may be used within:

Re-Ignition analysis

without changing the RTT source meaning.

If Re-Ignition uses a term differently, the difference should be stated explicitly.

Canonical Source Rule

When a source reference and local summary differ:

CANONICAL SOURCE
       ↓
takes precedence
for source-framework meaning

Then:

LOCAL CROSSWALK
       ↓
should be revised

A crosswalk exists to track the source.

The source should not be rewritten to fit the crosswalk.

ORIGINAL DEFINITION
        ↓

restore definition

or:

define a new term

The References layer should never become a mechanism for manufacturing authority.

Its job is almost the opposite.

It tells the reader:

what came from where

what a term means

what is only analogous

what remains exploratory

what has not been established

and what still needs to be tested

The guiding discipline is:

Preserve the source.
Define the language.
Map the relationship.
Mark the boundary.
Keep unanswered questions visible.

Or in the shortest form:

REFERENCE
    ≠
AUTHORITY TRANSFER

CROSSWALK
    ≠
EQUIVALENCE

QUESTION
    ≠
HIDDEN ANSWER

The References layer exists so Triadic Re-Ignition can continue exploring without forgetting which parts are established, which parts are borrowed, which parts are newly proposed, and which parts remain genuinely unknown.

That gives references/ a clean internal logic: Terminology = meaning, RTT Crosswalk = lineage, Spine Crosswalk = structural relationship, Open Questions = uncertainty. It also creates a nice handoff to experiments/, since anything that moves beyond structural analogy now has somewhere obvious to go: operationalize it and test it.
