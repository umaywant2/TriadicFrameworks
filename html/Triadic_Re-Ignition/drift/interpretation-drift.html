Interpretation Drift

Interpretation begins when observation acquires meaning. Drift begins when the growing meaning becomes difficult to distinguish from the observation that started it.

Interpretation Drift is a condition in which an observation gradually accumulates:

meaning

assumptions

relationships

mechanisms

or ontological claims

without those additions remaining clearly distinguishable from the original observation.

A common progression is:

OBSERVATION
    ↓
RELATIONSHIP
    ↓
INTERPRETATION
    ↓
HYPOTHESIS
    ↓
MECHANISM
    ↓
ONTOLOGY

This progression is not inherently a problem.

Research requires interpretation.

The problem occurs when:

movement between levels
becomes invisible

and a later claim begins to appear as though it had been present in the original observation.

Core Proposition

Suppose the initial observation is:

A appears related to B.

A reasonable interpretation might be:

A and B may share
relationship R.

A hypothesis might become:

R explains the relationship
between A and B.

A mechanism claim might become:

M produces R.

An ontological claim might become:

M describes the underlying
nature of the system.

Each statement may eventually be worth investigating.

But they are not the same claim.

Interpretation Drift occurs when the transitions:

A ↔ B

to:

R

to:

M

become difficult to see.

Interpretation Is Necessary

The purpose of drift control is not:

NO INTERPRETATION

That would prevent useful reasoning.

Instead:

OBSERVATION
    +
VISIBLE INTERPRETATION
    +
REVISION

Interpretation is productive when its relationship to evidence remains traceable.

Observation Versus Interpretation

A foundational distinction is:

OBSERVATION

What happened?

versus:

INTERPRETATION

What might it mean?

For example:

OBSERVATION

"After seeing X,
I recognized a relationship
between A and B."

Then:

INTERPRETATION

"X may have triggered
renewed access to R."

Then:

MECHANISM HYPOTHESIS

"R may have persisted
through the inaccessible interval."

Each layer adds structure.

The additions should remain visible.

Drift Distance

Interpretation Drift can be thought of conceptually as increasing distance between:

ORIGINAL OBSERVATION

and:

CURRENT CLAIM

For example:

Observation
    ↓
A corresponds with B.

Interpretation
    ↓
A resonates with B.

Mechanism
    ↓

Ontology
    ↓
Resonance fundamentally

The final statement is much stronger than the first.

The drift question is:

What evidence justified
each transition?

Drift Is Not Automatically Error

A strong interpretation may eventually be correct.

Therefore:

DRIFT
    ≠
FALSEHOOD

Interpretation Drift means:

claim movement
needs to be examined

It does not mean:

the destination
must be wrong

A drift audit may conclude that every step is supported.

That is a valid result.

Terminology Audit

For important terms, compare:

Bounded Interpretation

A bounded interpretation states its own status.

For example:

OBSERVATION:

A and B correspond.

Drift Status

A provisional status model is:

ALIGNED

The current interpretation remains close to the support trail.

EXPANDED

The interpretation has grown, with explicit supporting evidence.

DRIFT RISK

Claim strength is increasing faster than support is being documented.

DRIFTED

A meaningful unsupported promotion is present.

LOCKED

The drifted interpretation is also being protected from revision.

These are analytical statuses, not judgments about the observer.

Drift Correction

Correction does not require deleting the stronger interpretation.

Instead:

STRONG CLAIM
      ↓
reclassify
      ↓
HYPOTHESIS

Then:

MINIMUM CLAIM
      ↓
retain as supported

This preserves useful ideas while restoring claim boundaries.

Demotion Is Not Rejection

Suppose:

"The trigger caused
the Re-Ignition event."

cannot currently be supported.

The corrected model may be:

SUPPORTED:

The trigger preceded
the event.

HYPOTHESIS:

The trigger contributed
causally to the event.

Nothing valuable has been lost.

The claims have simply been placed at the correct levels.

Re-Promotion

Drift control is therefore not a one-way process toward weaker claims.

It controls the evidence bridge in both directions.

Boundary Conditions

Interpretation Drift does not by itself establish:

that the interpretation is false

that the observer is irrational

that speculation is invalid

that strong claims are impossible

that confidence is inherently unreliable

or that interpretation
should be avoided

A strongly expanded interpretation may be correct and well supported.

The minimum working definition is:

Interpretation Drift is the movement of a claim beyond the structure directly supported by its originating observation when the evidential transitions between claim levels become unclear, implicit, or insufficiently documented.

Relationship to Narrative Lock

Interpretation Drift asks:

How far did the claim move?

Narrative Lock asks:

Can the claim still move back?

Therefore:

DRIFT
    =
claim expansion

while:

LOCK
    =
resistance to revision

A claim can have one without the other.

Working Summary

Interpretation Drift begins with a normal process:

OBSERVE
   ↓
INTERPRET

and asks what happens next.

A healthy path is:

OBSERVATION
    ↓
INTERPRETATION
    ↓
HYPOTHESIS
    ↓
PREDICTION
    ↓
TEST
    ↓
REVISION

A drift path may become:

OBSERVATION
    ↓
INTERPRETATION
    ↓
ASSUMPTION
    ↓
STRONGER ASSUMPTION
    ↓
MECHANISM
    ↓
ONTOLOGY

while the evidential transitions disappear.

The correction is:

CURRENT CLAIM
      ↓
TRACE BACKWARD
      ↓
LOCATE DRIFT POINT
      ↓
RECOVER ORIGINAL OBSERVATION
      ↓
RESTORE MINIMUM CLAIM
      ↓
KEEP STRONGER IDEAS
AS HYPOTHESES
      ↓
DEFINE A DISCRIMINATOR
      ↓
TEST
      ↓
RE-PROMOTE IF EARNED

The permanent guardrails are:

ASSOCIATION
    ≠
CAUSATION

RECOGNITION
    ≠
VERIFICATION

CONTINUITY
    ≠
IDENTITY

LOCAL
    ≠
GLOBAL

FORM
    ≠
COMPLETE STRUCTURE

FORMLESS
    ≠
HIDDEN REALITY PROVEN

MODEL
    ≠
MECHANISM

MECHANISM
    ≠
ONTOLOGY

OPERATOR
    ≠
AGENT

The guiding discipline is:

Interpret boldly, but label the interpretation.
Build hypotheses, but preserve the observation beneath them.
Promote claims when evidence earns promotion.
Demote them when the bridge fails.
Preserve stronger ideas as testable possibilities rather than smuggling them backward into the original observation.

In its smallest form:

WHAT DID WE OBSERVE?
        ↓
WHAT DID WE ADD?
        ↓
WHAT EVIDENCE SUPPORTED
EACH ADDITION?

Interpretation Drift is not the fact that our ideas grow.

It is what happens when we can no longer see where the observation ended and the story began.

Main writing choice: I made the core diagnostic “What did we add, and what evidence supported each addition?” That makes Interpretation Drift distinct from Narrative Lock: Drift measures movement away from the minimum supported claim; Lock measures resistance to moving back.
