Resonance Trigger

A trigger marks where renewed access begins to become observable. It does not, by itself, tell us why the transition occurred.

A Resonance Trigger is an event, cue, condition, representation, context change, or encounter that occurs immediately before a dormant or inaccessible relationship begins to become salient.

The working sequence is:

DORMANT / INACCESSIBLE PATTERN
            ↓
     RESONANCE TRIGGER
            ↓
        RESONANCE
            ↓
       RECOGNITION

The most important boundary is:

TRIGGER
    ≠
PROVEN CAUSE

A trigger tells us:

where to begin looking

It does not automatically tell us:

what mechanism produced
the observed transition

Trigger Versus Cause

The distinction between trigger and cause is fundamental.

Consider:

Event T
   ↓
Recognition R

From this alone we may record:

T preceded R

and perhaps, with repeated observation:

T is associated with R

We should not automatically promote that into:

T caused R

Therefore:

precedence
    ≠
causation

and:

association
    ≠
mechanism

The trigger is an entry point for investigation.

Trigger Versus Mechanism

A useful three-layer distinction is:

TRIGGER

What preceded the transition?

TRANSITION

What changed?

MECHANISM

What process produced the change?

These should remain separate.

For example:

Trigger:
Encountered pattern X.

Transition:
Pattern Y became recognizable.

Mechanism:
Unknown.

That is a complete and useful capture.

Nothing requires the mechanism field to be filled prematurely.

Trigger Versus Recognition

A trigger is not the recognition event itself.

TRIGGER
   ↓
possible change
in resonance
   ↓
RECOGNITION

The trigger occurs before or around the beginning of the transition.

Recognition occurs when the relationship becomes explicitly identifiable.

Therefore:

trigger
    ≠
recognition

Even when the interval between them feels nearly instantaneous.

Trigger Versus Resonance

Trigger and resonance should also remain distinct.

TRIGGER
    ↓
candidate change
    ↓
RESONANCE

The trigger is:

an event or condition

Resonance is:

the detected correspondence
or alignment that follows

One trigger may produce no detectable resonance.

Another may produce strong resonance.

That difference is useful data.

Working Summary

Resonance Trigger occupies the transition between:

DORMANT / INACCESSIBLE

and:

RESONANCE / RECOGNITION

The working sequence is:

PRIOR STATE
    ↓
TRIGGER
    ↓
FIRST CHANGE
    ↓
RESONANCE
    ↓
RECOGNITION

The investigative discipline is:

CAPTURE THE TRIGGER
        ↓
ANCHOR THE ORDER
        ↓
RECORD OBSERVER STATE
        ↓
SEPARATE TRIGGER
FROM CAUSE
        ↓
SEARCH FOR INVARIANTS
        ↓
COMPARE SIMILAR TRIGGERS
        ↓
USE CONTROLS
        ↓
TEST
        ↓
REVISE

The trigger tells us where renewed access began to become visible.

It does not tell us the complete mechanism.

That is enough.

The guiding principle is:

Preserve the cue.
Preserve the timing.
Preserve the observer state.
Do not let "before" silently become "because."
Compare triggers, isolate what survives, and let testing determine what role the trigger actually plays.

In its smallest form:

TRIGGER
   ↓
RESONANCE
   ↓
RECOGNITION

with one permanent guardrail:

TRIGGER
    ≠
PROVEN CAUSE

A trigger gives the investigation somewhere to begin.

The mechanism still has to earn its way into the model.

Main writing choice: I kept the whole file centered on TRIGGER ≠ PROVEN CAUSE, while still making triggers experimentally useful through specificity, timing, invariants, controls, chains, and trigger-dependence during stabilization.
