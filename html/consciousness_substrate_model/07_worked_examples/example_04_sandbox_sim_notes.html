Example 04 — Sandbox Simulation Notes

This document provides guidance for sandbox experimentation.

Purpose

Sandbox simulations allow: - exploration of failure modes - testing of boundary conditions - observation of resonance behavior

Recommendations

  • Start with minimal dimensionality.
  • Introduce regime changes deliberately.
  • Observe coherence degradation patterns.
  • Avoid embedding decision logic in the core.

Caution

Sandbox results are illustrative. They do not constitute validation outside the tested context.
