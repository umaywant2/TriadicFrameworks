Alignment with Resonance Substrate Model (RSM)

CSM aligns with RSM at the level of structural substrate.

Shared Principles

Both models emphasize: - minimal primitives - non‑intrusive structure - resonance as alignment, not force - regime awareness

CSM adopts these principles without extending or redefining RSM.

Distinctions

RSM focuses on: - substrate behavior across domains

CSM focuses on: - internal coherence of autonomous forms

CSM may be viewed as a specialized application of substrate concepts within autonomous systems.

Compatibility

No changes to RSM are required to evaluate or implement CSM.
