Peak Demand Conditions

Peak demand conditions represent an operating regime characterized by elevated load levels that approach or stress generation and transmission capacity.

This regime commonly occurs during: - Extreme weather events - Seasonal demand peaks - Large‑scale consumption shifts

Assumptions in this regime include reduced operational margins, heightened sensitivity to asset availability, and increased reliance on reserves or demand response mechanisms.

Structural awareness helps distinguish between expected stress within the peak demand regime and boundary crossings that indicate loss of validity. Recognizing this regime explicitly supports proportional operational responses without conflating high demand with system failure.
