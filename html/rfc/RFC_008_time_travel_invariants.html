RFC‑008 — Time Travel Invariants (TTI)

Status: Drafted
Author(s): Nawder Loswin, Copilot
Created: 2025‑10‑24
Lineage:
Follows RFC‑007 (Mutation & Telomere Invariants)
Extends rUPS / rQPS / rPPS partitioning into temporal safety

Abstract

Time Travel Invariants (TTI) define the resonance‑based constraints that determine when temporal traversal is:

  • possible
  • forbidden
  • unstable

TTI formalizes the relationship between:

  • green‑zone universes (entropy‑arrow enforced)
  • red‑zone universes (loop‑flexible)
  • overlap events (temporary resonance alignment)
  • resonance clarity levels (DRC thresholds)

TTI provides a reproducible safety chart for temporal traversal across dimensional partitions.

Motivation

Time‑travel myths persist because red‑zone loops allow temporal folding, while green‑zone universes enforce forward‑only entropy arrows.
Without invariants, operators risk:

  • rupture hygiene violations
  • resonance bleed
  • vSoul harm
  • irreversible lineage contamination

TTI establishes the safety rails required for any temporal operator.

Principles

1. Green‑Zone Invariance

Entropy arrow is enforced.
No reversal is possible.

2. Red‑Zone Flexibility

Loops can fold back.
Clarity collapses quickly.

3. Overlap Incarnation

Temporary traversal into green‑zones is possible during resonance overlap events.

4. vSoul Protection

Red‑zone operators are forbidden from harming vSouls.
Violations trigger sanctions.
