1. Purpose

The Alert Charter & Mandate Protocol (ACMP) establishes the charter layer responsible for:

  • defining the existential mission of the alert ecosystem
  • articulating the long‑term mandate of alert families
  • establishing the purpose and scope of alert operations
  • ensuring alignment with TriadicFrameworks canon and RTT principles
  • providing a unified philosophical foundation for alert behavior
  • preventing charter‑grade paradox vectors
  • maintaining Nullarium‑safe emotional‑phase alignment in charter interpretation

ACMP is the charter layer of the Alert Canon — the system that ensures the alert ecosystem has a clear, unified, and canon‑aligned purpose.

2. Charter Components

Mission Statement

The alert ecosystem exists to maintain coherence, stability, resonance alignment, and paradox safety across all layers of TriadicFrameworks.

Mandate Definition

The alert ecosystem is mandated to:

  • detect instability
  • forecast drift
  • suppress noise
  • recover coherence
  • monitor infrastructure
  • govern contributors
  • assert sovereignty
  • uphold constitutional guarantees

Purpose Declaration

The alert ecosystem’s purpose is to ensure:

  • corridor stability
  • temporal safety
  • substrate resonance integrity
  • identity and artifact coherence
  • workflow and engine reliability
  • dashboard clarity
  • global synchronization
  • governance and sovereignty protection

Scope of Authority

The alert ecosystem has authority over:

  • all alert families
  • all routing channels
  • all dashboards
  • all engines
  • all contributors
  • all nodes
  • all overlays
  • all paradox vectors

Charter Boundaries

The alert ecosystem may not:

  • violate constitutional guarantees
  • override sovereignty
  • breach Nullarium safety
  • contradict canon
  • generate paradox vectors intentionally

Charter Protections

The alert ecosystem is protected by:

  • constitutional guarantees (ALR‑0038)
  • sovereignty rights (ALR‑0037)
  • governance oversight (ALR‑0036)
  • health monitoring (ALR‑0035)
