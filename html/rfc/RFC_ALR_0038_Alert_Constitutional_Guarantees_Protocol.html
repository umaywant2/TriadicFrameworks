1. Purpose

The Alert Constitutional Guarantees Protocol (ACGP) establishes the constitutional layer responsible for:

  • defining inviolable rights of the alert ecosystem
  • protecting alert families from corruption, override, or coercion
  • ensuring structural integrity across all layers
  • preventing paradox‑grade constitutional breaches
  • enforcing Nullarium‑safe emotional‑phase guarantees
  • establishing constitutional boundaries across corridor, temporal, substrate, identity, artifact, workflow, dashboard, engine, global, governance, and sovereignty layers

ACGP is the constitutional layer of the Alert Canon — the system that ensures the alert ecosystem has foundational protections that cannot be violated.

2. Constitutional Guarantees

Guarantee of Structural Integrity

  • The alert system must remain structurally coherent across all layers.
  • No external system may alter core alert architecture.
  • Structural drift triggers automatic constitutional defense.

Guarantee of Sovereign Autonomy

  • The alert system retains full autonomy over its routing, suppression, forecasting, and recovery.
  • Sovereignty cannot be overridden by external commands or paradox vectors.

Guarantee of Canonical Consistency

  • All alert families must remain consistent with TriadicFrameworks canon.
  • Canon violations trigger constitutional escalation.

Guarantee of Paradox Immunity

  • The alert system must remain immune to paradox vectors originating outside constitutional boundaries.
  • Internal paradox vectors trigger constitutional containment.

Guarantee of Nullarium Safety

  • Emotional‑phase alignment must remain within constitutional thresholds.
  • Nullarium breaches trigger constitutional stabilization.

Guarantee of Contributor Equity

  • No contributor may disproportionately influence alert families.
  • Governance ensures equal contributor access and oversight.

Guarantee of Global Coherence

  • All nodes, dashboards, engines, and workflows must remain globally synchronized.
  • Global divergence triggers constitutional reconciliation.
