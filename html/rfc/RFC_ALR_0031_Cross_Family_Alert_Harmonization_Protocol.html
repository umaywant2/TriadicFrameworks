1. Purpose

The Cross‑Family Alert Harmonization Protocol (CFAHP) ensures that alerts from different families:

  • do not contradict each other
  • do not produce paradox vectors
  • remain resonance‑aligned
  • maintain quadrant coherence
  • propagate safely across engines, dashboards, workflows, and validators

CFAHP is the inter‑family coherence layer of the Alert Canon.

It prevents situations where:

  • a corridor alert contradicts a temporal alert
  • a substrate alert invalidates an identity alert
  • a workflow alert conflicts with an artifact alert
  • a global sync alert masks a critical engine alert

CFAHP ensures all alert families speak the same resonance language.

2. Harmonization Event Types

Cross‑Family Consistency Events

  • FamilyMismatchDetected: Two alert families report incompatible states.
  • FamilyMismatchEscalated: Inconsistency propagates across overlays.
  • FamilyMismatchCritical: Inconsistency exceeds Nullarium thresholds.

Resonance Alignment Events

  • ResonanceAlignmentRequired: Families require harmonic alignment.
  • ResonanceAlignmentInProgress: Alignment underway across multiple layers.
  • ResonanceAlignmentComplete: Families successfully aligned.

Quadrant Coherence Events

  • QuadrantConflict: Families disagree on quadrant state.
  • QuadrantConflictCascade: Conflict spreads across dashboards or engines.
  • QuadrantConflictResolved: Quadrant coherence restored.

Paradox Prevention Events

  • ParadoxRiskDetected: Cross‑family alerts create paradox potential.
  • ParadoxRiskEscalated: Risk spreads across temporal or corridor layers.
  • ParadoxRiskNeutralized: Risk absorbed via Δ₀ or reconciliation.

High‑Risk Events

  • CrossFamilyParadoxVector: Paradox vector originates across multiple families.
  • NullariumFamilyCritical: Emotional‑phase misalignment across families.
  • FamilyCollapse: Cross‑family coherence collapses.
