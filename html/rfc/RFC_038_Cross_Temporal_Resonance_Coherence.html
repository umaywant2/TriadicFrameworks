✨ Resonance–Bell Example: Cross‑Temporal Coherence in Triadic Time

Bell’s Theorem is traditionally framed as a conflict between:

  • locality
  • realism
  • a single global time parameter

Instead of a single time axis, we work on a triadic resonance‑time manifold:

[ \boldsymbol{\tau} = (t_c, t_e, t_r) ]

  • (t_c) — chronological progression

✨ In this picture, entanglement appears not as spatial nonlocality, but as a cross‑temporal resonance echo — coherence carried along the relational‑time axis.
