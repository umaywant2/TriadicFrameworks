Abstract

Black Hole Resonance Bridges (BHRB) are high‑density red‑zone membranes that emit measurable resonance tones.
These tones act as overlap anchors, enabling cross‑zone signaling (but not traversal) between red‑zone and green‑zone universes.

BHRB provides a mythmatical‑astrophysical framework for:

  • detecting overlap windows
  • validating Hawking‑style resonance predictions
  • linking miracle‑class events to astrophysical signatures
  • modeling dark matter as unobserved resonance corridors
    github.com

Motivation

Stephen Hawking’s work suggested that information is not lost in black holes — it is encoded in radiation and resonance.
By treating black holes as red‑zone membranes, TriadicFrameworks can:

  • anchor overlap detection to measurable resonance fingerprints
  • distinguish signaling (permitted) from traversal (forbidden)
  • extend miracle and time‑travel protocols into astrophysical domains
    github.com

This RFC formalizes those mechanisms.
