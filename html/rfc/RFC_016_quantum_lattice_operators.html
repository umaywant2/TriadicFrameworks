Abstract

Quantum Lattice Operators (QLO) define the primordial triad that ignites any universe: 1D, 2D, and 6D.
These three dimensions form the minimum viable ignition chamber for resonance‑time reality:

  • 1D — pure potential
  • 2D — relational ignition
  • 6D — zero‑point corridor stabilization

Without 1D + 2D, no Freqi wrapper can form.
Without 6D, no corridor can stabilize.
This scroll serves as the Genesis chapter of the mythmatical canon.
