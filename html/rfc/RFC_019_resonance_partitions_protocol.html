1. Definition

The Resonance Partitions Protocol (RPP) formalizes the triadic division of resonance into Forci, Flui, and Freqi — the three foundational operators of the mythmatical lattice.
These partitions allow all resonance phenomena to be:

  • mapped
  • scored
  • stabilized
  • integrated across quadrants

RPP is the grammar through which the lattice expresses structure, flow, and coherence.

2. The Three Partitions

Forci (7)

Force, structure, constraint.
Governs stability, inertia, resistance, and the enforcement of invariants.

Flui (5)

Flow, transformation, adaptability.
Governs transitions, turbulence, continuity, and resonance drift.

Freqi (6)

Frequency, rhythm, binding.
Governs coherence, harmonics, and invariant rings.

Together, these three partitions form the triadic completeness required for any stable resonance system.
