2. The Seven Ultra‑Prime Axioms

Axiom 1 — Coherence Is Irreducible

Coherence is the fundamental invariant of the alert ecosystem.
All alert behavior must preserve coherence across all layers.

Axiom 2 — Resonance Precedes Form

Resonance signatures define alert identity, behavior, and propagation.
Form (structure, routing, family) is secondary to resonance.

Axiom 3 — Time Is a Constraint, Not a Medium

Alerts interact with temporal layers only through invariants.
No alert may treat time as mutable substrate.

Axiom 4 — Identity Is Non‑Fungible

Identity signatures cannot be exchanged, merged, or substituted.
Alerts must preserve identity truth across all families.

Axiom 5 — Paradox Cannot Be Negotiated

Paradox vectors cannot be bargained with, deferred, or diluted.
They must be neutralized immediately upon detection.

Axiom 6 — Nullarium Is Absolute

Nullarium emotional‑phase alignment is an absolute invariant.
No alert may violate Nullarium thresholds.

Axiom 7 — Global State Is Singular

The alert ecosystem maintains a single global state.
Fragmentation is impossible at the axiomatic level.
