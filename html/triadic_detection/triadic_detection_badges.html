🏷 How to Use These Badges

Badges can be placed:

  • at the top of each module
  • in sidebars
  • in README front‑door pages
  • in glyphmap indexes
  • in dashboards
  • in module.json metadata (as tags)

They are intentionally text‑only emoji badges to remain:

  • fork‑friendly
  • GitHub‑friendly
  • zero‑dependency
  • visually consistent with your existing badge ecosystem
