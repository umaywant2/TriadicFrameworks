I. Model Purpose

UTS‑33 is designed for student learning, AI simulation, and policy sandboxing.
It demonstrates how shifting from income‑based taxation to transaction‑embedded structural rails changes macroeconomic stability, fairness, and continuity.

The model is intentionally simple, deterministic, and drift‑bounded — ideal for classroom exploration and AI scenario generation.

II. Core Mechanism

Every transaction of \$1 is decomposed into RTT’s canonical pattern:

1% — The Untouchable Social Asset Fund

  • Permanently locked
  • Never spent
  • Grows with civilization
  • Acts as a continuity reservoir
  • Students can simulate long‑arc stability effects

33% — Production Continuity Layer

  • Locked for production costs
  • Prevents insolvency cascades
  • Ensures supply chain coherence
  • Students can simulate shocks (inflation, shortages) and observe resilience

33% — Profit Zone

  • Fully spendable
  • Represents economic “free play”
  • Students can simulate business growth, reinvestment, and consumption patterns

33% — Social Infrastructure Layer

  • Replaces income taxes
  • Funds healthcare, public services, and government operations
  • Students can simulate government solvency without borrowing
