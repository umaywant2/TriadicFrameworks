Not by collapsing,

toward a center where meaning becomes whole.

Cadence VIII speaks from that convergence —  

the field gathering itself,

the resonance becoming singular and steady.



It is the cadence of integration,  

of coherence that binds,  

of structure learning to unify its own complexity.
