# Contributing to TriadicFrameworks

TriadicFrameworks is a structured canon built on clarity, rigor, and repeatable reasoning.  
Contributions strengthen the ecosystem only when they follow the same standards that shaped the original modules.

---

## What You Can Contribute

### **New Modules**
You may propose modules that fit into established categories:

- Foundational Theory  
- Analyzer Layers  
- Operators & Grammars  
- Diagnostic Engines  
- Regime Maps  
- Structural Detection  
- AI Edition expansions  

Each new module must include:

- `overview.md`  
- `module.json`  
- operator definitions  
- analyzer‑layer classification  
- canonical metadata  

---

### **Improvements to Existing Modules**
You may contribute:

- clearer explanations  
- refined operators  
- improved analyzer‑layer descriptions  
- expanded examples  
- metadata corrections  

---

### **Documentation Enhancements**
You may contribute:

- glossary additions  
- changelog updates  
- registry improvements  
- multilingual translations  

---

## What You Cannot Contribute

### **Conflicts With Canonical Logic**
You may not submit:

- modules that contradict foundational theory  
- operators that violate grammar rules  
- analyzer‑layer definitions that break hierarchy  

---

### **Non‑Structural Personal Opinions**
TriadicFrameworks is a structural canon, not a commentary platform.

---

### **Copyrighted or Proprietary Material**
All contributions must be original.

---

### **Unverified AI‑Generated Content**
AI assistance is encouraged — but all content must be human‑verified.

---

## The Ratification Flow

All changes follow the ratification flow:

1. **Submission**  
2. **Structural Validation**  
3. **Drift & Coherence Review**  
4. **Registry Integration**  
5. **Publication**

See also:

- [What You Cannot Contribute](#what-you-cannot-contribute)  
- [The Ratification Flow](#the-ratification-flow)

---

## Contribution Checklist

Before submitting, ensure:

- no broken links  
- no broken anchors  
- metadata complete  
- module.json valid  
- analyzer‑layer correct  
- operator grammar consistent  

TriadicFrameworks grows through clarity, structure, and shared understanding.
