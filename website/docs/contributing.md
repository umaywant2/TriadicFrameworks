**# Contributing to TriadicFrameworks**



**TriadicFrameworks is a structured canon built on clarity, rigor, and repeatable reasoning.**  

**Contributions strengthen the ecosystem only when they follow the same standards that shaped the original modules.**  

**This document explains \*\*what you may contribute\*\*, \*\*what you may not\*\*, and \*\*how the ratification flow works\*\*.**



**---**



**## Purpose of This Document**



**This guide ensures that all contributions:**



**- preserve the internal logic of the canon**  

**- maintain structural consistency across modules**  

**- follow the analyzer‑layer and operator‑grammar conventions**  

**- integrate cleanly with the module.json registry**  

**- remain compatible with future AI‑assisted tooling**  



**It also defines the boundaries of acceptable contributions.**



**---**



**## What You \*Can\* Contribute**



**### \*\*1. New Modules\*\***

**You may propose new modules that fit into one of the established categories:**



**- \*\*Foundational Theory\*\***  

**- \*\*Analyzer Layers\*\***  

**- \*\*Operators \& Grammars\*\***  

**- \*\*Diagnostic Engines\*\***  

**- \*\*Maps \& Regimes\*\***  

**- \*\*Extensions \& Examples\*\***



**Each new module must include:**



**- `index.md` or `overview.md`**  

**- `module.json` (following the TriadicFrameworks schema)**  

**- clear operator definitions**  

**- analyzer‑layer classification**  

**- purpose, audience, and canonical metadata**  



**---**



**### \*\*2. Improvements to Existing Modules\*\***

**You may contribute:**



**- clearer explanations**  

**- corrected diagrams**  

**- expanded examples**  

**- improved operator definitions**  

**- refined analyzer‑layer descriptions**  

**- additional regime logic**  

**- better cross‑module references**  



**All improvements must preserve the module’s original intent.**



**---**



**### \*\*3. Documentation Enhancements\*\***

**You may contribute:**



**- glossary additions**  

**- changelog entries**  

**- session grammar clarifications**  

**- registry updates**  

**- better navigation**  

**- multilingual translations (i18n folders)**



**---**



**### \*\*4. AI Edition Content\*\***

**You may contribute:**



**- improved AI‑Edition overviews**  

**- expanded Book1–Book9 summaries**  

**- better capture.md explanations**  

**- alignment between human‑readable and AI‑readable versions**  



**---**



**## What You \*Cannot\* Contribute**



**### \*\*1. Content That Conflicts With Canonical Logic\*\***

**You may not submit:**



**- modules that contradict foundational theory**  

**- operators that violate established grammar rules**  

**- analyzer‑layer definitions that break the hierarchy**  

**- regime logic that conflicts with existing modules**  



**---**



**### \*\*2. Non‑Structural Personal Opinions\*\***

**TriadicFrameworks is a structural canon, not a commentary platform.**  

**You may not contribute:**



**- personal essays**  

**- subjective opinions**  

**- non‑technical narratives**  

**- unrelated philosophical content**  



**---**



**### \*\*3. Proprietary or Copyrighted Material\*\***

**All contributions must be original.**  

**You may not submit:**



**- copyrighted diagrams**  

**- proprietary algorithms**  

**- licensed text**  

**- external content without permission**  



**---**



**### \*\*4. AI‑Generated Content Without Human Verification\*\***

**AI assistance is encouraged — but:**



**- all AI‑generated text must be reviewed**  

**- contributors must validate correctness**  

**- hallucinated structures must be removed**  

**- metadata must be manually checked**  



**---**



**## The Ratification Flow**



**The ratification flow ensures that every contribution becomes part of the canon \*\*only after structural validation\*\*.**



**### \*\*Step 1 — Submission\*\***

**A contributor submits:**



**- markdown content**  

**- module.json**  

**- diagrams (optional)**  

**- metadata**  

**- operator definitions**  

**- analyzer‑layer classification**  



**### \*\*Step 2 — Structural Validation\*\***

**The submission is checked for:**



**- canonical consistency**  

**- operator grammar correctness**  

**- analyzer‑layer alignment**  

**- metadata completeness**  

**- cross‑module compatibility**  

**- broken links or anchors**  



**### \*\*Step 3 — Drift \& Coherence Review\*\***

**The contribution is evaluated using:**



**- \*\*Drift Engine\*\* (detects structural deviation)**  

**- \*\*Coherence Engine\*\* (validates internal logic)**  



**If drift is detected, the submission is returned for revision.**



**### \*\*Step 4 — Registry Integration\*\***

**Once validated:**



**- the module.json is added to the registry**  

**- the sitemap metadata is updated**  

**- cross‑module references are generated**  

**- canonical metadata is refreshed**  



**### \*\*Step 5 — Publication\*\***

**The contribution becomes part of:**



**- `/docs/book/`**  

**- `/docs/book/AI\_Edition/`**  

**- `/docs/speeches/` (if applicable)**  

**- multilingual folders (if provided)**  



**---**



**## Contribution Checklist**



**Before submitting, ensure:**



**- no broken links**  

**- no broken anchors**  

**- metadata is complete**  

**- module.json is valid**  

**- analyzer‑layer is correct**  

**- operator grammar is consistent**  

**- overview.md exists**  

**- filenames follow Docusaurus conventions**  

**- i18n folders mirror the English structure**  



**---**



**## Contact \& Support**



**For questions, clarifications, or structural guidance, contributors may open an issue in the repository or consult the module registry.**



**TriadicFrameworks grows through clarity, structure, and shared understanding.**  

**Your contributions help expand the canon for everyone.**
