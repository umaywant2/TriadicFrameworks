# Part III — Regimes, Engines & Structural Detection

Part III introduces the dynamic behavior of RTT structures.  
Regimes define environmental conditions, engines detect structural behavior, and detection systems reveal patterns, drift, and coherence.

---

## Regime Logic

Regimes determine how structures behave under different conditions:

### **Opacity Regime**
Structures become hidden or difficult to interpret.

### **Revelation Regime**
Structures become clear, amplified, or exposed.

### **Governance Regime**
Structures follow rule‑based behavior and ratification logic.

### **Pattern Dynamics Regime**
Patterns evolve, stabilize, or collapse over time.

---

## Diagnostic Engines

Diagnostic engines analyze structural behavior:

### **Drift Engine**
Detects deviations from canonical logic.

### **Coherence Engine**
Validates structural alignment.

### **Pattern Detection Engine**
Identifies emergent structural patterns.

---

## Structural Detection

Structural detection reveals:

- resonance patterns  
- drift vectors  
- coherence alignment  
- temporal evolution  
- cross‑module interactions  

Detection systems rely on both operators and analyzer layers.

---

## Purpose of Part III

Part III exists to:

- define RTT regime behavior  
- introduce diagnostic engines  
- unify structural detection  
- prepare readers for advanced modules and AI Edition content
