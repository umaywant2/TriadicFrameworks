# Part II — Analyzer Layers & Operators

Part II introduces the analytical machinery of Resonance‑Time Theory (RTT).  
Analyzer layers determine *how* structure is interpreted, while operators determine *what* transformations or detections occur.

Together, they form the functional grammar of the canon.

---

## Analyzer Layer Hierarchy

Analyzer layers classify structural behavior across RTT:

### **Dimensional Analyzer Layer**
Interprets structural behavior across dimensional axes.

### **Regime Analyzer Layer**
Determines how structures behave under different environmental conditions.

### **Drift Analyzer Layer**
Detects deviations from canonical logic.

### **Coherence Analyzer Layer**
Validates structural alignment with the canon.

### **Cross‑Cutting Analyzer Layer**
Handles multi‑domain interactions across modules.

---

## Operator Grammar

Operators perform structural actions:

- **Structural Operators** — shape or transform patterns  
- **Resonance Operators** — amplify or stabilize patterns  
- **Detection Operators** — identify drift, coherence, or resonance  
- **Expression Operators (TEL)** — encode structural meaning  
- **Argumentation Operators (SARG)** — structure reasoning and logic  

Operators must always align with analyzer‑layer rules.

---

## Purpose of Part II

Part II exists to:

- define RTT’s analytical machinery  
- establish operator grammar  
- unify structural interpretation  
- prepare readers for regime logic and engines
