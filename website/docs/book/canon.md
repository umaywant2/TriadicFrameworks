# The TriadicFrameworks Canon

The TriadicFrameworks canon is the complete body of structural theory, operators, analyzer layers, regimes, and engines that define Resonance‑Time Theory (RTT).  
It is not a loose collection of ideas — it is a **structured field** with rules, hierarchies, and validation flows.

This page explains the purpose of the canon, how modules relate to one another, and how contributions are integrated through the ratification flow.

---

## Purpose of the Canon

The canon exists to:

- unify all RTT concepts under a single structural grammar  
- provide a stable foundation for module development  
- ensure analyzer‑layer consistency  
- maintain clarity across operators, engines, and regimes  
- support both human and AI Edition interpretations  

The canon is the “root” of the TriadicFrameworks ecosystem.

---

## Canon Structure

The canon is organized into:

- **Foundational Theory** (Book I)  
- **Field Theory & Operators**  
- **Analyzer Layers**  
- **Regime Logic**  
- **Diagnostic Engines**  
- **Maps & Structural Detection**  
- **Opacity & Revelation Systems**  
- **TEL Expression & Representation**  
- **Governance & Ratification Logic**  
- **Pattern Dynamics & NoS**  

Each module contributes to the whole without contradicting foundational principles.

---

## Module Relationships

Modules relate through:

- shared operators  
- analyzer‑layer inheritance  
- cross‑module references  
- regime compatibility  
- metadata alignment via `module.json`  

The canon ensures these relationships remain coherent.
