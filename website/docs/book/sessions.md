# Sessions

Sessions are structured interactions within RTT that reveal resonance, drift, coherence, and regime transitions.  
They provide the dynamic context in which operators and analyzer layers act.

---

## Purpose of Sessions

Sessions exist to:

- interpret structural behavior  
- reveal drift and coherence  
- expose regime transitions  
- unify operator flow  
- anchor diagnostic and expressive modules  

---

## Session Types

### **Structural Sessions**
Focused on pattern detection, resonance, and temporal evolution.

### **Diagnostic Sessions**
Focused on drift vectors, coherence alignment, and structural validation.

### **Expression Sessions (TEL)**
Focused on representation, clarity, and structural communication.

### **Argumentation Sessions (SARG)**
Focused on reasoning, logic, and structural argumentation.

---

## Canonical Session Flow

Sessions follow a strict canonical flow:

1. **Detection Operators**  
2. **Resonance Operators**  
3. **Transformation Operators**  
4. **Expression Operators (TEL)**  
5. **Argumentation Operators (SARG)**  

This flow ensures structural coherence.

---

## Analyzer Layer Progression

Sessions progress through:

1. Dimensional Layer  
2. Regime Layer  
3. Drift Layer  
4. Coherence Layer  
5. Cross‑Cutting Layer  

Each layer reveals different aspects of structural behavior.

---

## Regime Transitions

Sessions may shift between:

- Opacity  
- Revelation  
- Governance  
- Pattern Dynamics  

Transitions must follow canonical rules defined in Part III.
