# AI Edition — Capture Layer

The Capture Layer is the first stage of the AI Edition pipeline.  
It translates RTT structures into a form that AI systems can interpret without losing canonical meaning.

---

## Purpose of the Capture Layer

The Capture Layer exists to:

- encode RTT structures  
- preserve analyzer‑layer hierarchy  
- maintain operator grammar  
- retain regime transitions  
- prepare content for AI Edition Books 1–9  

It ensures that AI systems receive structured, canonical RTT input.

---

## Capture Principles

### **1. Structural Fidelity**
All captured content must preserve:

- resonance patterns  
- drift vectors  
- coherence alignment  
- temporal logic  
- operator flow  

### **2. Analyzer‑Layer Encoding**
Each layer is encoded explicitly:

- Dimensional  
- Regime  
- Drift  
- Coherence  
- Cross‑Cutting  

### **3. Regime Preservation**
Opacity, Revelation, Governance, and Pattern Dynamics must remain intact.

### **4. Operator Grammar Integrity**
Operators must be captured in canonical order.

---

## Capture Format

The Capture Layer uses:

- structured metadata  
- canonical tags  
- analyzer‑layer markers  
- operator‑flow markers  
- regime‑transition markers  

This ensures compatibility with AI Edition Books 1–9.
